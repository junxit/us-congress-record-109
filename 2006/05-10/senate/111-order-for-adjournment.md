# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the previous order.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask the majority leader if he would give 
me the option to make a closing statement, and that the Senate adjourn 
after that option is given.
  Mr. FRIST. I have no objection to that.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Illinois is recognized.
```
