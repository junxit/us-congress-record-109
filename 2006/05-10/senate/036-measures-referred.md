# MEASURES REFERRED

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 3829. An act to designate the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs Medical Center in Muskogee, Oklahoma, as the Jack C. 
     Montgomery Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center; to 
     the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
       H.R. 4204. An act to direct the Secretary of the Interior 
     to transfer ownership of the American River Pump Station 
     Project, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Energy 
     and Natural Resources.
       H.R. 4902. An act to award a Congressional gold medal to 
     Byron Nelson in recognition of his significant contributions 
     to the game of golf as a player, a teacher, and a 
     commentator; to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban 
     Affairs.
       H.R. 4912. An act to amend section 242 of the National 
     Housing Act to extend the exemption for critical access 
     hospitals under the FHA program for mortgage insurance for 
     hospitals; to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban 
     Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
