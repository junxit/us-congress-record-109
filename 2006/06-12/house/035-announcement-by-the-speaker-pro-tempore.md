```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The Speaker pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on questions previously postponed. Votes will be taken in 
the following order:
  H. Res. 794, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Res. 804, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Res. 608, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Con. Res. 338, by the yeas and nays; ordering the previous 
question on H. Res. 857, by the yeas and nays.
  Proceedings on H. Con. Res. 408 will resume tomorrow.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. The 
other votes in this series will be 5-minute votes.
```
