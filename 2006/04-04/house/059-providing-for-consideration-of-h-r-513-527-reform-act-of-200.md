# PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 513, 527 REFORM ACT OF 2005

**Measures:** H.R. 513, H.Res. 755

```
    PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 513, 527 REFORM ACT OF 2005

  Mr. Dreier, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 109-404) on the resolution (H. Res. 755) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 513) to amend the Federal Election 
Campaign Act of 1971 to clarify when organizations described in section 
527 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 must register as political 
committees, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
