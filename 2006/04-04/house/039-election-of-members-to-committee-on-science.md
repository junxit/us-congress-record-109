# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO COMMITTEE ON SCIENCE

```
              ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO COMMITTEE ON SCIENCE

  Mr. LATHAM. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 754) and I ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 754

       Resolved, That the following Members be and are hereby 
     elected to the following standing committee of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on Science: Mr. Neugebauer to rank after Mr. 
     Feeney, and Mr. Mario Diaz-Balart of Florida.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Iowa?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
