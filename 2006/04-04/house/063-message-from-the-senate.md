# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a Joint Resolution and a Concurrent 
Resolution of the following titles in which the concurrence of the 
House is requested:

       S.J. Res. 28. Joint resolution approving the location of 
     the commemorative work in the District of Columbia honoring 
     former President Dwight D. Eisenhower.
       S. Con. Res. 60. Concurrent resolution designating the 
     Negro Leagues Baseball Museum in Kansas City, Missouri, as 
     America's National Negro Leagues Baseball Museum.
```
