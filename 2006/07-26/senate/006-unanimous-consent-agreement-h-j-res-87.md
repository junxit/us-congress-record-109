# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 87

```
               UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 87

  Mr. MARTINEZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 12 noon 
on Wednesday, July 26, the Senate proceed to the immediate 
consideration of H.J. Res. 87, which was received from the House. I 
further ask unanimous consent that there be 30 minutes equally divided 
between the two leaders or their designees, and that following the use 
or yielding of time, the joint resolution be read a third time and the 
Senate proceed to a vote on passage without intervening action or 
debate.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection? Without 
objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
