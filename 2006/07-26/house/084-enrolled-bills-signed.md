# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 9, H.J.Res. 86, H.R. 4019, H.R. 4472, H.R. 5865

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mrs. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker:

       H.R. 9. An act to amend the Voting Rights Act of 1965.
       H.J. Res. 86. Joint resolution approving the renewal of 
     import restrictions contained in the Burmese Freedom and 
     Democracy Act of 2003, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4019. An act to amend title 4 of the United States 
     Code to clarify the treatment of self-employment for purposes 
     of the limitation on State taxation of retirement income.
       H.R. 4472. An act to protect children from sexual 
     exploitation and violent crime, to prevent child abuse and 
     child pornography, to promote Internet safety, and to honor 
     the memory of Adam Walsh and other child crime victims.
       H.R. 5865. An act to amend section 1113 of the Social 
     Security Act to temporarily increase funding for the program 
     of temporary assistance for United States citizens returned 
     from foreign countries, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
