# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 889, H.R. 4912

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  At 6:31 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hanrahan, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker 
has signed the following enrolled bills:

       H.R. 889. An act to authorize appropriations for the Coast 
     Guard for fiscal year 2006, to make technical corrections to 
     various laws administered by the Coast Guard, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 4912. An act to amend section 242 of the National 
     Housing Act to extend the exemption for critical access 
     hospitals under the FHA program for mortgage insurance for 
     hospitals.

  The enrolled bills were subsequently signed by the Acting President 
pro tempore (Mr. McConnell).

                          ____________________
```
