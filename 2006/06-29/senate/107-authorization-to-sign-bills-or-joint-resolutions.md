# AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
            AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority whip and both Senators from 
Virginia be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint 
resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
