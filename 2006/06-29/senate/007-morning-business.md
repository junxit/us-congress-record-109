# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
2 hours, with the first 30 minutes under the control of the majority 
leader or his designee, the next 30 minutes under the control of the 
Democratic leader or his designee, the next 30 minutes under the 
control of the majority leader or his designee, and with the final 30 
minutes under the control of the Democratic leader or his designee.

                          ____________________
```
