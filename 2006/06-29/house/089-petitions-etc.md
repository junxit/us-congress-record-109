# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       124. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the City 
     Commission of Pembroke Pines, Florida, relative to Resolution 
     No. 3092 supporting no less than $4.3 Billion in 
     Congressional funding for the fiscal year 2007 for the 
     Community Development Block Grant Program; to the Committee 
     on Financial Services.
       125. Also, a petition of the City Coucil of Atlanta, 
     Georgia, relative to Resolution 06-R-0950 urging the Congress 
     of the United States to support the principles put forward by 
     Senators Conrad Burns (R-Montana) and Daniel Inouye (D-
     Hawaii) that place local authority at the center of 
     Franchising reform; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       126. Also, a petition of the City Council of Atlanta, 
     Georgia, relative to Resolution 06-R-0951 urging the Congress 
     of the United States to support a United States Department of 
     Peace and Nonviolence; jointly to the Committees on 
     Government Reform, International Relations, and the 
     Judiciary.
```
