# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JULY 3, 2006

```
            CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JULY 3, 2006

  Mr. PRICE of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today pursuant to this order, it adjourn to meet at 
noon on Monday, July 3, 2006, unless it sooner has received a message 
from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent 
Resolution 440, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant 
to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
