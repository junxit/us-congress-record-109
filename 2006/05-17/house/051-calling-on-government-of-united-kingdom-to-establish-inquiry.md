  Mr. POE changed his vote from ``yea'' to ``present.''
  So (two-thirds of those voting having responded in the affirmative) 
the rules were suspended and the resolution, as amended, was agreed to.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title of the resolution was amended so as to read: ``Resolution 
calling on the Government of the United Kingdom immediately to 
establish a full, independent, public judicial inquiry into the murder 
of Northern Ireland defense attorney Patrick Finucane, as recommended 
by Judge Peter Cory as part of the Weston Park Agreement, in order to 
move forward on the Northern Ireland peace process.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
