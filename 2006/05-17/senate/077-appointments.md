U.S.C. 1928a-1928d, as amended, appoints the following Senators to the 
Senate Delegation to the NATO Parliamentary Assembly, during the 109th 
Congress: the Honorable Charles Grassley of Iowa, the Honorable Wayne 
Allard of Colorado, the Honorable Jeff Sessions of Alabama, the 
Honorable George Voinovich of Ohio, and the Honorable Norm Coleman of 
Minnesota.
