# 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF CROCKER, SOUTH DAKOTA

```
               100TH ANNIVERSARY OF CROCKER, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE, Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Crocker, 
SD. The town of Crocker will celebrate the 100th anniversary of its 
founding this year.
  Located in Clark County, Crocker was founded as an agricultural town 
in 1906. Crocker is just one example of what has made South Dakota the 
place it is today.
  I offer my congratulations to Crocker on their centennial.●

                          ____________________
```
