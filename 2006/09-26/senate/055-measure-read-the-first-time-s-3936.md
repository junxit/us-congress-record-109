# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3936

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3936

  Mr. McCONNELL. I understand there is a bill at the desk, and I ask 
for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3936) to invest in innovation and education to 
     improve the competitiveness of the United States in the 
     global economy.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I now ask for a second reading in order to place the 
bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, and I object to 
my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.
```
