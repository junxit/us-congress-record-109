  Mr. MOLLOHAN changed his vote from ``yea'' to ``nay.''
  So the bill was passed.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title of the bill was amended so as to read: ``A bill to amend 
the Revised Statutes of the United States to prevent the use of the 
legal system in a manner that extorts money from State and local 
governments, and the Federal government, and inhibits such governments' 
constitutional actions under the first, tenth, and fourteenth 
amendments.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
