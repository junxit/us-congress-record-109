# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6166, MILITARY COMMISSIONS ACT OF 2006

**Measures:** H.Res. 1042, H.R. 6166

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6166, MILITARY 
                        COMMISSIONS ACT OF 2006

  Mr. GINGREY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 109-688) on the resolution (H. Res. 1042) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 6166) to amend title 10, United 
States Code, to authorize trial by military commission for violations 
of the law of war, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
