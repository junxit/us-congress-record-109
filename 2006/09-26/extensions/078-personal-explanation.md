                         HON. MARK STEVEN KIRK

following votes: Rollcall No. 471: H.R. 5059, To designate the Wild

New Hampshire, and for other purposes.; Rollcall No. 472: H.R. 5062, To

Road, NW in Vienna, Virginia, as the `Captain Christopher P. Petty and 
Major William F. Hecker, III Post Office Building'. Had I been present, 
I would have voted ``yea'' on Rollcall No. 471, Rollcall No. 472, and 
Rollcall No. 473.
