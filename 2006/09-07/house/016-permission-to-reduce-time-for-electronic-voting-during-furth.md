# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING FURTHER PROCEEDINGS IN THE HOUSE AND IN THE COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE

```
    PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING FURTHER 
       PROCEEDINGS IN THE HOUSE AND IN THE COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, during 
further proceedings today in the House and in the Committee of the 
Whole, the Chair be authorized to reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time 
for electronic voting on any question that otherwise could be subjected 
to 5-minute voting under clause 8 or 9 of rule XX or under clause 6 of 
rule XVIII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Foley). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
