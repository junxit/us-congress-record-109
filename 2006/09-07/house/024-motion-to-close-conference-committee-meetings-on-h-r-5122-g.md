**Speaking:** Drake, Thelma D. (D000605) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 5122

```

``SONNY'' MONTGOMERY NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 
     2007, WHEN CLASSIFIED NATIONAL SECURITY INFORMATION IS UNDER 
                             CONSIDERATION

that meetings of the conference between the House and Senate on H.R. 
5122 may be closed to the public at such times as classified national

of Congress shall be entitled to attend any meeting of the conference.

motion is not debatable, and the yeas and nays are ordered.
  This will be a 5-minute vote.
```
