# KATHERINE DUNHAM POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 KATHERINE DUNHAM POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 5929) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 950 Missouri Avenue in East St. Louis, 
Illinois, as the ``Katherine Dunham Post Office Building'', was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
