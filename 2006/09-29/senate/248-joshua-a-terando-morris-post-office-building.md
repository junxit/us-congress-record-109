# JOSHUA A. TERANDO MORRIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
             JOSHUA A. TERANDO MORRIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5428) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 202 East Washington Street in Morris, 
Illinois, as the ``Joshua A. Terando Morris Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
