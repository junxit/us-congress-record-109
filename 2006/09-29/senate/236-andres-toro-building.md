# ANDRES TORO BUILDING

```
                          ANDRES TORO BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 5026) to designate the Investigations Building of the 
Food and Drug Administration located at 466 Fernandez Juncos Avenue in 
San Juan, Puerto Rico, as the ``Andres Toro Building'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
