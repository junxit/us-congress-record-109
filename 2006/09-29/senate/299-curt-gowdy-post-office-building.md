# CURT GOWDY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5224

```
                    CURT GOWDY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R.5224) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 350 Uinta Drive in Green River, Wyoming, as 
the ``Curt Gowdy Post Office Building'', was considered ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
