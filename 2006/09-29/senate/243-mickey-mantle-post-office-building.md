# MICKEY MANTLE POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                   MICKEY MANTLE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (S. 3845), to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 301 Commerce Street in Commerce, Oklahoma, as 
the ``Mickey Mantle Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
