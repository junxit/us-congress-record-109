# NATIONAL PARK SYSTEM STUDY--CASTLE NUGENT FARMS, ST. CROIX, VIRGIN ISLANDS

```
  NATIONAL PARK SYSTEM STUDY--CASTLE NUGENT FARMS, ST. CROIX, VIRGIN 
                                ISLANDS

  The bill (H.R. 318) to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
study the suitability and feasibility of designating Castle Nugent 
Farms located on St. Croix, Virgin Islands, as a unit of the National 
Park System, and for other purposes, was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
