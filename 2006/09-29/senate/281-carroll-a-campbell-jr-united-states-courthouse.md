# CARROLL A. CAMPBELL, JR. UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

```
           CARROLL A. CAMPBELL, JR. UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

constructed in Greenville, South Carolina, as the ``Carroll A. 
Campbell, Jr. United States Courthouse'' was considered, ordered to a
```
