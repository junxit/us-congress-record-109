# GENE VANCE POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4805

```
                    GENE VANCE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 4805) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 105 North Quincy Street in Clinton, Illinois, 
as the ``Gene Vance Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
