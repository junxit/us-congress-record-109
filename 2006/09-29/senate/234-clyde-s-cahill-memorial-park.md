# CLYDE S. CAHILL MEMORIAL PARK

```
                     CLYDE S. CAHILL MEMORIAL PARK

  A bill (H.R. 1556) to designate a parcel of land located on the site 
of the Thomas F. Eagleton United States Courthouse in St. Louis, 
Missouri, as the ``Clyde S. Cahill Memorial Park'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
