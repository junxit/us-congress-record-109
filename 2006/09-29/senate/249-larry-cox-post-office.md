# LARRY COX POST OFFICE

```
                         LARRY COX POST OFFICE

  A bill (H.R. 5434) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 40 South Walnut Street in Chillicothe, Ohio, 
as the ``Larry Cox Post Office'' was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
