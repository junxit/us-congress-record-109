# JACOB SAMUEL FLETCHER POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               JACOB SAMUEL FLETCHER POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 5564) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 110 Cooper Street in Babylon, New York, as 
the ``Jacob Samuel Fletcher Post Office Building'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
