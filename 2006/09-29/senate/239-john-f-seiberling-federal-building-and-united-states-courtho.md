# JOHN F. SEIBERLING FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

```
    JOHN F. SEIBERLING FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

   A bill (H.R. 6051) to designate the Federal building and United 
States courthouse located at 2 South Main Street in Akron, Ohio, as the 
``John F. Seiberling Federal Building and United States Courthouse'' 
was considered, ordered a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
