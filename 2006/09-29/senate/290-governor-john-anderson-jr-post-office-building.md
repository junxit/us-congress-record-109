# GOVERNOR JOHN ANDERSON, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4674

```
            GOVERNOR JOHN ANDERSON, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 4674) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 110 North Chestnut Street in Olathe, Kansas, 
as the ``Governor John Anderson, Jr. Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
