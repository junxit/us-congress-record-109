# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate will return to legislative session.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, we have completed a lot of business. We may 
have a little more business in a bit. While we are conducting that 
business, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
