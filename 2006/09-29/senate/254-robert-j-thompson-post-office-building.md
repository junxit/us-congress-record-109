# ROBERT J. THOMPSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                ROBERT J. THOMPSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6075) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 101 East Gay Street in West Chester, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``Robert J. Thompson Post Office Building'', was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
