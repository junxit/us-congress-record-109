# LARRY WINN, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  LARRY WINN, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5504) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 6029 Broadmoor Street in Mission, Kansas, as 
the ``Larry Winn, Jr. Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
