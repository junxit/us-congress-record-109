# WILLIAM M. STEIGER FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

```
    WILLIAM M. STEIGER FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

States courthouse located at 221 and 211 West Ferguson Street in Tyler, 
Texas, as the ``William M. Steger Federal Building and United States 
Courthouse'' was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third
```
