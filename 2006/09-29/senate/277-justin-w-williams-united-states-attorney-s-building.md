# JUSTIN W. WILLIAMS UNITED STATES ATTORNEY'S BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1463

```
          JUSTIN W. WILLIAMS UNITED STATES ATTORNEY'S BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1463), to designate a portion of the Federal building 
located at 2100 Jamieson Avenue, in Alexandria, Virginia, as the 
``Justin W. Williams United States Attorney's Building'', was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
