# SALT CEDAR AND RUSSIAN OLIVE CONTROL DEMONSTRATION ACT

```
         SALT CEDAR AND RUSSIAN OLIVE CONTROL DEMONSTRATION ACT

  The bill (H.R. 2720) to further the purposes of the Reclamation 
Projects Authorization and Adjustment Act of 1992 by directing the 
Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Commissioner of 
Reclamation, to carry out an assessment and demonstration program to 
control salt cedar and Russian olive, and for other purposes, was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
