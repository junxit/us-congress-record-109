# ROBERT LINN MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               ROBERT LINN MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4768) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 777 Corporation Street in Beaver, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``Robert Linn Memorial Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
