# THOMAS J. MANTON POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 THOMAS J. MANTON POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 6033) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 39-25 61st Street in Woodside, New York, as 
the ``Thomas J. Manton Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
