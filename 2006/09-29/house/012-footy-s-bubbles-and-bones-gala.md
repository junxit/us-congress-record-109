# FOOTY'S BUBBLES AND BONES GALA

```
                     FOOTY'S BUBBLES AND BONES GALA

  (Ms. ROS-LEHTINEN asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Ms. ROS-LEHTINEN. Mr. Speaker, I would like to recognize south 
Florida Y-100 radio station, and especially John Kross, known to our 
south Florida community as ``Footy,'' for this year's sixth annual 
Anti-Drug Bubbles and Bones Gala. Proceeds benefit Here's Help, a 
private, nonprofit, comprehensive rehabilitation agency that caters to 
inner-city youth with substance dependency and addiction.
  In my congressional district, programs such as these have been 
instrumental in saving the lives of many teenagers and young adults by 
helping them cope with their addictions.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Footy on this 
wonderful drug rehab program. This event will provide assistance to so 
many young people in need to make sure that they one day can live in a 
Nation where drug and alcohol addictions are no longer a fact of their 
lives.

                          ____________________
```
