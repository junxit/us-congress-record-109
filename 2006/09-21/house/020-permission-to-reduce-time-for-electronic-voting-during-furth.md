# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING FURTHER PROCEEDINGS TODAY

```
    PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING FURTHER 
                           PROCEEDINGS TODAY

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, during 
further proceedings today, the Chair be authorized to reduce to 2 
minutes the minimum time for electronic voting on any question that 
otherwise could be subjected to 5-minute voting under clause 8 or 9 of 
rule XX.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
