# MOTION TO CLOSE CONFERENCE COMMITTEE MEETINGS ON H.R. 5631, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007, WHEN CLASSIFIED NATIONAL SECURITY INFORMATION IS UNDER CONSIDERATION

**Speaking:** Young, C. W. Bill (Y000031) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 5631

```
MOTION TO CLOSE CONFERENCE COMMITTEE MEETINGS ON H.R. 5631, DEPARTMENT 
OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007, WHEN CLASSIFIED NATIONAL SECURITY 
                   INFORMATION IS UNDER CONSIDERATION

  Mr. YOUNG of Florida. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 12 of rule 
XXII, I move that meetings of the conference between the House and the 
Senate on H.R. 5631 be closed to the public at such times as classified 
national security information may be broached, providing that any 
sitting Member of the Congress shall be entitled to attend any meeting 
of the conference.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 12 of rule XXII, the 
motion is not debatable, and the yeas and nays are ordered.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 411, 
nays 12, not voting 9, as follows:
```
