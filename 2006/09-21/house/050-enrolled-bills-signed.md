# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mrs. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker;

       H.R. 3408. An act to reauthorize the Livestock Mandatory 
     Reporting Act of 1999 and to amend the swine reporting 
     provisions of that Act.
       H.R. 3858. An act to amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster 
     Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to ensure that State and 
     local emergency preparedness operational plans address the 
     needs of individuals with households pets and service animals 
     following a major disaster or emergency.
```
