# LIGHTS ON AFTERSCHOOL

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 116

```
                         LIGHTS ON AFTERSCHOOL

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the HELP 
Committee be discharged from further consideration, and the Senate now 
proceed to S. Con. Res. 116.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the concurrent resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 116) supporting 
     ``Lights On Afterschool,'' a national celebration of after 
     school programs.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
```
