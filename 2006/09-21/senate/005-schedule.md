# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, today, we will begin a 30-minute period of 
morning business, which will be equally divided between the two sides. 
Following that morning business, we will return to the motion to 
proceed to the border fence act. Cloture on the motion to proceed was 
unanimously invoked yesterday. The postcloture time will expire at 5:45 
this afternoon. We will be on the bill at that time, or if an agreement 
is reached with the Democratic leader, we hope to proceed to it at an 
earlier time. Senators will be alerted as to the prospects for rollcall 
votes as we determine what the rest of today's schedule is.
```
