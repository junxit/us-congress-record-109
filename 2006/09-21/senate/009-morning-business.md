# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 30 
minutes, with the first half of the time under the control of the 
Democratic leader or his designee, and the second half of the time 
under the control of the majority leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Michigan is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
