# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3925

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 3925

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3925

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3925) to provide certain authorities for the 
     Secretary of State and the Broadcasting Board of Governors, 
     and for other purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I now ask for a second reading, and in 
order to place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule 
XIV, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.
```
