                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                 Week of June 19 through June 24, 2006

                             Senate Chamber

  On Monday, at 2 p.m., Senate will resume consideration of S. 2766, 
National Defense Authorization; at 4 p.m., Senate will begin
