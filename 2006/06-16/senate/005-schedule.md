# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, today, at 10:45, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the Department of Defense authorization bill. The 
bill's two managers, Chairman Warner and Senator Levin, will be here to 
continue to work on this important bill. Although there will be no 
votes during today's session, Senators are encouraged to come today and 
speak on the bill. Senators who are intending to offer amendments 
should be consulting with the managers on getting their amendments in 
the queue.
  Senators are reminded there is a scheduled vote for 5:30 on Monday on 
a U.S. circuit judge nomination. We could possibly have additional 
votes on Monday evening on amendments to the Defense bill.
```
