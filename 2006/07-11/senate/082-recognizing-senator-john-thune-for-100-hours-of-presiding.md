# RECOGNIZING SENATOR JOHN THUNE FOR 100 HOURS OF PRESIDING

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
       RECOGNIZING SENATOR JOHN THUNE FOR 100 HOURS OF PRESIDING

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I wish to take a moment to recognize the 
Presiding Officer, Senator John Thune, for tonight reaching 100 hours 
of presiding. Senator Thune should be commended for his perseverance. 
He often rushes to the floor to preside for only 10 minutes as we close 
our business for the day, making 100 hours seem almost unattainable. 
Congratulations to our Presiding Officer.

                          ____________________
```
