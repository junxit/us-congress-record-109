# PERMITTING USE OF CAPITOL ROTUNDA FOR 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 427

```
     PERMITTING USE OF CAPITOL ROTUNDA FOR 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE 
                     DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 427, which was 
received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 427) permitting the 
     use of the rotunda of the Capitol for a ceremony to 
     commemorate the 75th anniversary of the establishment of the 
     Department of Veterans Affairs.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent 
resolution be agreed to, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to the bill be printed in the 
Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 427) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
