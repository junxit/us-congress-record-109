# ABRAHAM LINCOLN BIRTHPLACE POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2894

```
            ABRAHAM LINCOLN BIRTHPLACE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2894) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 102 South Walters Avenue in Hodgenville, 
Kentucky, as the ``Abraham Lincoln Birthplace Post Office Building,''
```
