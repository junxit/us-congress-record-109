# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 4519, H.R. 4659

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mrs. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker:

       H.R. 4519. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     extend funding for the operation of State high risk health 
     insurance pools.
       H.R. 4659. An act to amend the USA PATRIOT Act to extend 
     the sunset of certain provisions of such act.
```
