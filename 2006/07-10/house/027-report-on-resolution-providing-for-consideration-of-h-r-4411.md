# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4411, UNLAWFUL INTERNET GAMBLING ENFORCEMENT ACT OF 2006

**Measures:** H.Res. 907, H.R. 4411

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4411, UNLAWFUL 
               INTERNET GAMBLING ENFORCEMENT ACT OF 2006

  Mrs. CAPITO, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 109-551) on the resolution (H. Res. 907) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4411) to prevent the use of certain 
payment instruments, credit cards, and fund transfers for unlawful 
Internet gambling, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
