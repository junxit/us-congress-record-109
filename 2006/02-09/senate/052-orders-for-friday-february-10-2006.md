# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 10, 2006

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 10, 2006

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand in recess until 9:30 a.m. on Friday, February 
10; the time for the two leaders be reserved, and the Senate proceed to 
a period of morning business until 10 a.m.; further, that at 10 a.m. 
the Senate resume consideration of S. 852, the asbestos bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
