# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. ALEXANDER. On behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous consent that 
there now be a period of morning business with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
allowed to speak for up to 10 minutes in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator is recognized for 10 minutes.
```
