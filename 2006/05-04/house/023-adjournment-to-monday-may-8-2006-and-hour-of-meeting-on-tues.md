# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 8, 2006, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, MAY 9, 2006

**Speaking:** Price, Tom (P000591) [R-GA]

```
ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 8, 2006, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, MAY 
                                9, 2006

  Mr. PRICE of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday next, 
and further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 
12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, May 9, 2006, for morning hour debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.
```
