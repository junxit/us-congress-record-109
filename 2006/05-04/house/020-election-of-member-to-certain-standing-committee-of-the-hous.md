# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEE OF THE HOUSE

```
     ELECTION OF MEMBER TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEE OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. CLYBURN. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Democratic Caucus, I 
offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 796) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution as follows:

                              H. Res. 796

       Resolved, That the following named Member be and is hereby 
     elected to the following standing committee of the House of 
     Representatives:
       (1) Committee on science.--Ms. Matsui.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
