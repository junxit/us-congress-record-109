# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Sovereign Lord, the way, the truth, and the life, give us the courage 
to follow You. Help us to follow You in our quest for ethical fitness. 
Help us to follow You in service to the lost, the lonely, and the 
least. Help us to follow You in going the second mile in our labors. 
Help us to follow You in loving our enemies, in blessing those who 
curse us, and in praying for those who misuse us.
  Today, guide our Senators with Your might. Empower them with wisdom 
and courage.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
