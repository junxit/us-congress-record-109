# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 19, 2006

**Speaking:** Chambliss, Saxby (C000286) [R-GA]

**Measures:** S. 2611

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 19, 2006

  Mr. CHAMBLISS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Friday, May 19; I further ask that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved, and the 
Senate resume consideration of S. 2611, the Comprehensive Immigration 
Reform Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
