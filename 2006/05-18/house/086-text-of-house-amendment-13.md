# Text of House Amendment 13

**Speaking:** Tiahrt, Todd (T000260) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 5386

```
H.R. 5386

                         Offered By: Mr. Tiahrt

       Amendment No. 13: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. _. None of the funds made available in this Act may be 
     used to promulgate regulations without consideration of the 
     effect of such regulations on the competitiveness of American 
     businesses.
```
