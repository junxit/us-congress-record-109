# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JUNE 12, 2006, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, JUNE 13, 2006

**Speaking:** Boehner, John A. (B000589) [R-OH]

```
 ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, JUNE 12, 2006, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, 
                             JUNE 13, 2006

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 12:30 p.m. on Monday next for 
morning hour debate; and further, when the House adjourns on that day, 
it adjourn to meet at 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, June 13, 2006, for morning 
hour debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
