# CONGRATULATIONS TROY McCURRY

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                      CONGRATULATIONS TROY McCURRY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, June 9, 2006

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I would like to recognize 
Troy McCurry, a native of Augusta, GA, and a graduate of the University 
of Georgia. Throughout the past 3 years, Troy has helped me serve the 
Second Congressional District. From working with legislative aides on 
special projects to helping communicate with citizens throughout South 
Carolina, he has been an important part of our team. I have truly 
appreciated his service and his commitment.
  A graduate of Catholic University Law School, Troy recently accepted 
a position with the Republican Governors Association. I am confident 
that he will bring the same level of professionalism and commitment to 
his new job. As Troy begins the next stage of his career, I would like 
to sincerely thank him for his efforts.

                          ____________________
```
