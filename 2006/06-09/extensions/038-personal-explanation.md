  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, I was detained the evening of June 6, 2006 
and wish to clarify any confusion as to how I intended to vote, most 
notably on rollcall 225, the motion to table the appeal of the ruling

manner: Rollcall 223, King Amendment to H.R. 5441--``aye''; rollcall 
224, Kingston Amendment to H.R. 5441--``aye''; rollcall 225, motion to 
table the appeal of the ruling of the chair--``aye''; rollcall 226, on 
passage of H.R. 5441--``aye.''
