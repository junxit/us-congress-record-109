# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S. 2348, S. 3771, S. 3879

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. INHOFE, from the Committee on Environment and Public 
     Works, with amendments:
       S. 3879. A bill to implement the Convention on 
     Supplementary Compensation for Nuclear Damage, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. No. 109-346).
       By Mr. INHOFE, from the Committee on Environment and Public 
     Works, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 2348. A bill to amend the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 to 
     require a licensee to notify the Atomic Energy Commission, 
     and the State and county in which a facility is located, 
     whenever there is an unplanned release of fission products in 
     excess of allowable limits (Rept. No. 109-347).
       By Mr. ENZI, from the Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions, with an amendment in the nature of a 
     substitute:
       S. 3771. A bill to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     provide additional authorizations of appropriations for the 
     health centers program under section 330 of such Act.
```
