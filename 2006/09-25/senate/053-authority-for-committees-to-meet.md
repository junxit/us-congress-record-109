# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


           committee on commerce, science, and transportation

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Commerce, Science, and Transportation be authorized to hold a 
business meeting, off-the-floor, on the nomination of Mary Peters to be 
Secretary of Transportation on Monday, September 25, 2006 after the 
first rollcall vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
