# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 992, H.R. 4772, H.R. 5059, H.R. 5062, H.R. 5092, H.R. 6102

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on motions to suspend the rules previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 5059, proceeding de novo;
  H.R. 5062, proceeding de novo;
  H.R. 6102, by the yeas and nays.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining votes in this series will be 5-minute votes.
  Postponed votes on H.R. 5092, H.R. 4772, and H. Res. 992 will be 
taken tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```
