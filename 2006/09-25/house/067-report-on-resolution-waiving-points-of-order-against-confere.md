# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 5631, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007

**Measures:** H.Res. 1037, H.R. 3127, H.R. 5631

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
      ON H.R. 5631, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007

  Mr. GINGREY (during consideration of H.R. 3127) from the Committee on 
Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 109-677) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 1037) waiving points of order against the 
conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 5631) making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2007, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
