# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. COLEMAN:
       S. 3883. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide an alternate sulfur dioxide removal measurement 
     for advanced coal-based generation technology units under the 
     qualifying advanced coal project credit; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
           By Mr. LUGAR:
       S. 3884. A bill to impose sanctions against individuals 
     responsible for genocide, war crimes, and crimes against 
     humanity, to support measures for the protection of civilians 
     and humanitarian operations, and to support peace efforts in 
     the Darfur region of Sudan, and for other purposes; read the 
     first time.
           By Mr. JOHNSON:
       S. 3885. A bill to amend Public Law 98-513 to provide for 
     the inheritance of small fractional interests within the Lake 
     Traverse Indian Reservation; to the Committee on Indian 
     Affairs.
           By Mr. FRIST (for himself and Mr. McConnell):
       S. 3886. A bill to authorize military commissions to bring 
     terrorists to justice, to strengthen and modernize terrorist 
     surveillance capabilities, and for other purposes; read the 
     first time.
           By Mr. DORGAN (for himself, Mrs. Murray, Ms. Mikulski, 
             Mr. Leahy, Mrs. Feinstein, Mr. Akaka, Mr. Kerry, Mr. 
             Kennedy, and Mr. Lieberman):
       S. 3887. A bill to prohibit the Internal Revenue Service 
     from using private debt collection companies, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
```
