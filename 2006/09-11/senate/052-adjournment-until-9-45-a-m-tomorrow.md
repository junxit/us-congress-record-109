# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:45 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S.Res. 565

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:45 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the provisions of S. Res. 565 as a further mark of 
respect for those who died as a result of the September 11, 2001, 
terrorist attacks.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 5:42 p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, September 12, 2006, at 9:45 a.m.
```
