# MEASURES PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 3882 and H.R. 503

```
           MEASURES PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 3882 and H.R. 503

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand there are two bills at the 
desk due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The leader is correct. The clerk will read the 
titles of the bills for a second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3882) to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
     support the war on terrorism, and for other purposes.
       A bill (H.R. 503) to amend the Horse Protection Act to 
     prohibit the shipping, transporting, moving, delivering, 
     receiving, possessing, purchasing, selling, or donation of 
     horses and other equines to be slaughtered for human 
     consumption, and for other purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. In order to place the bills on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceeding en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard, and the bills will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
