# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow we will continue to work through 
the port security bill. Members wishing to offer amendments should be 
consulting with the bill managers and be ready to debate their 
amendments tomorrow and Wednesday. Members should note that we will be 
voting throughout the day tomorrow, and the first vote will be at noon 
on Senator DeMint's national hazard alert system.
  In a few moments, Members of Congress will be marking the fifth 
anniversary of the terrorist attacks of 9/11 with a ceremony on the 
Capitol steps. Senators are reminded to meet in the Chamber by 5:45 
p.m. to walk over as a body for the 6 o'clock ceremony.

                          ____________________
```
