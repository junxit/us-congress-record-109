# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

```
      PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

submitted an adverse privileged report (Rept. No. 109-385) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 641) requesting the President to provide to the 
House of Representatives certain documents in his possession relating 
to electronic surveillance without search warrants on individuals in 
the United States, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered
```
