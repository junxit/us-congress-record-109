# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 2320, H.R. 3199

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 2320. An act to make available funds included in the 
     Deficit Reduction Act of 2005 for the Low-Income Home Energy 
     Assistance Program for fiscal year 2006, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate agree to the report of the 
committee of conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on 
the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 3199) entitled ``An Act 
to extend and modify authorities needed to combat terrorism, and for 
other purposes.''.

                          ____________________
```
