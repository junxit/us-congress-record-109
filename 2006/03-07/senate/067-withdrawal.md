# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
7, 2006 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       James Hardy Payne, of Oklahoma, to be United States Circuit 
     Judge for the Tenth Circuit, which was sent to the Senate on 
     September 29, 2005.
```
