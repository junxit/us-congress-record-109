# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
North Carolina, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess 
until 2:15 p.m. to accommodate the weekly party lunches and that the 
time will be counted postcloture.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:24 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Isakson).
```
