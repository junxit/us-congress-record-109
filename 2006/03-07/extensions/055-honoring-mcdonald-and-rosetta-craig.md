# HONORING McDONALD AND ROSETTA CRAIG

```
                  HONORING McDONALD AND ROSETTA CRAIG

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MARSHA BLACKBURN

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 7, 2006

  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege today to honor 
McDonald Craig, the owner of a Tennessee Century Farm in Linden, 
Tennessee. On Christmas Day in 1871, Craig's great-grandparents, Tapp 
and Amy, purchased the 110 acre farm. Tapp and Amy Craig, freed from 
slavery after the Civil War, were the first African-Americans to buy 
property in Perry County.
  The Craig family has owned and farmed this land in Perry County for 
more than 130 years. As a farmer and musician, Craig has not only 
maintained his family's legacy, but he has also made a name for himself 
as a country musician performing at local festivals and fairs.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing McDonald, his wife 
Rosetta, and their family for contributing so much to our community.

                          ____________________
```
