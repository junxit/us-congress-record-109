# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 26, 2006, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY NOURI AL-MALIKI, PRIME MINISTER OF THE REPUBLIC OF IRAQ

```
  AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 26, 
  2006, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY 
        NOURI AL-MALIKI, PRIME MINISTER OF THE REPUBLIC OF IRAQ

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may in 
order at any time on Wednesday, July 26, 2006, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Nouri Al-Maliki, Prime 
Minister of the Republic of Iraq.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Terry). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
