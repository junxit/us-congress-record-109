# WILLIAM F. CLINGER, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
              WILLIAM F. CLINGER, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3549) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 210 West 3rd Avenue in Warren, Pennsylvania, 
as the ``William F. Clinger, Jr. Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, read the third time, and passed.
```
