# DR. JOSE CELSO BARBOSA POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
              DR. JOSE CELSO BARBOSA POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3440) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 100 Avenida RL Rodriguez in Bayamon, Puerto 
Rico, as the ``Dr. Jose Celso Barbosa Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, read the third time, and passed.
```
