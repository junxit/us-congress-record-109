# PAUL KASTEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2977

```
                    PAUL KASTEN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2977) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 306 2nd Avenue in Brockway, Montana, as the 
``Paul Kasten Post Office Building,'' was considered, read the third 
time, and passed.
```
