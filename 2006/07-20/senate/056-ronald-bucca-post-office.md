# RONALD BUCCA POST OFFICE

```
                        RONALD BUCCA POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 4995) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 7 Columbus Avenue in Tuckahoe, New York, as 
the ``Ronald Bucca Post Office,'' was considered, read the third time,
```
