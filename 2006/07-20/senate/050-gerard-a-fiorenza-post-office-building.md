# GERARD A. FIORENZA POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                GERARD A. FIORENZA POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3934) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 80 Killian Road in Massapequa, New York, as 
the ``Gerard A. Fiorenza Post Office Building,'' was considered, read 
the third time, and passed.
```
