# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nominations on today's Executive Calendar: Calendar Nos. 599, 603, and

en bloc, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table, the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the 
Senate then return to legislative session.
