# House of Representatives

```
[[Page H4011]]

House of Representatives

  The House met at 10 a.m.
  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  God of wisdom and love, with the many issues facing the Nation 
regarding foreign policy, as well as national security and prosperity, 
Congress, this governing body of the people, by the people, and for the 
people, is ready to serve.
  Send Your light and spirit, Lord, to teach Members the depths of 
truth in the pursuit of justice. Guide all their actions that the bonds 
of unity and civility between civilian leadership, military forces and 
the populace may be strengthened, and in the end, all glory, power and 
honor be given to Your Holy Name, both now and forever. Amen.
```
