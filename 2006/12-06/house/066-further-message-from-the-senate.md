# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 5385

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with amendments in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 5385. An act making appropriations for the military 
     quality of life functions of the Department of Defense, 
     military construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, 
     and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
     2007, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate insists upon its 
amendments to the bill (H.R. 5385) ``An Act making appropriations for 
military quality of life functions of the Department of Defense, 
military construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2007, and for other 
purposes'', requests a conference with the House on the disagreeing 
votes of the two Houses thereon, and appoints
  Mrs. Hutchison, Mr. Burns, Mr. Craig, Mr. DeWine, Mr. Brownback, Mr. 
Allard, Mr. McConnell, Mr. Cochran, Mr. Stevens, Mrs. Feinstein, Mr. 
Inouye, Mr. Johnson, Ms. Landrieu, Mr. Byrd, Mrs. Murray, Mr. Leahy, 
and Mr. Harkin, to be the conferees on the part of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
