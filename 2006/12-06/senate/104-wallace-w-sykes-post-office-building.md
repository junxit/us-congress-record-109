# WALLACE W. SYKES POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5990

```
                 WALLACE W. SYKES POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5990) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 415 South 5th Avenue in Maywood, Illinois, as 
the ``Wallace W. Sykes Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
