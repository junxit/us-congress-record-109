# CHUCK FORTENBERRY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 CHUCK FORTENBERRY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6078) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 307 West Wheat Street in Woodville, Texas, as 
the ``Chuck Fortenberry Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
