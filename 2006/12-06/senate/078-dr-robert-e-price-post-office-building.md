# DR. ROBERT E. PRICE POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                DR. ROBERT E. PRICE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 4246) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 8135 Forest Lane in Dallas, Texas, as the 
``Dr. Robert E. Price Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
