# TRIBUTE TO SENATOR RICK SANTORUM

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO SENATOR RICK SANTORUM

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I rise to take a moment to congratulate my 
colleague from Pennsylvania, Senator Santorum, who spoke very 
eloquently about the world threat that we face today. Rick Santorum is 
someone of great passion. He is someone who is fearless. He is someone 
who, frankly, does not care whether people agree with him or do not 
agree with him.
  I will say this: This Senate is going to be a lesser body without 
Rick Santorum's great passion and his great drive, his great 
creativity. He will take those attributes out of this body, but I know 
that we will hear from him. He will be vocal. He will be concerned. He 
will be involved in whatever role he decides to assume after the first 
of January.

                          ____________________
```
