# CAPTAIN CHRISTOPHER PETTY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6102

```
             CAPTAIN CHRISTOPHER PETTY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6102) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 200 Lawyers Road, NW in Vienna, Virginia, as 
the ``Captain Christopher Petty Post Office Building'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
