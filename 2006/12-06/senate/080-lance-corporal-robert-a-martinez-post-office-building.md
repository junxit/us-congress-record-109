# LANCE CORPORAL ROBERT A. MARTINEZ POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
         LANCE CORPORAL ROBERT A. MARTINEZ POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 5108) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1213 East Houston Street in Cleveland, Texas, 
as the ``Lance Corporal Robert A. Martinez Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
