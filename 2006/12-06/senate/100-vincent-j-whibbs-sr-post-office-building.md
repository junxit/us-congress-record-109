# VINCENT J. WHIBBS, SR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5736

```
              VINCENT J. WHIBBS, SR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 5736) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 101 Palafox Place in Pensacola, Florida, as 
the ``Vincent J. Whibbs, Sr. Post Office Building'' was considered,
```
