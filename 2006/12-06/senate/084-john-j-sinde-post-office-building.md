# JOHN J. SINDE POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                   JOHN J. SINDE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5989) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 10240 Roosevelt Road in Westchester, 
Illinois, as the ``John J. Sinde Post Office Building'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
