# TITO PUENTE POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                    TITO PUENTE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 1472) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 167 East 124th Street in New York, New York, 
as the ``Tito Puente Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
