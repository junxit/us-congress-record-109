# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 2116, S. 2120

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, March 30, 2006, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 2116. An act to transfer jurisdiction of certain real 
     property to the Supreme Court.
       S. 2120. An act to ensure regulatory equity between and 
     among all dairy farmers and handlers for sales of packaged 
     fluid milk in federally regulated milk marketing areas and 
     into certain nonfederally regulated milk marketing areas from 
     federally regulated areas, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
