# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 1259, H.R. 4911

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mrs. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker:

       H.R. 1259. An act to award a congressional gold medal on 
     behalf of the Tuskogee Airmen, collectively, in recognition 
     of their unique military record, which inspired revolutionary 
     reform in the Armed Forces.
       H.R. 4911. An act to temporarily extend the programs under 
     the Higher Education Act of 1965, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
