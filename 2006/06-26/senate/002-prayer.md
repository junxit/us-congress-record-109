# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray:
  O God, who unites us with Your love, order our steps. May no passing 
irritation rob us of our appreciation for others. Keep us patient 
regarding human failings; permit us to see Your image in our world.
  Use our Senators to accomplish Your purposes. Give them wisdom to 
avoid majoring in minors or minoring in majors. As they offer You their 
best, give them Your abundant blessings. Give us all generous hearts 
and use us to bless Your world.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
