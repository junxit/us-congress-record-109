# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** DeGette, Diana (D000197) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 5672

```
H.R. 5672

                        Offered By: Ms. DeGette

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by increasing the amount made available for ``Office 
     of Justice Programs justice assistance'' (consisting of an 
     additional $5,000,000 for Internet Crimes Against children 
     Task Forces, as authorized by Public Law 105-119) and 
     reducing the amount made available under title I for 
     ``Department of justice general administration salaries and 
     expenses'', by $5,000,000.
```
