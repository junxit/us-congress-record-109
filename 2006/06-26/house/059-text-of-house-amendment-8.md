# Text of House Amendment 8

**Speaking:** Johnson, Eddie Bernice (J000126) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 5672

```
H.R. 5672

             Offered By: Ms. Eddie Bernice Johnson of Texas

       Amendment No. 8. At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available for 
     ``DEPARTMENT OF STATE AND RELATED AGENCY--Administration of 
     Foreign Affairs--educational and cultural exchange 
     programs'', and increasing the amount made available for 
     ``Office of Justice Programs--juvenile justice programs'', by 
     $9,872,000.
```
