# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Garrett, Scott (G000548) [R-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 5672

```
H.R. 5672

                 Offered By: Mr. Garrett of New Jersey

       Amendment No. 5: Page 110, after line 8, insert the 
     following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to send or otherwise

[[Page H4556]]

     pay for the attendance of more than 50 employees from a 
     Federal department or agency at any single conference 
     occurring outside the United States.
```
