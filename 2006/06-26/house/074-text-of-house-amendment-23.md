# Text of House Amendment 23

**Speaking:** Stearns, Cliff (S000822) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 5672

```
H.R. 5672

                        Offered By: Mr. Stearns

       Amendment No. 23: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the International Boundary and Water Commission, 
     United States and Mexico for new projects located solely in 
     Mexico until Mexico enforces its northern border.
```
