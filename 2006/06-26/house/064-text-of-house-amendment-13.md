# Text of House Amendment 13

**Speaking:** Mica, John L. (M000689) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 5672

```
H.R. 5672

                          Offered By: Mr. Mica

       Amendment No. 13: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used in contravention of the Buy American Act (41 U.S.C. 
     10a et seq.).
```
