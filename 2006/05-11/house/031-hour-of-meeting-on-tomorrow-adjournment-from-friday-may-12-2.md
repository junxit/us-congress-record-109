# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW, ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, MAY 12, 2006, TO MONDAY, MAY 15, 2006, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, MAY 16, 2006

**Speaking:** Boehner, John A. (B000589) [R-OH]

```
HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW, ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, MAY 12, 2006, TO 
   MONDAY, MAY 15, 2006, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, MAY 16, 2006

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. tomorrow; that when the 
House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday 
next, and further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to 
meet at 12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, May 16, 2006, for morning hour debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
