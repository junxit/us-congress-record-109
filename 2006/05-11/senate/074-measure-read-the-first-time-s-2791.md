# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2791

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2791

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2791

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk, and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2791) to amend titles 46 and 49, United States 
     Code, to provide improved maritime, rail, and public 
     transportation security, and for other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading, and 
in order to place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule 
XIV, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
