# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, this evening we have continued to work on 
an agreement for lobbying reform, as well as the border control bill 
which is scheduled for tomorrow. Under the agreement just reached, the 
cloture vote on the motion to proceed to the border control bill will 
be postponed to the afternoon unless further agreement is reached. We 
will notify Members as we lock in votes on either of the bills 
mentioned, and we do expect votes throughout Tuesday's session of the 
Senate.
  Before we close today's session, I want to again say how much the 
Senate mourns the loss of Erma Byrd, the wife of our esteemed 
colleague, Senator Byrd. As the Chaplain opened the Senate remembering 
her in prayer, it is only fitting to close honoring this lovely and 
remarkable lady. Our thoughts and prayers are with Senator Byrd and his 
entire family.

                          ____________________
```
