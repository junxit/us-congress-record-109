# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MARCH 13, 2006 AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, MARCH 14, 2006

**Speaking:** Boehner, John A. (B000589) [R-OH]

```
 ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MARCH 13, 2006 AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, 
                             MARCH 14, 2006

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday next, and 
further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 
12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, March 14, 2006, for morning hour debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Campbell of California). Is there 
objection to the request of the gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
```
