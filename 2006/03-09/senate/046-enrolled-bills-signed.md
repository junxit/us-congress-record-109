                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The following enrolled bills, previously signed by the Speaker of the 
House, were signed yesterday, March 8, 2006, by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens).

       H.R. 3199. An act to extend and modify authorities needed 
     to combat terrorism, and for other purposes.
       S. 2271. An act to clarify that individuals who receive 
     FISA orders can challenge nondisclosure requirements, that 
     individuals who receive national security letters are not 
     required to disclose the name of their attorney, that 
     libraries are not wire or electronic communication service 
     providers unless they provide specific services, and for 
     other purposes.
