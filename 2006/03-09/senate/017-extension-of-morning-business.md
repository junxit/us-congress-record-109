# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Coleman, Norm (C001057) [R-MN]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. COLEMAN. Mr. President, I also ask unanimous consent that the 
period of morning business be extended until 2 p.m. with the time 
equally divided in the usual form, and the time between 1:30 and 2 p.m. 
be reserved for the proponents and opponents.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
