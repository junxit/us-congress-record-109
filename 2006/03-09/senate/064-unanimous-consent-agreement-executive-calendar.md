# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 5:30 
p.m. on Monday, March 13, the Senate proceed to executive session and 
an immediate vote on the confirmation of Calendar No. 520, Leo Gordon 
to be a Judge of the United States Court of International Trade; 
provided further that following that vote the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action and the Senate resume legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
