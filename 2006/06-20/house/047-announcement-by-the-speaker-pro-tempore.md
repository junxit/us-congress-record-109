# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair must remind Members of a rule of 
decorum.
  The appropriate dress for Members in the Chamber while the House is 
in session is business attire, and this standard applies even when a 
Member enters the Chamber only to vote by electronic device.
  Without objection, 5-minute voting will continue.
  There was no objection.
```
