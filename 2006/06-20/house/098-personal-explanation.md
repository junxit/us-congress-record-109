# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. RANDY NEUGEBAUER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 20, 2006

  Mr. NEUGEBAUER. Mr. Speaker, due to official business, I missed 
rollcall votes 290 and 291 on Monday, June 19, 2006. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``yea'' on both votes. Rollcall vote 290 
was a vote to pass H.R. 5504, legislation to designate a post office in 
Mission, Kansas for Larry Winn, Jr. Rollcall vote 291 was a vote to 
pass H. Res. 826, a resolution expressing support for the creation of a 
National Youth Sports Week.
```
