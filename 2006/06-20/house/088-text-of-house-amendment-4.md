# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Stearns, Cliff (S000822) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 5631

```
H.R. 5631

                         Offered By Mr. Stearns

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                 TITLE X--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 10001. None of the funds made available in this Act 
     may be used to implement guidelines for military chaplains 
     that do not allow a chaplain covered by the guidelines to 
     pray according to the dictates of the chaplain's own 
     conscience, except as must be limited by military necessity.
```
