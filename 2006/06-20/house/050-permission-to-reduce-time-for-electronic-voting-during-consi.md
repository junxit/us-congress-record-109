# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5631, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007

**Speaking:** Boehner, John A. (B000589) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.Res. 877, H.R. 5631

```
PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF 
       H.R. 5631, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
consideration of the bill H.R. 5631, pursuant to House Resolution 877, 
the Chair may reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time for electronic 
voting under clause 6 of rule XVIII and clause 9 of rule XX.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
