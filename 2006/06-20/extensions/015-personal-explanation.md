# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Moran, Jerry (M000934) [R-KS]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JERRY MORAN

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 20, 2006

  Mr. MORAN of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, on June 19, 2006, I was unavoidably 
detained because of travel delays and missed the following rollcall 
votes:
  1. Rollcall vote No. 289: the Sergeant Jacob Dan Dones Post Office 
Designation Act;
  2. Rollcall vote No. 290: the Larry Winn, Jr., Post Office 
Designation Act; and
  3. Rollcall vote No. 291: Expressing the sense of the House of 
Representatives that a National Youth Sports Week should be 
established.
  Had I been present I would have voted ``yea'' to rollcall vote No. 
289, ``yea'' to rollcall vote No. 290, and ``yea'' to rollcall vote No. 
291.
```
