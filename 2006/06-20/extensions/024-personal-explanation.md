# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Myrick, Sue Wilkins (M001134) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.Res. 826, H.R. 5504, H.R. 5540

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. SUE WILKINS MYRICK

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 20, 2006

  Mrs. MYRICK. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to participate in the 
following votes due to a delayed flight. If I had been present, I would 
have voted as follows on June 19, 2006:
  Rollcall vote 289, on motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 
5540--to designate the facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 217 Southeast 2nd Street in Dimmitt, Texas, as the 
``Sergeant Jacob Dan Dones Post Office'', I would have voted ``aye.''
  Rollcall vote 290, on motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 
5504--to designate the facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 6029 Broadmoor Street in Mission, Kansas, as the ``Larry 
Winn, Jr. Post Office Building'', I would have voted ``aye.''
  Rollcall vote 291, on motion to suspend the rules and agree to H. 
Res. 826--Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives that a 
National Youth Sports Week should be established, I would have voted 
``aye.''

                          ____________________
```
