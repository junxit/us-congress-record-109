# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 5682, HENRY J. HYDE U.S.-INDIA PEACEFUL ATOMIC ENERGY COOPERATION ACT OF 2006

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
     ON H.R. 5682, HENRY J. HYDE U.S.-INDIA PEACEFUL ATOMIC ENERGY 
                        COOPERATION ACT OF 2006

  Mr. BISHOP of Utah, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 109-724) on the resolution (H. Res. 1101) 
waiving points of order against the conference report to accompany the 
bill (H.R. 5682) to exempt from certain requirements of the Atomic 
Energy Act of 1954 a proposed nuclear agreement for cooperation with 
India, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
