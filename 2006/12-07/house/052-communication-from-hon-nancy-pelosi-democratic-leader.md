# COMMUNICATION FROM HON. NANCY PELOSI, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
        COMMUNICATION FROM HON. NANCY PELOSI, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, December 7, 2006.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to section 1238(b)(3) of the 
     Floyd D. Spence National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal 
     Year 2001 (22 U.S.C. 7002), amended by Division P of the 
     Consolidated Appropriations Resolution, 2003 (22 U.S.C. 
     6901), I hereby reappoint Mr. Michael Wessel of Falls Church, 
     Virginia, to the United States-China Economic and Security 
     Review Commission for a term expiring December 31, 2008. His 
     current term expires December 31, 2006.
       In addition, I hereby appoint to that Commission Mr. 
     Jeffrey L. Fiedler of Great Falls, Virginia, to fill the 
     remainder of the term of Mr. George Becker, who is resigning 
     effective December 31, 2006. The current term on which Mr. 
     Fiedler succeeds Mr. Becker expires December 31, 2007.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     House Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
