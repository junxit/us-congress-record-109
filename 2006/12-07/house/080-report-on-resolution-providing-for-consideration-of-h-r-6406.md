# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6406, TRADE LAWS MODIFICATION

**Measures:** H.Res. 1100, H.R. 6406

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6406, TRADE 
                           LAWS MODIFICATION

  Mr. BISHOP of Utah, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 109-723) on the resolution (H. Res. 1100) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 6406) to modify 
temporarily certain rates of duty and make other technical amendments 
to the trade laws, to extend certain trade preference programs, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to
```
