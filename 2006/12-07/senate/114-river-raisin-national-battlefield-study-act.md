# RIVER RAISIN NATIONAL BATTLEFIELD STUDY ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 5132

```
              RIVER RAISIN NATIONAL BATTLEFIELD STUDY ACT

  The bill (H.R. 5132), to direct the Secretary of the Interior to 
conduct a special resource study to determine the suitability and 
feasibility of inc1uding in the National Park System certain sites in 
Monroe County, Michigan, relating to the Battles of the River Raisin 
during the War of 1812, was considered, ordered to a third reading, 
read the third time, and passed.
```
