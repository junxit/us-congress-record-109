# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR NO. 819

```
        UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR NO. 819

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 4:30 p.m. 
on Tuesday, September 5, the Senate proceed to executive session and 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Executive Calendar No. 819, 
Kimberly Ann Moore, to be U.S. circuit judge, with 1 hour of debate 
equally divided, to be followed by a vote on confirmation at 5:30 p.m., 
with no intervening action or debate. Finally, I ask unanimous consent 
that following that vote the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's previous action and the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
