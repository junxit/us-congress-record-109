# ORDER FOR SIGNING AUTHORIZATION

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                    ORDER FOR SIGNING AUTHORIZATION

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader, majority whip, and 
senior Senator from New Mexico be authorized to sign duly enrolled 
bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
