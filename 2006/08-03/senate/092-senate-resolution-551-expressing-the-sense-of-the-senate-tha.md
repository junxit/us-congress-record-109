# SENATE RESOLUTION 551--EXPRESSING THE SENSE OF THE SENATE THAT ILLEGAL IMMIGRANTS SHOULD NOT RECEIVE SOCIAL SECURITY BENEFITS AND THAT THIS PROHIBITION SHOULD BE STRICTLY ENFORCED

**Measures:** S.Res. 551

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 551--EXPRESSING THE SENSE OF THE SENATE THAT ILLEGAL 
 IMMIGRANTS SHOULD NOT RECEIVE SOCIAL SECURITY BENEFITS AND THAT THIS 
                PROHIBITION SHOULD BE STRICTLY ENFORCED

  Mr. REID (for himself, Mr. Baucus, Ms. Stabenow, Mr. Pryor, Mrs. 
Clinton, Mr. Lieberman, Mr. Bingaman, Ms. Landrieu, Mrs. Lincoln, Mr. 
Wyden, and Mr. Johnson) submitted the following resolution; which was 
referred to the Committee on Finance:

                              S. Res. 551

       Resolved, That it is the sense of the Senate that illegal 
     immigrants should not receive Social Security benefits and 
     that this prohibition should be strictly enforced.
```
