# HONORING MORGAN MAYSE OF ATHENS HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Hensarling, Jeb (H001036) [R-TX]

```
              HONORING MORGAN MAYSE OF ATHENS HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JEB HENSARLING

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 19, 2006

  Mr. HENSARLING. Mr. Speaker, today I would like to honor Morgan Mayse 
of Athens High School for her first place finish in the Texas 
University Interscholastic League State 4-A Track and Field Meet.
  On Friday May 12, 2006, Morgan competed at the University of Texas in 
Austin's Mike A. Myers Stadium for the Girls High Jump earning the gold 
with the record jump of 5'9".
  As the Congressional representative for Athens and the Fifth District 
of Texas, I would like to congratulate Morgan, as well as her family, 
coaches, and supporters on her tremendous undefeated season and her 
second straight District 14-AAAA and Region II-AAAA titles.

                          ____________________
```
