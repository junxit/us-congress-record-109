# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 866, H.R. 2808

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House reports that on September 18, 2006, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills:

       H.R. 866. to make technical corrections to the United 
     States Code.
       H.R. 2808. To require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint 
     coins in commemoration of the bicentennial of the birth of 
     Abraham Lincoln.
```
