# SENATE BILL REFERRED

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

  A bill of the Senate of the following title was taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 2463. An act to designate certain land in New England as 
     wilderness for inclusion in the National Wilderness 
     Preservation system and certain land as a National Recreation 
     Area, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Resources; 
     in addition to the Committee on Agriculture for a period to 
     be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
```
