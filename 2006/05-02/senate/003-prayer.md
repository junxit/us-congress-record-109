# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Sovereign Guide of humanity, we come to You as Your pilgrims in need 
of direction. We come as Your soldiers in need of strength for life's 
battles. We come as Your disciples in need of knowledge in our 
perplexity. We come as Your ambassadors in need of grace to represent 
You with honor.
  Today, as Senators serve as Your pilgrims, soldiers, disciples, and 
ambassadors, infuse them with wisdom. Provide them with insights for 
every challenge and help for every need.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
