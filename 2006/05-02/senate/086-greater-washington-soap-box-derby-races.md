# GREATER WASHINGTON SOAP BOX DERBY RACES

**Speaking:** Ensign, John (E000194) [R-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 349

```
                GREATER WASHINGTON SOAP BOX DERBY RACES

  Mr. ENSIGN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con Res. 349, just 
received from the House, and at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 349) authorizing the 
     use of the Capitol Grounds for the Greater Washington Soap 
     Box Derby.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consideration of 
the concurrent resolution.
  Mr. ENSIGN. I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent resolution be 
agreed to, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and that 
any statements be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 349) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
