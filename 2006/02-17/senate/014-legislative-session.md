# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN], Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
return to legislative session.
  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I should first propound an inquiry of the 
Chair: What is the situation? What is the order of business?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate is in a period of morning business, 
and Senators may speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The Senator has 9 minutes remaining.

                          ____________________
```
