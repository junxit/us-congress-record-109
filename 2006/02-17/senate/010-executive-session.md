# EXECUTIVE SESSION

```
                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nomination on 
today's Executive Calendar: Calendar No. 525. I further ask unanimous 
consent that the nomination be confirmed, the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table, the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action, and that the Senate then return to legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nomination considered and confirmed is as follows:

       Reginald I. Lloyd, of South Carolina, to be United States 
     Attorney for the District of South Carolina for the term of 
     four years, vice J. Strom Thurmond, Jr., resigned.
```
