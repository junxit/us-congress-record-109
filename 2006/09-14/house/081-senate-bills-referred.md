# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 1902, S. 2464

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 1902. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     authorize funding for the establishment of a program on 
     children and the media within the Centers for Disease Control 
     and Prevention to study the role and impact of electronic 
     media in the development of children; to the Committee on 
     Energy and Commerce.
       S. 2464. An Act to revise a provision relating to a 
     repayment obligation of the Fort McDowell Yavapai Nation 
     under the Fort McDowell Indian Community Water Rights 
     Settlement Act of 1990, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Resources.
```
