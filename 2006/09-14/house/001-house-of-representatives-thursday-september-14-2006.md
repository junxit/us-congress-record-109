# HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES--Thursday, September 14, 2006

```
[[Page 18233]]

         HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES--Thursday, September 14, 2006


  The House met at 9 a.m.
  Rabbi Amy Rader, B'nai Torah Congregation, Boca Raton, Florida, 
offered the following prayer:
  When the theologian of my tradition, Dr. Abraham Joshua Heschel, 
marched in Selma, Alabama, with Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., Rabbi 
Heschel said: ``My feet were praying.''
  Esteemed men and women in this Chamber, I ask for God's help to move 
our prayers from our lips to our feet. Our world is in desperate need 
of action, change, and presence. As the leaders of this sacred 
democracy, your feet in any one place can make the difference between 
life and death.
  May it be God's will that your feet lead our country on the path of 
compassion and justice. May your feet walk steadily to draw the 
estranged closer and the vulnerable into protection. May your feet 
stand firmly and united as the agents of freedom, equality, progress, 
and hope.
  Master of the universe, inspire our deeds to be their own prayers. 
May our work join with God's spirit to bring about a better day for all 
creation. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
