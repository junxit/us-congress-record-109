              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Under authority of the order of the Senate of January 18, 2006, the 
following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the first 
and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. REID (for himself, Mr. Durbin, Ms. Stabenow, Mr.

     laws and regulations, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
           By Mr. LAUTENBERG (for himself, Ms. Snowe, Mr. Schumer,

           By Mr. ISAKSON:

           By Mr. ROCKEFELLER (for himself, Mr. Reid, Mrs. Murray,

             Johnson, Ms. Cantwell, Mr. Akaka, Mr. Lieberman, Mr. 
             Kohl, Ms. Landrieu, Mr. Sarbanes, and Mrs. Boxer):
       S. 2183. A bill to provide for necessary beneficiary
