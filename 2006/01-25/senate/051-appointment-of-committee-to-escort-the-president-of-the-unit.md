# APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
 APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Presiding 
Officer of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part 
of the Senate to join with the like committee on the part of the House 
of Representatives to escort the President of the United States into 
the House Chamber for the joint session to be held at 9 p.m. on 
Tuesday, January 31, 2006.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
