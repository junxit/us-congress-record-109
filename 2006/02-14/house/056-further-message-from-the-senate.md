# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 71, H.Con.Res. 315

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment concurrent 
resolutions of the House of the following titles:

       H. Con. Res. 71. Concurrent resolution expressing the sense 
     of Congress that there should be established a Caribbean-
     American Heritage Month.
       H. Con. Res. 315. Concurrent resolution urging the 
     President to issue a proclamation for the observance of an 
     American Jewish History Month.

                          ____________________
```
