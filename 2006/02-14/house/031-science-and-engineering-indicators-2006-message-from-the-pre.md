# SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING INDICATORS, 2006--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING INDICATORS, 2006--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF 
                           THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Science:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Consistent with 42 U.S.C. 1863(j)(1), I transmit herewith a report 
prepared for the Congress and the Administration by the National 
Science Board entitled, ``Science and Engineering Indicators--2006.'' 
This report represents the seventeenth in the series examining key 
aspects of the status of science and engineering in the United States.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, February 14, 2006.

                          ____________________
```
