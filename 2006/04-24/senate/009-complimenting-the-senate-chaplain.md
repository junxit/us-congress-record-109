# COMPLIMENTING THE SENATE CHAPLAIN

```
                   COMPLIMENTING THE SENATE CHAPLAIN

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I first would like to acknowledge the prayer 
of Admiral Black, our good Chaplain. I hope that all those who heard 
his prayer, as I did, will take it to heart. Certainly, I am going to 
try to. I try to get here every day--and I am not able to do it every 
day--to hear his prayer. He is a remarkable man, and we are fortunate 
to have him as our Chaplain.

                          ____________________
```
