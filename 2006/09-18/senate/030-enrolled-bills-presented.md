# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 2590, S. 2784

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, September 18, 
2006, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bills:

       S. 2590. An act to require full disclosure of all entities 
     and organizations receiving Federal funds.
       S. 2784. An act to award a congressional gold medal to 
     Tenzin Gyatso, the Fourteenth Dalai Lama, in recognition of 
     his many enduring and outstanding contributions to peace, 
     non-violence, human rights, and religious understanding.

                          ____________________
```
