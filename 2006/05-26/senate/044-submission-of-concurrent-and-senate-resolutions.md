```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. BROWNBACK (for himself and Mr. Roberts):
       S. Res. 496. A resolution commending the Kansas City Kansas 
     Community College Debate Team for their National Championship

     May 21, 2006, as ``National Hurricane Preparedness Week''; to 
     the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
