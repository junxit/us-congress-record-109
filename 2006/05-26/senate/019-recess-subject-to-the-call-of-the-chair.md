# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from Georgia, I 
ask unanimous consent that the quorum call be rescinded.
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  In my capacity as a Senator from Georgia, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Senate stand in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, at 12:30 p.m., the Senate recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair.
  The Senate reassembled at 2:42 p.m., when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. Bennett).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
