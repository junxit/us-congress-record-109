# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 34

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       435. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature of 
     the State of Hawaii, relative to House Concurrent Resolution 
     No. 34 commemorating the Fifty-Second anniversary of the 
     detonation of the Bravo Hydrogen Bomb over Bikini Atoll, 
     declaring March 1st as a day of rememberance, and requesting 
     the Congress of the United States to enact appropriate 
     measures to provide for the full health needs of the hydrogen 
     bomb tests survivors and their progeny; to the Committee on 
     International Relations.

                          ____________________
```
