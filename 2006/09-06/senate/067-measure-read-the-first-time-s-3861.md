# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3861

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S. 3861

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3861

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk, 
S. 3861, and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will state the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3861) to facilitate bringing justice to 
     terrorists and other unlawful enemy combatants through full 
     and fair trials by military commissions, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask for a second reading, and in order 
to place the bill on the calendar under rule XIV, I object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will receive its second reading on 
the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
