# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Larson, John B. (L000557) [D-CT]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN B. LARSON

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 7, 2006

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, I was present and voting 
during the series of rollcall votes that included rollcall No. 226, 
final passage of the FY2007 Homeland Security Appropriations bill. 
While I believed that I had voted ``yea'' on the measure, apparently 
the electronic voting system did not register this vote. I would like 
to ensure that the record reflect that my vote, had it been recorded, 
would have been ``yea'' on rollcall No. 226.
```
