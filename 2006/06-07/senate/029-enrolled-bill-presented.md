# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, June 7, 2006, she 
had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bill:

       S. 1235. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     improve and extend housing, insurance, outreach, and benefits 
     programs provided under the laws administered by the 
     Secretary of Veterans Affairs, to improve and extend 
     employment programs for veterans under laws administered by 
     the Secretary of Labor, and for other purposes.
```
