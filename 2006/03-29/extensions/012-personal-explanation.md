# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gibbons, Jim (G000152) [R-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2120, H.R. 4882

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM GIBBONS

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 29, 2006

  Mr. GIBBONS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to explain how I would have 
voted on March 28, 2006 during rollcall votes No. 68 and No. 69 during 
the second session of the 109th Congress. The first vote was for H.R. 
4882--the Vietnam Veterans Memorial Visitor Center Deadline Enforcement 
Act--and the second was S. 2120--the Milk Regulatory Equity Act of 
2005.
  If present, I would have voted ``yes'' on these rollcall votes.
```
