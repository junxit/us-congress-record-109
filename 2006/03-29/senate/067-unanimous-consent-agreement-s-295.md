# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 295

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** S. 295

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 295

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
with respect to S. 295, the China currency bill, be modified to reflect 
a date no later than September 29, 2006, or the last day of the second 
session of the 109th Congress, whichever is earliest, and that all 
other provisos remain in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
