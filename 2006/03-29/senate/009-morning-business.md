# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for up to 1 hour, 
with the first 30 minutes under the control of the majority leader or 
his designee and the remaining 30 minutes under the control of the 
Democratic leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Alabama.
```
