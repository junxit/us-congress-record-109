# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 852, H.R. 4297

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will complete 
consideration of the pending motions to instruct the conferees on H.R. 
4297, the tax relief bill, and send that bill to conference. We will 
have multiple votes starting close to 10 a.m. Senators should plan to 
stay around the floor for a series of up to 16 stacked votes in the 
morning. I truly hope that some of those will not be pushed to a 
rollcall vote and we would finish at a reasonable time.
  Tomorrow afternoon, we will resume consideration of S. 852, the 
asbestos bill. There is a pending motion to waive the Budget Act that 
must be disposed of before we can move forward with the amendment 
process.

                          ____________________
```
