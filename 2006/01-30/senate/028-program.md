# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. INHOFE. Tomorrow morning at 11 o'clock we will vote on the 
confirmation of Judge Alito to be an Associate Justice on the Supreme 
Court. Senators should be seated at their desks for this historic vote. 
Following that vote, we will consider the nomination of Ben Bernanke to 
be Chairman of the Federal Reserve under a time agreement reached last 
week. Tomorrow evening we will proceed as a body to the House Chamber 
to hear the President's State of the Union Message, which is due to be 
delivered at 9 o'clock eastern standard time.
```
