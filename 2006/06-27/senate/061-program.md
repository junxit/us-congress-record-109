# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow the Finance Committee is expected 
to report the Oman Free Trade Agreement. That trade agreement is 
privileged, and we expect to turn to that as soon as it is made 
available. We hope we do not have to use all of the time allowed under 
the statute and, therefore, votes would occur tomorrow afternoon.
  This week we also have an important Cabinet nomination to address. 
That nomination is Henry Paulson to be the Secretary of the Treasury, 
and we will turn to the nomination when it is made available for 
consideration.
```
