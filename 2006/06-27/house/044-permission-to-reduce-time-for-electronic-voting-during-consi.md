# PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5672, SCIENCE, STATE, JUSTICE, COMMERCE, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007

```
PERMISSION TO REDUCE TIME FOR ELECTRONIC VOTING DURING CONSIDERATION OF 
  H.R. 5672, SCIENCE, STATE, JUSTICE, COMMERCE, AND RELATED AGENCIES 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, during 
consideration of H.R. 5672 pursuant to House Resolution 890, the Chair 
may reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time for electronic voting under 
clause 6 of rule XVIII and clause 9 of rule XX.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
