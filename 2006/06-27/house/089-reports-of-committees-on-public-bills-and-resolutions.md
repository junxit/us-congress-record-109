# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 4125

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. YOUNG of Alaska: Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure. H.R. 4125. A bill to permit the Administrator 
     of General Services to make repairs and lease space without 
     approval of a prospectus if the repair or lease is required 
     as a result of damages to buildings or property attributable 
     to Hurricane Katrina or Hurricane Rita (Rept. 109-532.) 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the State of 
     the Union.
```
