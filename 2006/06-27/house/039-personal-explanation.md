today, Tuesday, June 27, 2006, due to an excused absence. I would like

have voted ``yea.''
  On roll No. 321, On Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass, as Amended 
for the Veterans' Compensation Cost-of-Living Adjustment Act; I would 
have voted ``yea.''
