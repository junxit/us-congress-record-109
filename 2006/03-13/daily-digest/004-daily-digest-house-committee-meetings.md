# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
LEGISLATIVE ISSUES APPROPRIATIONS
Committee on Appropriations: On March 10, the Committee held a hearing 
on GPO, Library of Congress, Open World Leadership Center, GAO, and 
CBO. Testimony was heard from Bruce R. James, Public Printer, GPO; 
James H. Billington, Librarian of Congress; David M. Walker, 
Comptroller General, GAO; and Donald Marron, Acting Director, CBO.

Joint Meetings
EMPLOYMENT
Joint Economic Committee: On Friday, March 10, 2006, Committee 
concluded hearings to examine the employment-unemployment situation for 
February 2006, after receiving testimony from Kathleen P. Utgoff, 
Commissioner, Bureau of Labor Statistics, Department of Labor.
```
