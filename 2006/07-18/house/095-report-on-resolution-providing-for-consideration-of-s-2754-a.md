**Measures:** H.Res. 924, S. 2754

```
     REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. 2754, 
      ALTERNATIVE PLURIPOTENT STEM CELL THERAPIES ENHANCEMENT ACT

  Mr. GINGREY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 109-578) on the resolution (H. Res. 924) providing 
for consideration of the Senate bill (S. 2754) to derive human
```
