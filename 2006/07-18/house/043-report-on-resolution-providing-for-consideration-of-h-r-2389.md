# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2389, PLEDGE PROTECTION ACT OF 2005

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2389, PLEDGE 
                         PROTECTION ACT OF 2005

  Mr. GINGREY (during consideration of H.R. 860), from the Committee on 
Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 109-577) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 920) providing for consideration of the bill (H.R.

involving the Pledge of Allegiance, which was referred to the House
```
