# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mrs. CLINTON (for herself, Ms. Collins, and Mrs. 
             Lincoln):
       S. 3914. A bill to establish an Advisory Committee on 
     Gestational Diabetes, to provide grants to better understand 
     and reduce gestational diabetes, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. HARKIN (for himself, Mr. Durbin, Mr. Dodd, Mr. 
             Lautenberg, Mr. Schumer, and Mr. Johnson):
       S. 3915. A bill to amend title XIX of the Social Security 
     Act to encourage States to provide pregnant women enrolled in 
     the Medicaid program with access to comprehensive tobacco 
     cessation services; to the Committee on Finance.
```
