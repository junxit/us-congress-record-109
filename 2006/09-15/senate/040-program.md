# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President and Members of the Senate, next week we 
will resume consideration of the Oman free trade bill. Under the time 
agreement, we will have a vote on passage on Tuesday. Senators should 
expect this vote to occur before our weekly policy lunches. This will 
be the first vote of the week.
  We now also have an agreement to finish a very important nomination, 
that of Alice Fisher--of Louisville, KY, I might add--to be Assistant 
Attorney General for the Criminal Division. We will consider that 
nomination early next week as well. I thank all Members for their 
attention.

                          ____________________
```
