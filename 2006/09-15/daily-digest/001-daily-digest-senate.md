Fisher Nomination--Agreement: A unanimous-consent-time agreement was 
reached providing that at 2:15 p.m. on Tuesday, September 19, 2006, 
Senate begin consideration of the nomination of Alice S. Fisher, of 
Virginia, to be an Assistant Attorney General; that there be 90 minutes 
under the control of Senator Levin, 30 minutes under the control of 
Senator Leahy, 90 minutes under the control of Senator Specter, or his 
designee, 60 minutes under the control of Senator Reid, and 60 minutes 
under the control of Senator Frist and Senator McConnell; provided 
further, that following the use, or yielding back of time, Senate 
proceed to a vote on confirmation of the nomination.

Doc. 109-3); and
