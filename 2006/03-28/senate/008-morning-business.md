# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for not to exceed 1 
hour, with the first 30 minutes under the control of the minority 
leader or his designee, and the remaining 30 minutes under the control 
of the majority leader or his designee.
```
