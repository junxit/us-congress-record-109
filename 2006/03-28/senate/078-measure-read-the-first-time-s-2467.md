# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2467

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2467

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2467

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.

[[Page S2482]]

  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2467) to enhance and improve the trade relations 
     of the United States trade enforcement efforts and 
     encouraging United States trading partners to adhere to the 
     rules and norms of international trade, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I now ask for a second reading and in order to place 
the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I object to 
my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
