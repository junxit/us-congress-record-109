# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Our God and Savior, You are eternal love, and all peoples are 
embraced by Your spirit. Show Your love to this Congress, shower Your 
wisdom upon all who work in public office for the good of Your people 
to build a just society.
  Draw us closer into Your love and peace. Teach us to follow Your 
ways, that we may become capable of true love ourselves and be a 
fountain of living water in the midst of a thirsting world.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
