# MEDICARE PART D

```
                            MEDICARE PART D

  (Mr. DAVIS of Illinois asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, yesterday, I was pleased to join 
with Representative Jan Schakowsky, Representative Dan Lipinski, a 
grass-roots community group known as Citizens Action, a large number of 
senior citizens, as well as a group of retired workers, calling for the 
redesign and redevelopment of part D of the Medicare program.
  As a matter of fact, the seniors who were there all condemned 
everything that they had come into contact with relative to the 
terrible frustration. I hope that Americans all across the country 
would join with us to revise Medicare part D.
```
