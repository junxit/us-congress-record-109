# AMENDING THE IRAN AND LIBYA SANCTIONS ACT OF 1996

```
           AMENDING THE IRAN AND LIBYA SANCTIONS ACT OF 1996

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 5877, which was received 
from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5877) to amend the Iran and Libya Sanctions 
     Act of 1996 to extend the authorities provided in such Act 
     until September 29, 2006.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read a third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to the bill be printed in the 
Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 5877) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.

                          ____________________
```
