# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
               MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2005, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on February 17, 2006, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, received a message from the House of 
Representatives announcing that the Speaker has signed the following 
enrolled bill:

       H.R. 4745. An act making supplemental appropriations for 
     fiscal year 2006 for the Small Business Administration's 
     disaster loans program, and for other purposes.


[[Page 2143]]


  Under authority of the order of the Senate of February 17, 2006, the 
enrolled bill was signed subsequently on Friday, February 17, 2006, by 
the Majority Leader (Mr. Frist).

                          ____________________
```
