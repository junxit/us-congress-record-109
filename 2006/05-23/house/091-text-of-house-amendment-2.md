# Text of House Amendment 2

**Speaking:** Doolittle, John T. (D000429) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 5441

```
H.R. 5441

                       Offered By: Mr. Doolittle

       Amendment No. 2: Page 62, after line 17, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. 537. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to carry out expedited removal of aliens under 
     section 235 of the Immigration and Nationality Act unless 
     inadmissible citizens and nationals of El Salvador are 
     subject to the expedited removal procedures set forth in such 
     section to the same extent as other inadmissible aliens.
```
