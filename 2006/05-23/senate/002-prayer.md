# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty Creator, the source of blessings, thank You for blessing us 
with the opportunity to work with a diverse group of people from 
different racial and religious backgrounds. Thank You for the strength 
and courage to face this new day.
  Help our Senators to produce legislation to guide America on a proper 
course. Clear their minds and speak to their hearts so that they will 
succeed in their worthwhile endeavors.
  Strengthen us all to tackle life's challenges as You unite us to 
achieve Your will. Bless us with the forbearance to forgive and work 
even with our enemies. Hear our prayer and guide us to Your salvation. 
We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
