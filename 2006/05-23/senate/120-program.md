# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow morning we will be debating 
Senator McConnell's amendment related to ballots. That vote will occur 
at approximately 9:30 a.m., and that will be the first vote of the day. 
That will be followed by the cloture vote on the immigration bill. We 
have an agreement in place that will allow other amendments to be 
offered, and therefore everyone can expect another lengthy day of 
votes. I do thank everyone for allowing us to line up amendments as 
agreed to over the course of the day. I expect that cloture will be 
invoked tomorrow morning and that we will then finish this bill later 
on Wednesday or Thursday at the latest.
```
