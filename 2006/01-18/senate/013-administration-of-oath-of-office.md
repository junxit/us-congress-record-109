# ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

```
                    ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator-designate will present himself 
at the desk and the Chair will administer the oath of office as 
required by the Constitution and prescribed by law.
  The Senator-designate, Robert Menendez, escorted by Mr. Lautenberg, 
advanced to the desk of the Vice President; the oath prescribed by law 
was administered to him by the President pro tempore, and he subscribed 
to the oath in the Official Oath Book.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Congratulations.
  (Applause, Senators rising.)

                          ____________________
```
