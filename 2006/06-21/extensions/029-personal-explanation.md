# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Pombo, Richard W. (P000419) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 826, H.R. 5504, H.R. 5540

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. RICHARD W. POMBO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 20, 2006

  Mr. POMBO. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to vote on June 19, 2006 on the 
House floor. I take responsibility to vote very seriously and would 
like my intentions included in the Congressional Record.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall 289 for 
H.R. 5540, which designates the facility of the United States Postal

[[Page E1229]]

Service located at 217 Southeast 2nd Street in Dimmitt, Texas, as the 
Sergeant Jacob Dan Dones Post Office; ``yea'' on rollcall 290 for H.R. 
5504, which designates the facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 6029 Broadmoor Street in Mission, Kansas, as the Larry Winn, 
Jr. Post Office Building; and ``yea'' on rollcall 291 for H. Res. 826, 
expressing the sense of the House of Representatives that a National 
Youth Sports Week should be established.
```
