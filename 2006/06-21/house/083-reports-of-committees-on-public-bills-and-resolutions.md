# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 885, H.Res. 886, H.R. 4890, H.R. 5638

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. HASTINGS of Washington: Committee on Rules. House 
     Resolution 885. Resolution providing for consideration of the 
     bill (H.R. 5638) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to increase the unified credit against the estate tax to an 
     exclusion equivalent of $5,000,000 and to repeal the sunset 
     provision for the estate and generation-skipping taxes, and 
     for other purposes (Rept. 109-517). Referred to the House 
     Calendar.
       Mr. PUTNAM: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 886. 
     Resolution providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 
     4890) to amend the Congressional and Impoundment Act of 1974 
     to provide for the expedited consideration of certain 
     proposed rescissions of budget authority (Rept. 109-518). 
     Referred to the House Calendar.

                          ____________________
```
