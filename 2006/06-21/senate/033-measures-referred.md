# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1285, H.R. 4356, H.R. 5631

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1285. An act to amend the Nursing Relief for 
     Disadvantaged Areas Act of 1999 to remove the limitation for 
     nonimmigrant classification for nurses in health professional 
     shortage areas; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       H.R. 4356. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, 
     with respect to fraud in connection with major disaster or 
     emergency funds; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       H.R. 5631. An act making appropriations for the Department 
     of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2007, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Appropriations.
```
