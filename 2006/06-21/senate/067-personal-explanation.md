# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN BOOZMAN

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 20, 2006

  Mr. BOOZMAN. Mr. Speaker, as the chairman of the Economic 
Opportunities Subcommittee under the House Veterans' Affairs Committee, 
I traveled to South Dakota on Sunday, June 18, 2006, to hold a field 
hearing. Due to the timing of the hearing and return travel, I was 
unable to participate in votes on Monday, June 19, 2006. I am 
requesting my absence for this date. I would also like you to note 
that, should I have been present, I would have voted in the following 
manner: H.R. 5540--Sergeant Jacob Dan Dones Post Office Designation 
Act, ``yes''; H.R. 5504--Larry Winn, Jr. Post Office Building 
Designation Act, ``yes''; and H. Res. 826--Expressing the sense of the 
House of Representatives that a National Youth Sports Week should be 
established, ``yes.''
```
