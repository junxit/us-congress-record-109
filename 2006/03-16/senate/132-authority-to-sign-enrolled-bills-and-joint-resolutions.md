# AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
         AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader and the senior Senator 
from New Mexico be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint 
resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
