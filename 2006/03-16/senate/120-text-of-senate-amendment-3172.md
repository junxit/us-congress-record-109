       On page 27, line 24, decrease the amount by $9,000,000.
       On page 28, line 2, decrease the amount by $31,000,000.
       On page 28, line 5, decrease the amount by $65,000,000.
       On page 28, line 8, increase/decrease the amount by 
     $95,000,000.
