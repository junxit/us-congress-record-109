# APPOINTMENT OF HON. ROBERT B. ADERHOLT, HON. MICHAEL K. SIMPSON, AND HON. WAYNE T. GILCHREST TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MARCH 28, 2006

```
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. ROBERT B. ADERHOLT, HON. MICHAEL K. SIMPSON, AND 
HON. WAYNE T. GILCHREST TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED 
           BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MARCH 28, 2006

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, March 16, 2006.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Robert B. Aderholt, the 
     Honorable Michael K. Simpson, and the Honorable Wayne T. 
     Gilchrest to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled 
     bills and joint resolutions through March 28, 2006.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
