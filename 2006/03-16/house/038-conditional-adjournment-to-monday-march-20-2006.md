# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MARCH 20, 2006

```
           CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MARCH 20, 2006

  Mr. HUNTER. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today pursuant to this order, it adjourn to meet at noon 
on Monday, March 20, 2006, unless it sooner has received a message from 
the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 
361, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Drake). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
