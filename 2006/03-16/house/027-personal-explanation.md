# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. FRANKS of Arizona. Mr. Speaker, I regret that I was unable to be 
present for rollcall votes No. 50, 51, 52, 53, and 54. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``aye'' on the amendment offered by Ms. 
Foxx (rollcall vote No. 50), ``no'' on the amendment offered by Mr. 
Melancon (rollcall vote No. 51), ``no'' on both amendments offered by 
Mr. Jefferson (rollcall votes Nos. 52 and 53) and ``no'' on the 
amendment offered by Mr. Taylor (rollcall vote No. 54).
  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Chairman, I move that the Committee do now rise.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Accordingly, the Committee rose; and the Speaker pro tempore (Mr. 
Marchant) having assumed the chair, Mr. Chocola, Acting Chairman of the 
Committee of the Whole House on the State of the Union, reported that 
that Committee, having had under consideration the bill (H.R. 4939) 
making emergency supplemental appropriations for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2006, and for other purposes, had come to no resolution 
thereon.
```
