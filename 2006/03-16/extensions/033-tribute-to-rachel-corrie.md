# TRIBUTE TO RACHEL CORRIE

**Speaking:** McKinney, Cynthia A. (M000523) [D-GA]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO RACHEL CORRIE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CYNTHIA McKINNEY

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 16, 2006

  Ms. McKINNEY. Mr. Speaker, today is the third anniversary of the 
murder of Rachel Corrie, an American who was crushed to death by 
Israel's American-manufactured Caterpillar bulldozers. Rachel was 
protesting the Israeli Army's illegal bulldozing of Palestinian homes 
in the Gaza Strip. The Corrie family has filed suit against 
Caterpillar, charging it with knowingly selling machines used to 
violate human rights.
  From the Rachel Corrie website I found a link to these lyrics by Ten 
Foot Pole entitled, ``Rachel Corrie.''
```
