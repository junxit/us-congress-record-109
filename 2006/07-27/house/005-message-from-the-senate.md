# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 250, S. 3741

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 3741. An act to provide funding authority to facilitate 
     the evacuation of persons from Lebanon, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate agrees to the report of 
the committee of conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses 
on the amendments of the House to the bill (S. 250) ``An Act to amend 
the Carl D. Perkins Vocational and Technical Education Act of 1998 to 
improve the Act.''

                          ____________________
```
