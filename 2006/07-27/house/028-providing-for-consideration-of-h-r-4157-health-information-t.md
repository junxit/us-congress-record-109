                          Legislative Program

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I know a lot of Members are interested in 
what the schedule is. I think all of you know that today we will move 
to consider the conference report on the Carl Perkins vocational 
education program. We will then move to the health IT bill. We expect 
that that will take us to late afternoon/early evening.
  The reason I stood up is that it is pretty clear that we are in fact 
going to have votes tomorrow. There are a number of Members, though, 
from New York who want to go to former Representative Tom Manton's 
funeral. We will work with those Members to carve out a window so that 
those Members who want to go to New York can come back.
  But we will have votes tomorrow. I wish I could tell you what those 
votes would be, but I expect we are going to have votes tomorrow.
