# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 3711

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, today the Senate continued consideration of 
S. 3711, the gulf coast Energy bill. This morning we filed cloture on 
the bill, and that cloture vote will occur at 5:30 p.m. on Monday. I 
encourage Senators to come to the floor on Friday to speak on the 
Energy bill.
  I notified all Senators actually about a week ago that we would be 
voting for sure next Monday. Although we are doing our best to 
accommodate Senators, it is a very important vote, and we will be 
having it at 5:30 p.m. on Monday. I ask Senators to adjust their 
schedules so they can be here.
```
