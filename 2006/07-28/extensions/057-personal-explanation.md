# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** DeGette, Diana (D000197) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 5013

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DIANA DeGETTE

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 28, 2006

  Ms. DeGETTE. Mr. Speaker, I am listed as voting ``yea'' during 
rollcall vote number 401 on H.R. 5013, the ``Disaster Recovery Personal 
Protection Act of 2006,'' when it was before the House of 
Representatives on Tuesday, July 25, 2006. This is an error. I oppose 
H.R. 5013 and want it noted that had my intention been properly 
expressed I would be recorded as having voting ``nay.''

                          ____________________
```
