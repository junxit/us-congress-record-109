# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 2, 2006

**Speaking:** Cannon, Chris (C000116) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 459

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 2, 2006

  Mr. CANNON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today pursuant to this order, it adjourn to meet at 11 a.m. on 
Wednesday, August 2, 2006, unless it sooner has received a message from 
the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 
459, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Utah?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
