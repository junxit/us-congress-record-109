# WILFRED EDWARD "COUSIN WILLIE" SIEG, SR. POST OFFICE ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 5169

```
[[Page S8668]]
       WILFRED EDWARD ``COUSIN WILLIE'' SIEG, SR. POST OFFICE ACT

  The bill (H.R. 5169) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1310 Highway 64 NW. in Ramsey, Indiana, as 
the ``Wilfred Edward `Cousin Willie' Sieg, Sr. Post Office,'' was
```
