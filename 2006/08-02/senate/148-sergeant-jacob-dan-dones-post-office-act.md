**Measures:** H.R. 5540

```
                SERGEANT JACOB DAN DONES POST OFFICE ACT

  The bill (H.R. 5540) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 217 Southeast 2nd Street in Dimmitt, Texas, 
as the ``Sergeant Jacob Dan Dones Post Office,'' was ordered to a third
```
