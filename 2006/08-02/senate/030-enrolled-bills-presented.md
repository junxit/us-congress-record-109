# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 250, S. 3693, S. 3741

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, August 2, 2006, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 250. An act to amend the Carl D. Perkins Vocational and 
     Technical Education Act of 1998 to improve the Act.
       S. 3693. An act to make technical corrections to the 
     Violence Against Women Act and Department of Justice 
     Reauthorization Act of 2005.
       S. 3741. An act to provide funding authority to facilitate 
     the evacuation of persons from Lebanon, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
