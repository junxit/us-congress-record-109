# JOHN PAUL HAMMERSCHMIDT POST OFFICE ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 4811

```
                JOHN PAUL HAMMERSCHMIDT POST OFFICE ACT

  The bill (H.R. 4811) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 215 West Industrial Park Road in Harrison, 
Arkansas, as the ``John Paul Hammerschmidt Post Office Building,'' was 
ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
