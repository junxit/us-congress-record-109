# EARL D. HUTTO POST OFFICE BUILDING ACT

```
                 EARL D. HUTTO POST OFFICE BUILDING ACT

  The bill (H.R. 5107) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1400 West Jordan Street in Pensacola, 
Florida, as the ``Earl D. Hutto Post Office Building,'' was ordered to 
a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
