# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 467

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, pursuant to the previous 
order of the House of today, the House stands adjourned until 4 p.m. on 
Friday, August 4, 2006, unless it sooner has received a message from 
the Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 
467, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.
  There was no objection.
  Thereupon, (at 11 o'clock and 8 minutes a.m.), pursuant to the 
previous order of the House of today, the House adjourned until 4 p.m. 
on Friday, August 4, 2006, unless it sooner has received a message from 
the Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 
467, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.

                          ____________________
```
