# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, AUGUST 4, 2006

```
           CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, AUGUST 4, 2006

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, when the House adjourns 
today, it shall adjourn to meet at 4 p.m. on Friday, August 4, 2006, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 467, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
