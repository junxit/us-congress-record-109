# CONGRATULATING MR. AND MRS. HENRY HYDE

**Speaking:** Kirk, Mark Steven (K000360) [R-IL], Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

```
                 CONGRATULATING MR. AND MRS. HENRY HYDE

  (Mr. KIRK asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. KIRK. Mr. Speaker, I would like to announce to the House that 
Henry Hyde has made a major decision in his life.
  Mr. Speaker, I yield to the gentleman from Indiana.
  Mr. PENCE. On behalf of an extraordinary career, but what we know 
will be an extraordinary future, I am pleased to announce Henry Hyde's 
recent marriage on the day after Thanksgiving. We wish you all the 
best, our friend, for many, many years.
```
