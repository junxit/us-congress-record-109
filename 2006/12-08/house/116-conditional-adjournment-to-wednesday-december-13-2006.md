# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2006

**Speaking:** Boehner, John A. (B000589) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 503

```
        CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2006

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns on this legislative day pursuant to this order, it adjourn to 
meet on the third Constitutional day thereafter, unless it sooner has 
received a message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in 
House Concurrent Resolution 503, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
