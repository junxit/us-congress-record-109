# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 102

```
                    ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  At 1:20 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by on of its clerks, announced that the Speaker has signed the 
following enrolled joint resolution:

       H.J. Res. 102. Joint resolution making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2007, and for other 
     purposes.

  The joint resolution was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens).

                          ____________________
```
