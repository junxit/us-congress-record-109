# Text of Senate Amendment 5236

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
  SA 5236. Mr. FRIST proposed an amendment to the bill H.R. 6111, to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide that the Tax Court 
may review claims for equitable innocent spouse relief and to suspend 
the running on the period of limitations while such claims are pending; 
as follows:

       At the end of the House amendment, add the following:
       This Act shall become effective 2 days after the date of 
     enactment.
```
