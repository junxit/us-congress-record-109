# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader, the junior Senator from 
Virginia, and the junior Senator from Mississippi be authorized to sign 
duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
