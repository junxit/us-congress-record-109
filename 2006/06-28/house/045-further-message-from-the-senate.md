# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 889

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of 
conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment 
of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 889) ``An Act to authorize 
appropriations for the Coast Guard for fiscal year 2006, to make 
technical corrections to various laws administered by the Coast Guard, 
and for other purposes.''.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Committee will resume its sitting.

                          ____________________
```
