# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 448, H.R. 5684

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:32 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Chiappardi, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
has passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of 
the Senate:

       H.R. 5684. An act to implement the United States-Oman Free 
     Trade Agreement.

  The message also announced that the House has agreed to the following 
concurrent resolution, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 448. Concurrent resolution commending the 
     National Aeronautics and Space Administration on the 
     completion of the Space Shuttle's second Return-to-Flight 
     mission.

                          ____________________
```
