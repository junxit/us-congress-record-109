# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
POLICING CAPITAL SITES
Committee on Government Reform: Held a hearing entitled ``Policing 
Capital Sites: Improving Coordination, Training and Equipment.'' 
Testimony was heard from the following officials of the Department of 
Defense: RADM Terence McKnight, USN, Commandant, Naval District 
Washington, Department of the Navy; and MG Guy C. Swan, III, USA, 
Military District of Washington, Department of the Army; Joseph W. 
Trindal, Regional Director, National Capital Region, Federal Protective 
Service, Immigration and Customs Enforcement, Department of Homeland 
Security; and Michael D. Fogarty, Assistant Chief of Police, U.S. Park 
Police, National Park Service, Department of the Interior.
```
