# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HON. CHARLIE NORWOOD, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
    COMMUNICATION FROM THE HON. CHARLIE NORWOOD, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Charlie Norwood, Member of Congress:
                                                    U.S. Congress,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, July 17, 2006.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representtives, Washington DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to notify you formally, pursuant 
     to rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I have been served with a civil deposition subpoena, 
     issued by the Superior Court of Fulton County, GA, for 
     documents and testimony.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the precedents and privileges of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Charlie Norwood,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
