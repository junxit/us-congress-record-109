# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. COBURN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Energy and Natural Resources be authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Monday, July 17, 2006, at 2:30 p.m. The 
purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony relating to the 
implementation of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 Provisions on Hydrogen 
and Fuel Cell Research and Development.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

[[Page 14525]]




                     committee on foreign relations

  Mr. COBURN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Foreign Relations be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on Monday, July 17, 2006, at 3 p.m. to hold nominations 
hearings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
