# MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--H.R. 5020

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 5020

```
                 MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--H.R. 5020

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the bill for 
the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5020) to authorize appropriations for fiscal 
     year 2007 for intelligence and intelligence-related 
     activities of the U.S. Government, the Community Management 
     Account, and the Central Intelligence Agency Retirement and 
     Disability System, and for other purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, in order to place the bill on the calendar 
under the provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be placed on 
the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
