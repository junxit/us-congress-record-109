# CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF JOSHUA WILLIAM ALBON

```
             CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF JOSHUA WILLIAM ALBON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 14, 2006

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, today I am happy to 
congratulate Captain and Mrs. Brian Albon, USMC, of Jacksonville, North 
Carolina, on the birth of their new baby son. Joshua William Albon was 
born on June 10, 2006, at 7:42 a.m., weighing 6 pounds and 13 ounces. 
Joshua has been born into a loving home, where he will be raised by 
parents who are devoted to his well-being and bright future. His birth 
is a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
