# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 1445

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  At 6:01 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 1445. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 520 Colorado Avenue in 
     Arriba, Colorado, as the ``William H. Emery Post Office''.
```
