**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   REFERRAL OF DISCHARGED NOMINATION

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
nomination of Randall M. Fort to be Assistant Secretary of State be
```
