# APPOINTMENT OF ESCORT COMMITTEE

```
                    APPOINTMENT OF ESCORT COMMITTEE

  Mr. CHAMBLISS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
President of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the 
part of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the 
House of Representatives to escort the Honorable Silvio Berlusconi, 
Prime Minister of the Republic of Italy, into the House Chamber for a 
joint meeting tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
