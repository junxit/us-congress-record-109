# CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF ELLA BRIDGES STEGMAIER

```
            CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF ELLA BRIDGES STEGMAIER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 28, 2006

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, today I am happy to 
congratulate Paige and Christian Stegmaier of Chapin, SC, on the birth 
of their new baby daughter. Ella Bridges Stegmaier was born on February 
16, 2006, at 3:10 p.m., weighing 7 pounds and 8 ounces and measuring 20 
inches. Ella has been born into a loving home, where she will be raised 
by parents who are devoted to her well-being and bright future. Her 
birth is a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
