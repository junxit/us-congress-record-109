# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 4019. An act to amend title 4 of the United States 
     Code to clarify the treatment of self-employment for purposes 
     of the limitation on State taxation of retirement income.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed bills of the 
following titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 403. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
     prohibit taking minors across State lines in circumvention of 
     laws requiring the involvement of parents in abortion 
     decisions.
       S. 1950. An act to promote global energy security through 
     increased cooperation between the United States and India in 
     diversifying sources of energy, stimulating development of 
     alternative fuels, developing and deploying technologies that 
     promote the clean and efficient use of coal, and improving 
     energy efficiency.
       S. 2832. An act to reauthorize and improve the program 
     authorized by the Appalachian Regional Development Act of 
     1965.
       S. 3728. An act to promote nuclear nonproliferation in 
     North Korea.

                          ____________________
```
