# RECESS

**Speaking:** Enzi, Michael B. (E000285) [R-WY], Lautenberg, Frank R. (L000123) [D-NJ]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
having arrived and passed, the Senate stands in recess until 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:37 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:15 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. 
Voinovich).
  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
