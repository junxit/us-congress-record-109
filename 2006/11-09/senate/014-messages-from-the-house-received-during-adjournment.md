# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 5, 2005, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on September 30, 2006, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, received a message from the House of 
Representatives announcing that the Speaker pro tempore (Mr. Wolf) has 
signed the following enrolled bills, previously signed by the Acting 
President pro tempore:

       H.R. 6138. An act to temporarily extend the programs under 
     the Higher Education Act of 1965, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 6198. An act to hold the current regime in Iran 
     accountable for its threatening behavior and to support a 
     transition to democracy in Iran.
```
