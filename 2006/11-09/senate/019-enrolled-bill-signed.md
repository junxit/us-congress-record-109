# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the Order of the Senate of January 5, 2005, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on October 23, 2006, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, received a message from the House of 
Representatives, announcing that the Speaker pro tempore (Mr. Davis of 
Virginia) had signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 6061. An act to establish operational control over the 
     international land and maritime borders of the United States.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 5, 2005, 
the enrolled bills were signed on October 23, 2006, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, by the Vice President.

                          ____________________
```
