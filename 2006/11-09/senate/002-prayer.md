# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Immortal, invisible, God only wise, You still work wonders in the 
Earth. Help us to possess the humility to learn. Teach us that prayer 
still brings results. Teach us that we need not fear what the future 
holds, for Your mercies endure forever. Teach us that Your blessings 
come new every day. Teach us to number our days, to appreciate life's 
precious moments. Teach us to trust Your love and to celebrate Your 
presence.
  Bless our Senators, their families, and their staffs in the days 
ahead. We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
