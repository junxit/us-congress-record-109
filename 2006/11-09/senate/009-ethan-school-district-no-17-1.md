# ETHAN SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 17-1

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                     ETHAN SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 17-1

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Ethan 
School District No. 17-1 for being named a No Child Left Behind Blue 
Ribbon School. The commitment to quality education that has been shown 
by the faculty, teachers, and students at the Ethan School District is 
truly invaluable in shaping the future leaders of this country. The 
work that they are doing to develop higher education standards and 
greater accountability serves as a model to other schools throughout 
our State and Nation.
  Again, congratulations to Ethan School District No. 17-1 for being 
named a Blue Ribbon School and for making South Dakota proud.●

                          ____________________
```
