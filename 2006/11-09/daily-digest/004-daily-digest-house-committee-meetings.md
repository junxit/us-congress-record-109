# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
OVA-POLLUTION IN THE POTOMAC
Committee on Government Reform: On October 4, the Committee held a 
hearing entitled ``Ova-Pollution in the Potomac: Egg-Bearing Male Bass 
and Implications for Human and Ecological Health.'' Testimony was heard 
from Benjamin Grumbles, Assistant Administrator, Office of Water, EPA; 
the following officials of the Department of the Interior: Mark Myers, 
Director; and Sue Haseltine, Associate Director of Biology, both with 
the U.S. Geological Survey; Gregory Masson, Chief, Branch of 
Environmental Contaminants, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service; and public 
witnesses.
```
