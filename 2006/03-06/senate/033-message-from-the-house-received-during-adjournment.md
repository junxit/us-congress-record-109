# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2005, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on March 3, 2006, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing the Speaker had signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 1777. An act to provide relief for the victims of 
     Hurricane Katrina.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed during the session of the 
Senate by the President pro tempore (Mr. Stevens).

                          ____________________
```
