# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S. 2320

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now resume consideration of S. 2320, the LIHEAP funding bill, and that 
the Kyl amendment be temporarily set aside so I may offer a first-
degree amendment. It is amendment No. 2898. I further ask that 
following my statement on the amendment, the Senate then proceed to a 
period for morning business, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
