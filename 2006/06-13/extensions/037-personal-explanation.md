# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Oxley, Michael G. (O000163) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 5522

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL G. OXLEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 13, 2006

  Mr. OXLEY. Mr. Speaker, I was absent from the House floor during 
Friday's rollcall votes on 5 amendments to H.R. 5522, the Fiscal Year 
2007 Foreign Operations appropriations measure.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``no'' on rollcalls 245, 246, 
247, 248, and 249.
  I would have voted in favor of final passage of H.R. 5522.
```
