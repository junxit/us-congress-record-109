# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 5576

```
H.R. 5576

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to apply the assumption contained in section 
     A150.101(d) of title 14, Code of Federal Regulations.
```
