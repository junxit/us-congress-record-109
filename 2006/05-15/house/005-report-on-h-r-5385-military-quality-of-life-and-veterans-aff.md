# REPORT ON H.R. 5385, MILITARY QUALITY OF LIFE AND VETERANS AFFAIRS APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007

```
  REPORT ON H.R. 5385, MILITARY QUALITY OF LIFE AND VETERANS AFFAIRS 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2007

  Mr. LEWIS of California, from the Committee on Appropriations, 
submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 109-464) on the bill (H.R. 
5385) making appropriations for the military quality of life functions 
of the Department of Defense, military construction, the Department of 
Veterans Affairs, and related agencies for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2007, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
Union Calendar and ordered to be printed.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1, rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved on the bill.
```
