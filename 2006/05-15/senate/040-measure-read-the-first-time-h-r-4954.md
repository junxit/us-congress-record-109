# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 4954

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 4954

  Mr. FRIST. I understand there is a bill at the desk, and I ask for 
its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4954) to improve maritime and cargo security 
     through enhanced layered defenses, and for other purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading, and in 
order to place the bill on the calendar under provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will receive its second reading on the next legislative day.
```
