# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Hefley, Joel (H000444) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 5386

```
H.R. 5386

                         Offered By: Mr. Hefley

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill (before the short

                TITLE VI--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

     by 1 percent.
```
