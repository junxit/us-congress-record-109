# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 4297

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

                                 ______
                                 

                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  At 4:00 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by one of its clerks, announced that the Speaker has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 4297. An act to provide for reconciliation pursuant to 
     section 201(b) of the concurrent resolution on the budget for 
     fiscal year 2006.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens).
```
