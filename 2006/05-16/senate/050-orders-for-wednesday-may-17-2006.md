# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 17, 2006

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 17, 2006

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:15 
a.m. on Wednesday, May 17. I further ask that following the prayer and 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be

[[Page 8318]]

reserved, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 2611, the 
Comprehensive Immigration Reform Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
