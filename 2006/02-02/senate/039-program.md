# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, it has been a very long day, but a very 
productive day with the ultimate passage of the Tax Relief Act by a 
vote of 66 to 31. In addition, just a short time ago, we passed the 
temporary extension of a very important bill, the PATRIOT Act. We will 
start debate on the motion to proceed to the asbestos legislation on 
Monday. A cloture vote on the motion to proceed will occur on Tuesday 
at 6 p.m. We do have Members who will be attending the funeral of 
Coretta Scott King on Tuesday; therefore, we are postponing that vote 
until 6 p.m.
```
