# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Measures:** S. 22

```

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, on vote No. 115, I was necessarily absent, 
due to a mechanical problem with the plane on my United flight 115 from 
Chicago. Had I been present for that vote, I would have voted against

cloture on the motion to proceed to S. 22--I was necessarily absent due
```
