# ENROLLMENT OF S. 1932

```
                         ENROLLMENT OF S. 1932

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now proceed to the 
consideration of S. Con. Res. 80, which was submitted earlier today, 
the resolution be agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon 
the table.

[[Page 1209]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 80) was considered and agreed 
to, as follows:

                            S. Con. Res. 80

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That the enrollment of the bill S. 1932 as 
     presented to the President for his signature on February 8, 
     2006, is deemed the true enrollment of the bill reflecting 
     the intent of the Congress in enacting the bill into law.

                          ____________________
```
