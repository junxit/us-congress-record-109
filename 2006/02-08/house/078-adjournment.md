# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Wasserman Schultz, Debbie (W000797) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 332

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 332, 109th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Campbell of California). Accordingly, 
pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 332, 109th Congress, the House 
stands adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, February 14, 2006.
  Thereupon (at 11 o'clock and 21 minutes p.m.), pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 332, the House adjourned until Tuesday, February 
14, 2006, at 2 p.m.
```
