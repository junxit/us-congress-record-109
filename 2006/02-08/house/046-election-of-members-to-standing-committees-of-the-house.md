# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

```
        ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. GINGREY. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 671) and I 
ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 671

       Resolved, That the following Members be and are hereby 
     elected to the following standing committees of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on Appropriations: Mr. DeLay to rank after Mr. 
     Wolf.
       Committee on the Budget: Mr. Campbell of California.
       Committee on Energy and Commerce: Mr. Blunt to rank after 
     Mr. Fossella.
       Committee on Financial Services: Mr. Campbell of 
     California.
       Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Mr. Campbell of California.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
