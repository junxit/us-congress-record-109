# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO SECRETARY OF DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY

```
 PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO SECRETARY OF DEPARTMENT 
                          OF HOMELAND SECURITY

  Mr. Rogers of Alabama, from the Committee on Homeland Security, 
submitted an adverse privileged report (Rept. No. 109-484) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 809) directing the Secretary of the Department of 
Homeland Security to transmit to the House of Representatives not later 
than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution 
documents in the Secretary's possession relating to any existing or 
previous agreement between the Department of Homeland Security and 
Shirlington Limousine and Transportation, Incorporated, of Arlington, 
Virginia, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
