# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 7, 2006, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HER EXCELLENCY VAIRA VIKE-FREIBERGA, PRESIDENT OF LATVIA

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 7, 2006, 
  FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HER EXCELLENCY VAIRA 
                  VIKE-FREIBERGA, PRESIDENT OF LATVIA

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it 
may be in order at any time on Wednesday, June 7, 2006, for the Speaker 
to declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose 
of receiving in joint meeting Her Excellency Vaira Vike-Freiberga, 
President of Latvia.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Alabama?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
