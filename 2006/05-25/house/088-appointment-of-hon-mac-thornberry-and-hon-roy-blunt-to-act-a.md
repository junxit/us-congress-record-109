# APPOINTMENT OF HON. MAC THORNBERRY AND HON. ROY BLUNT TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 6, 2006

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. MAC THORNBERRY AND HON. ROY BLUNT TO ACT AS SPEAKER 
 PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 
                                6, 2006

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:


                                               Washington, DC,

                                                     May 25, 2006.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Mac Thornberry and the 
     Honorable Roy Blunt to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign 
     enrolled bills and joint resolutions through June 6, 2006.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                                         House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointments are 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
