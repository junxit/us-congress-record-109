# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Tancredo, Thomas G. (T000458) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 5441

```
H.R. 5441

                        Offered By: Mr. Tancredo

       Amendment No. 15: Page 62, after line 17, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. 537. None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to carry out the diversity visa program established 
     in section 203(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 
     U.S.C. 1153(c)).
```
