# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow, we will be in session at 8:45 in 
the morning, and we expect to proceed to a vote on the Kavanaugh 
nomination, to be followed by a vote on the Hayden nomination, and a 
cloture vote on the Kempthorne nomination. Thus, Senators can expect 
three votes very early tomorrow morning. Those votes should begin 
shortly after we convene at 8:45 a.m. I thank my colleagues for their 
work on the immigration bill that we passed earlier today.
```
