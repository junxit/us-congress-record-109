# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, today the Senate passed the PATRIOT Act 
amendments bill, and we are now considering the PATRIOT Act conference 
report. Early today, by a vote of 84 to 15, the Senate overwhelmingly 
invoked cloture on the conference report. Under an agreement just 
reached, we will be voting on the PATRIOT Act conference report 
tomorrow at 3 p.m. Following that vote, we will have a cloture vote 
with respect to the LIHEAP bill. The remaining schedule for tomorrow 
will depend on the outcome of that vote, and we will alert Members of 
the schedule following that cloture vote.
```
