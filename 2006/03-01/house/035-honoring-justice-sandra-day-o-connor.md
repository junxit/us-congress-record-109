  Mr. BURTON of Indiana. Mr. Speaker, due to illness I was regrettably

H. Res. 357, a bill to honor Justice Sandra Day O'Connor on the 
occasion of her retirement from the United States Supreme Court and to 
commend her for her hard work and dedication to the law.
  Had I been here I would have unquestionably voted ``yea'' on rollcall 
vote 17.
  Ms. JACKSON-LEE of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably detained at 
the United States Supreme Court, which is hearing the Texas 
redistricting case. Had I been present, I would have voted an 
enthusiastic ``yea'' on the Sandra Day O'Connor resolution.
