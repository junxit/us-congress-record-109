                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. IKE SKELTON

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 15, 2006

  Mr. SKELTON. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall vote 511, which was on the 
question of passage of H.R. 4772, the Private Property Rights 
Implementation Act, I inadvertently voted ``no.'' This bill was 
considered on September 29, 2006, and approved by the House on a vote 
of 231 to 181.
  My intent was to vote ``aye'' on rollcall vote 511, and I wish the 
Congressional Record to indicate my support for this legislation.
