# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2006

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2006

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today pursuant to this order, it adjourn to meet at 1:30 p.m. 
on Friday, November 17, 2006, unless it sooner has received a message 
from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent 
Resolution 496, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant 
to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
```
