# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

```
     ELECTION OF MEMBER TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 1083) and I 
ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 1083

       Resolved, That the following Member be and is hereby 
     elected to the following standing committees of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on Education and the Workforce: Ms. Sekula Gibbs.
       Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Ms. Sekula 
     Gibbs.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid upon the table.

                          ____________________
```
