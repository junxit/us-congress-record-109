# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2791

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 2791

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2791

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2791) to amend titles 46 and 49, United States 
     Code, to provide improved maritime, rail, and public 
     transportation security, and for other purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be placed on 
the calendar.
```
