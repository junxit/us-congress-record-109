# MAKING IN ORDER MORNING HOUR DEBATE

**Speaking:** Dreier, David (D000492) [R-CA]

```
                  MAKING IN ORDER MORNING HOUR DEBATE

  Mr. DREIER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the order of 
the House of January 4, 2005, providing for morning hour debate be 
extended for the remainder of the 109th Congress except that the date 
of May 15, 2006, shall be used in lieu of May 16, 2005.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
California?
  There was no objection.
```
