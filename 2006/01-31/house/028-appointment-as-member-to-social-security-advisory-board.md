# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

```
        APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 703 of the Social 
Security Act (42 U.S.C. 903 note), the order of the House of December 
18, 2005, and upon the recommendation of the minority leader, the Chair 
announces the Speaker on January 18, 2006, appointed the following 
member on the part of the House to the Social Security Advisory Board 
for a term of 6 years:
  Mrs. Barbara Kennelly, Connecticut
```
