# ALLOCATION OF TIME

```
                           ALLOCATION OF TIME

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, before the majority leader leaves the floor, 
we have an hour of time divided before the 11 o'clock vote. I know the 
short amount of time we have is allocated to the very second. I am 
wondering if it would be appropriate to have a full hour prior to the 
vote. I guess my question is, How much time do you need on your side?

                          ____________________
```
