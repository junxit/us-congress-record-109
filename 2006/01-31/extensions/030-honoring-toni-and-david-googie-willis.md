# HONORING TONI AND DAVID "GOOGIE" WILLIS

**Speaking:** Higgins, Brian (H001038) [D-NY]

```
               HONORING TONI AND DAVID ``GOOGIE'' WILLIS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRIAN HIGGINS

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 31, 2006

  Mr. HIGGINS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Toni and David 
``Googie'' Willis of Dunkirk, New York for their leadership in raising 
funds for the Muscular Dystrophy Association.
  This amazing pair of dedicated people have been the organizers for 
the annual Muscular Dystrophy Association for many years.
  Their countless hours of volunteer work should never go unnoticed. 
These unsung heroes give their all in the effort to raise funds so that 
others may have a better life.
  Through the years Toni and Googie have helped raise $65,000 for the 
cause. Without their devotion and passion this would never be possible.
  Toni and Googie have given their all for a wonderful cause without 
stopping to think of themselves, that is why, Mr. Speaker, I rise to 
honor them today.

                          ____________________
```
