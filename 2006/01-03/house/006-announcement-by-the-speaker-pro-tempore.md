# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 619, H.Res. 640

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 2 of House Resolution 
619, 109th Congress, as amended by section 2 of House Resolution 640, 
109th Congress, organizational and legislative business is dispensed 
with on this day.
  The House will follow the practice announced by the Speaker at the 
convening of the Second Session of the 106th Congress under a similar 
order. Bills and resolutions introduced today will be numbered today 
but will not be noted in the Record or referred by the Speaker until 
January 31, 2006. Executive communications, petitions, and memorials 
will not be numbered or referred until January 31, 2006.

                          ____________________
```
