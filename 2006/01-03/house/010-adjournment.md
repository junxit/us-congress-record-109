# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Kildee, Dale E. (K000172) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 326

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. KILDEE. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 326, 
109th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.

January 31, 2006.

Concurrent Resolution 326, the House adjourned until Tuesday, January 
31, 2006, at noon.
```
