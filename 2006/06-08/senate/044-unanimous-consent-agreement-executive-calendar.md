# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday, June 13, at 2:30 p.m., immediately following the official 
photograph, there be 60 minutes equally divided for debate prior to the 
cloture vote on Executive Calendar No. 553, with 15 minutes under the 
control of Senator Byrd, and 15 minutes under the control of Senator 
Kennedy, and 30 minutes under the control of Chairman Enzi; provided 
further, that if cloture is invoked on the nomination, the Senate 
proceed to an immediate vote on the confirmation of the nomination, 
with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
