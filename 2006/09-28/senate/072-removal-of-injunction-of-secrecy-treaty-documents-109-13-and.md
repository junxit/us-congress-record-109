# REMOVAL OF INJUNCTION OF SECRECY--TREATY DOCUMENTS 109-13 AND 109-14

```
  REMOVAL OF INJUNCTION OF SECRECY--TREATY DOCUMENTS 109-13 AND 109-14

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that the injunction of secrecy be removed from the following 
agreements transmitted to the Senate on September 28, 2006, by the 
President of the United States:
  Mutual legal assistance agreement with the European Union, Treaty 
Document 109-13.
  Extradition agreement with the European Union, Treaty Document 109-
14.
  I further ask that the agreements be considered as having been read 
the first time; that they be referred, with accompanying papers, to the 
Committee on Foreign Relations and ordered to be printed; and that the 
President's messages be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
