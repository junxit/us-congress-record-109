# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 2250, S. 2491

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 2250. An act to award a congressional gold medal to Dr. 
     Norman E. Borlaug.
       S. 2491. An act to award a Congressional gold medal to 
     Byron Nelson in recognition of his significant contributions 
     to the game of golf as a player, a teacher, and a 
     commentator.
```
