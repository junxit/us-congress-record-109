# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 683, H.R. 1036, H.R. 3127, H.R. 5574

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mrs. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker:

       H.R. 683. An act to amend the Trademark Act of 1946 with 
     respect to dilution by blurring or tarnishment.
       H.R. 1036. An act to amend title 17, United States Code, to 
     make technical corrections relating to Copyright Royalty 
     Judges, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3127. An act to impose sanctions against individuals 
     responsible for genocide, war crimes, and crimes against 
     humanity, to support measures for the protection of civilians 
     and humanitarian operations, and to support peace efforts in 
     the Darfur region of Sudan, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 5574. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     reauthorize support for graduate medical education programs 
     in children's hospitals.

                          ____________________
```
