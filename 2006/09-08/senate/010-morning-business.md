# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous 
consent that the Senate proceed to a period of morning business with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be allowed 
to speak for as much time as I may consume.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
