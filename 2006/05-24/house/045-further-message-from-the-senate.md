# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with an amendment in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 5037. An act to amend title's 38 and 18, United States 
     Code, to prohibit certain demonstrations at cemeteries under 
     the control of the National Cemetery Administration and at 
     Arlington National Cemetery, and for other purposes.
```
