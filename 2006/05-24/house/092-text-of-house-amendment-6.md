# Text of House Amendment 6

**Speaking:** Poe, Ted (P000592) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 5441

```
H.R. 5441

                          Offered By: Mr. Poe

       Amendment No. 6: Page 62, after line 17, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. 537. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available in this Act may be used to implement a plan under 
     section 7209 of the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism 
     Prevention Act of 2004 (8 U.S.C. 1185 note) that permits 
     travel into the United States from foreign countries using 
     any document other than a passport to denote citizenship and 
     identity.
```
