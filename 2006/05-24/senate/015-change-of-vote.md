# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Smith, Gordon H. (S001142) [R-OR]

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. SMITH. Mr. President, on rollcall vote 140, I was recorded as 
voting nay. My intention was to vote yea. Therefore, I ask unanimous 
consent that I be permitted to change my vote since it will not affect 
the outcome.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
