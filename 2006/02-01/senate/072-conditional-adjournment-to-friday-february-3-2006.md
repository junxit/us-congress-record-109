# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 2006

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 2006

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Friday, February 
3, 2006, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 332, in 
which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
