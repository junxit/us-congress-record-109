# VACATING ORDERING OF YEAS AND NAYS ON H.R. 4659, USA PATRIOT ACT 5-WEEK EXTENSION

```
VACATING ORDERING OF YEAS AND NAYS ON H.R. 4659, USA PATRIOT ACT 5-WEEK 
                               EXTENSION

  Mr. SENSENBRENNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
ordering of the yeas and nays on H.R. 4659 be vacated to the end that 
the Chair put the question de novo.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Wisconsin?

[[Page 630]]

  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Wisconsin (Mr. Sensenbrenner) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, H.R. 4659.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
