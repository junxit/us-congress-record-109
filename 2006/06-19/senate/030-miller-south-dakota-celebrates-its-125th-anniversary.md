# MILLER, SOUTH DAKOTA, CELEBRATES ITS 125TH ANNIVERSARY

```
         MILLER, SOUTH DAKOTA, CELEBRATES ITS 125TH ANNIVERSARY

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I rise to recognize the 125th 
anniversary celebration of Miller, SD.
  Located in Hand County, the town of Miller was originally founded in 
1881 by pioneer Henry Miller. Since its founding 125 years ago, the 
community of

[[Page 11696]]

Miller has continued to serve as a strong example of South Dakota 
values and traditions.
  I offer my congratulations to Miller on this milestone accomplishment 
and wish them continued prosperity in the years to come.●
```
