# 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF STRATFORD, SOUTH DAKOTA

```
              100TH ANNIVERSARY OF STRATFORD, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize the city of 
Stratford which is celebrating its 100th anniversary.
  Located in Brown County, Stratford was founded in 1906 as an 
agricultural community. Stratford is a welcoming community that 
reflects the values and principles that we as Americans hold dear.
  It gives me great pleasure to rise with the citizens of Stratford to 
celebrate the 100th anniversary of their fine city.●

                          ____________________
```
