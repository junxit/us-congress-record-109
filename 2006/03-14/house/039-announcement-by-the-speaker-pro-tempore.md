# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 190, H.Con.Res. 354, H.R. 4944

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on motions to suspend the rules previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:

       H. Con. Res. 354, by the yeas and nays;
       H. Con. Res. 190, by the yeas and nays;
       H.R. 4944, by the yeas and nays.

  The first and third electronic votes will be conducted as 15-minute 
votes. The second vote in this series will be a 5-minute vote.

                          ____________________
```
