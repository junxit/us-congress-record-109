                    EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following executive reports of committees were submitted:

       *Randall L. Tobias, of Indiana, to be Administrator of the 
     United States Agency for International Development.
       *Mark D. Wallace, of Florida, to be Representative of the

     Representative of the United States of America to the 
     Sessions of the General Assembly of the United Nations, 
     during his tenure of service as Representative of the United 
     States of America to the United Nations for U.N. Management 
     and Reform.

     United States of America on the Economic and Social Council 
     of the United Nations, with the rank of Ambassador.
       *Richard T. Miller, of Texas, to be an Alternate

     of America on the Economic and Social Council of the United

     of the Overseas Private Investment Corporation.
