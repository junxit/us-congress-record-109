# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 3699, H.R. 4377

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Under authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2005, the 
Secretary of the Senate, on December 4, 2006, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker pro tempore (Mr. Wolf) has signed the 
following enrolled bills:

       H.R. 3699. An act to provide for the sale, acquisition, 
     conveyance, and exchange of certain real property in the 
     District of Columbia to facilitate the utilization, 
     development, and redevelopment of such property, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 4377. An act to extend the time required for 
     construction of a hydroelectric project, and for other 
     purposes.

  The enrolled bills were subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens)

                          ____________________
```
