# TRIBUTE TO ERIC RODAWIG

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ERIC RODAWIG

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I rise to thank Eric Rodawig, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the years.
  Eric is a graduate of Dakota Valley High School in Dakota Dunes, SD, 
where he was the Valedictorian. Currently he is attending Georgetown 
University where he is majoring in government and economics and is 
active in writing for the school newspaper, The Hoya. He is a hard 
worker and has been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I would like to rise and give my thanks to Eric and wish him 
continued success in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
