# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 457, S. 2013

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 457. An act to require the Director of the Office of 
     Management and Budget to issue guidance for, and provide 
     oversight of, the management of micropurchases made with 
     Governmentwide commercial purchase cards, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. 2013. An act to amend the Marine Mammal Protection Act 
     of 1972 to implement the Agreement on the Conservation and 
     Management of the Alaska-Chukotka Polar Bear Population.

                          ____________________
```
