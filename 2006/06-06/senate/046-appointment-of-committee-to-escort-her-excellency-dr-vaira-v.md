# APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT HER EXCELLENCY, DR. VAIRA VIKE- FREIBERGA, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF LATVIA

```
   APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT HER EXCELLENCY, DR. VAIRA VIKE-
             FREIBERGA, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF LATVIA

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
President of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the 
part of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the 
House of Representatives to escort Her Excellency Dr. Vaira Vike-
Freiberga, President of the Republic of Latvia, to the House Chamber 
for a joint meeting on Wednesday, June 7, 2006.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
