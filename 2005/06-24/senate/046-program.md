# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Bennett, Robert F. (B000382) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, on Monday, following morning business, 
the Senate will resume consideration of the Interior appropriations 
bill. Under a previous agreement, all amendments to the bill must be 
offered during Monday's session. There will be no rollcall votes on 
Monday, but Senators who have amendments to the bill should make 
themselves available to offer and debate their amendments. We will 
begin voting with respect to amendments to the Interior appropriations 
bill on Tuesday.
  I also inform our colleagues the next vote will occur on Tuesday 
morning shortly before 10 a.m. That vote will be on passage of H.R. 6, 
the Energy bill.
  Finally, on behalf of the majority leader, I would like to remind all 
Senators that next week will be the final week prior to the Fourth of 
July recess, so Senators should expect a busy week with rollcall votes 
throughout.

                          ____________________
```
