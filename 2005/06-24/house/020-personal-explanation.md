# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Wilson, Heather (W000789) [R-NM]

**Measures:** H.R. 3010

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mrs. WILSON of New Mexico. Mr. Speaker, because I attended a BRAC 
Commission hearing in New Mexico, I missed the vote on final passage of 
the Departments of Labor, Health and Human Services, and Education 
Appropriations Act for Fiscal Year 2006, H.R. 3010 (rollcall vote No. 
321). If I had been there, I would have voted no on final passage.
  Additionally, if I had been in attendance, I would have voted in 
favor of the Obey Amendment to restore funding for the Corporation for 
Public Broadcasting (rollcall vote No. 305).
```
