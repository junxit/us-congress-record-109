# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 810

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 810

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 810

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk. I 
ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 810) to amend the Public Service Act to 
     provide for human embryonic stem cell research.
  Mr. FRIST. I now ask for its second reading, and in order to place 
the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I object to 
my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be read for the second time on 
the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
