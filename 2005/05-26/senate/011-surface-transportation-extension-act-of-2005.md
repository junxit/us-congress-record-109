# SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2005

```
              SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2005

  Mr. MARTINEZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 2566, which was received 
from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2566) to provide an extension of highway, 
     highway safety, motor carrier safety, transit, and other 
     programs funded out of the Highway Trust Fund pending 
     enactment of a law reauthorizing the Transportation Equity 
     Act for the 21st Century.

  Mr. MARTINEZ. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be read a third 
time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid on the table, and any 
statements be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 2566) was read the third time and passed.

                          ____________________
```
