# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 30, 2005

**Speaking:** Price, Tom (P000591) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 167

```
            CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 30, 2005

  Mr. PRICE of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at noon on Monday, May 30, 
2005, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 167, in 
which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.
```
