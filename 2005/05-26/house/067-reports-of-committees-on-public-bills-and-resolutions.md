```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as

     amendment (Rept. 109-99). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the State of the Union.
       Mr. THOMAS: Committee on Ways and Means. House Joint 
     Resolution 27. Resolution withdrawing the approval of the 
     United States from the Agreement establishing the World Trade 
     Organization; adversely; (Rept. 109-100). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the State of the Union.
```
