**Speaking:** Hoekstra, Peter (H000676) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2475

```
 PERMISSION FOR THE PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE TO HAVE 
 UNTIL MIDNIGHT, JUNE 3, 2005 TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON H.R. 2475, 
          INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2006
```
