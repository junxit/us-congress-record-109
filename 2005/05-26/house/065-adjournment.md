# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Beauprez, Bob (B001240) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 167

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. BEAUPREZ. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the previous order of the House 
today, the House stands adjourned until noon on Monday, May 30, 2005, 
unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting 
its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 167, in which case the 
House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  Thereupon, (at 6 o'clock and 23 minutes p.m.), pursuant to the 
previous order of the House of today, the House adjourned until noon on 
Monday, May 30, 2005, unless it sooner has received a message from the 
Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 167, in 
which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.

                          ____________________
```
