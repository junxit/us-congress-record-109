# HONORING THE ALTO LADY JACKETS

```
                     HONORING THE ALTO LADY JACKETS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JEB HENSARLING

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 26, 2005

  Mr. HENSARLING. Mr. Speaker, today I would like to honor the Alto 
Lady Jackets track

[[Page 11628]]

team who won the Texas 2A High School Track Championship on May 14, 
2005. For the third time in three years, the Lady Jackets went down to 
Austin and returned home with the State Championship trophy.
  I would like to recognize teammates Tiffany Hart, Angelitha 
Dickerson, Monique Hackney, Tashekia Mitchell, Kindal Baugh, Taneshia 
Pope, Tiffany Griffin, Margo Kahla, and Coaches Mildred Brown and 
Shanequa Redd. The outstanding team performance of the Alto Lady 
Jackets was highlighted by gold medals in the 800 and 16,000-meter 
relays and a silver medal in the 400-meter relay. Additionally Monique 
Hackney took gold in the long jump, setting a new class 2A record in 
the process.
  As the congressional representative of the families, coaches, and 
supporters of the Alto Lady Jackets, it is my pleasure to recognize 
their tremendous victory and outstanding season. This is an 
accomplishment that these young women will remember for the rest of 
their lives.

                          ____________________
```
