# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                    subcommittee on strategic forces

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on Strategic Forces of the Committee on Armed Services be 
authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on April 4, 2005 at 
2 p.m., in open and closed session to receive testimony on strategic 
forces and nuclear weapons issues in review of the Defense 
Authorization Request for Fiscal Year 2006.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
