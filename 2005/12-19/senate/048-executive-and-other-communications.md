                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together 
with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, and were referred as

     Secretary for Legislative Affairs, Department of State,

     Colombia; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.

     pursuant to the Arms Export Control Act, the certification of

     Italy; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
