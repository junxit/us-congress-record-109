# DEFICIT REDUCTION ACT OF 2005--CONFERENCE REPORT--MOTION TO PROCEED

```
  DEFICIT REDUCTION ACT OF 2005--CONFERENCE REPORT--MOTION TO PROCEED

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent to now proceed to the 
consideration of the conference report to S. 1932, the omnibus deficit 
reduction bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. CONRAD. Objection.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  Mr. FRIST. I now move to proceed to consideration of the conference 
report.
  Mr. CONRAD. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There is a sufficient second.
  The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. McCONNELL. The following Senators were necessarily absent: the 
Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Burr) and the Senator from Arizona 
(Mr. McCain).

the Senator from New Jersey (Mr. Corzine), and the Senator from 
Connecticut (Mr. Dodd) are necessarily absent.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Are there any other Senators in the Chamber 
desiring to vote?
```
