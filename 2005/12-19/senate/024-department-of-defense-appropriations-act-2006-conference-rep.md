                           MOTION TO PROCEED

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to the conference report to accompany H.R. 2863, the Defense 
appropriations bill.
  Mr. REID. I object.

  Mr. REID. I ask for the yeas and nays.

Connecticut (Mr. Dodd) are necessarily absent.

desiring to vote?
