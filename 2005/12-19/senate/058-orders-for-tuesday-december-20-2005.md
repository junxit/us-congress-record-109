# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 20, 2005

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

**Measures:** S. 1932

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 20, 2005

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:45 a.m. on 
Tuesday, December 20. I further ask that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved, the 
Senate then resume consideration

[[Page S14069]]

of the conference report to accompany S. 1932, the omnibus deficit 
reduction bill, with 8 hours of debate equally divided remaining.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
