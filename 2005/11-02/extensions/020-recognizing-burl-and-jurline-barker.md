# RECOGNIZING BURL AND JURLINE BARKER

```
                  RECOGNIZING BURL AND JURLINE BARKER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 2, 2005

  Mr. GRAVES. Mr. Speaker, I proudly pause to recognize two outstanding 
citizens of Missouri. Burl and Jurline Barker of Mount Vernon, MO 
celebrated their 60th wedding anniversary on June 10, 2005.
  Mr. Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in congratulating Mr. and 
Mrs. Barker. Burl and Jurline have set an outstanding example for all 
of us to follow. Their marriage of 60 years truly exemplifies the 
qualities of commitment and dedication.
```
