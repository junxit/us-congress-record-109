# REGARDING H. CON. RES. 34

**Speaking:** Marshall, Jim (M001146) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 34

```
                       REGARDING H. CON. RES. 34

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JIM MARSHALL

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 19, 2005

  Mr. MARSHALL. Mr. Speaker, on April 5, 2005, we short-circuited 
debate and used a suspension motion to honor Yogi Bhajan. It has since 
come to my attention that Mr. Bhajan is a controversial figure. Had I 
known of the controversy surrounding him, I would not have voted in 
favor of this suspension of the House's normal legislative process.
```
