# FINAL ASCERTAINMENT OF ELECTORS

```
                    FINAL ASCERTAINMENT OF ELECTORS

  The VICE PRESIDENT. The Chair lays before the Senate communications 
from the Archivist of the United States transmitting, pursuant to law, 
certified copies of the final ascertainment of the Electors for 
President and Vice President, which are ordered to lie on the table.
```
