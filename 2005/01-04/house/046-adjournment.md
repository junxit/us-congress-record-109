# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House 
Resolution 11, I move that the House do now adjourn in memory of the 
late Honorable Robert T. Matsui.

[[Page 107]]

  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 9 o'clock and 25 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order, the House adjourned until Thursday, 
January 6, 2005, at 11 a.m., in memory of the late Honorable Robert T. 
Matsui of California.

                          ____________________
```
