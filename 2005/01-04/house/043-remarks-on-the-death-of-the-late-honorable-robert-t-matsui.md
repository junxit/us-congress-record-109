# REMARKS ON THE DEATH OF THE LATE HONORABLE ROBERT T. MATSUI

```
      REMARKS ON THE DEATH OF THE LATE HONORABLE ROBERT T. MATSUI

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Under a previous order of the House, the 
gentlewoman from California (Ms. Pelosi) is recognized for 5 minutes.
  Ms. PELOSI. Mr. Speaker, I want to commend the gentlewoman from 
California (Ms. Zoe Lofgren) for taking out this special order on our 
dear friend Bob Matsui and to all of our colleagues who participated in 
it.
  I hope it is a comfort to the Matsui family, to Doris, Brian, Amy and 
Anna, that so many people in this Chamber and I know throughout the 
country share their grief and are praying for them at this sad time.

                          ____________________
```
