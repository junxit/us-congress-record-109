# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO COMMITTEE ON RULES

**Speaking:** Pryce, Deborah (P000555) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.Res. 6

```
               ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO COMMITTEE ON RULES

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican 
Conference, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 6) and ask for its 
immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 6

       Resolved, That the following Members be and are hereby 
     elected to the following standing committee of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on Rules: Mr. Dreier, Chairman; Mr. Lincoln Diaz-
     Balart of Florida; Mr.

[[Page H32]]

     Hastings of Washington; Mr. Sessions; Mr. Putnam; Mrs. 
     Capito; Mr. Cole and Mr. Bishop of Utah.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
