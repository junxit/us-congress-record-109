# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE CLERK

```
                       ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE CLERK

  The CLERK. The Clerk will state that credentials, regular in form, 
have been received showing the election of the Honorable Luis Fortuno 
as Resident Commissioner from the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico for a 
term of 4 years beginning January 3, 2005; the election of the 
Honorable Eleanor Holmes Norton as Delegate from the District of 
Columbia; the election of the Honorable Donna M. Christensen as 
Delegate from the Virgin Islands; the election of the Honorable Eni 
F.H. Faleomavaega as Delegate from American Samoa; and the election of 
the Honorable Madeleine Z. Bordallo as Delegate from Guam.

                          ____________________
```
