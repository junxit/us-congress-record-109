# WITHDRAWALS

```
                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
November 1, 2005 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nominations:

       Roger Francisco Noriega, of Kansas, to be a Member of the 
     Board of Directors of the Inter-American Foundation for a 
     term expiring September 20, 2006, which was sent to the 
     Senate on January 4, 2005.
       Roger Francisco Noriega, of Kansas, to be a Member of the 
     Board of Directors of the Inter-American Foundation for a 
     term expiring September 20, 2006, which was sent to the 
     Senate on February 14, 2005.
```
