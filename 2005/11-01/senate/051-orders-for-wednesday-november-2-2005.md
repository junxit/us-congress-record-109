# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 2, 2005

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

**Measures:** S. 1932

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 2, 2005

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 8:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, November 2. I further ask that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved, and the 
Senate resume consideration of S. 1932, as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
