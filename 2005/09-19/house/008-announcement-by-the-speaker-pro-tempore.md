# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 3169, H.R. 3668, H.R. 3672

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, Speaker pro 
tempore Thornberry signed the following enrolled bills on Friday, 
September 16, 2005:
  H.R. 3169, to provide the Secretary of Education with waiver 
authority for students who are eligible for Pell grants who are 
adversely affected by a natural disaster;
  H.R. 3668, to provide the Secretary of Education with waiver 
authority for students who are eligible for Federal student grant 
assistance who are adversely affected by a major disaster;
  H.R. 3672, to provide assistance to families affected by Hurricane 
Katrina, through the program of block grants to States for temporary 
assistance for needy families.

                          ____________________
```
