# Text of Senate Amendment 587

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
  SA 587. Mr. LEVIN (for himself and Ms. Stabenow) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 3, Reserved; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       Strike section 1701(b) and insert the following:
       (b) Congestion Mitigation and Air Quality Improvement 
     Program Eligibility.--Section 149(b) of title 23, United 
     States Code, is amended by striking paragraph (5) and 
     inserting the following:
       ``(5) if the program or project improves traffic flow, 
     including projects to improve signalization, construct high 
     occupancy vehicle lanes, improve intersections, improve 
     transportation systems management and operations, and 
     implement, operate, and maintain intelligent transportation 
     system strategies and such other projects that are eligible 
     for assistance under this section on the day before the date 
     of enactment of this paragraph.''.
                                 ______
```
