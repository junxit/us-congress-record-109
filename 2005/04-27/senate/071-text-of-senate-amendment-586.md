# Text of Senate Amendment 586

**Speaking:** Kohl, Herb (K000305) [D-WI]

```
  SA 586. Mr. KOHL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 3, Reserved; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       Section 105(b)(1)(B) of title 23, United States Code (as 
     amended by section 1104(a)) is amended by inserting after 
     ``that decennial census,'' in the second place it appears the 
     following: ``an indexed State motor fuel excise tax rate for 
     gasoline that is greater than 150 percent of the Federal 
     motor fuel excise tax rate for gasoline under section 4081 of 
     the Internal Revenue Code of 1986,''.
                                 ______
```
