# TEXT OF AMENDMENTS

**Measures:** S.Res. 149

```
                           TEXT OF AMENDMENTS

  SA 765. Mr. FRIST (for Ms. Snowe) proposed an amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 149, honoring the life and contributions of His 
Eminence, Archbishop Iakovos, former archibishop of the Greek Orthodox 
Archdiocese of North and South America; as follows:

       In the last whereas clause of the preamble, strike ``at the 
     Holy Trinity Cathedral in New York, New York'' and insert 
     ``at the Holy Cross Greek Orthodox School of Theology in 
     Brookline, Massachusetts''.
```
