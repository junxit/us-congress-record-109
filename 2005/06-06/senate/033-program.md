# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Tomorrow, the Senate will resume consideration of the 
nomination of Justice Brown to be a U.S. circuit judge for the DC 
Circuit. At noon, we will proceed to the cloture vote on the 
nomination. It is my expectation that cloture will be invoked and we 
could have an up-or-down vote on confirmation.
  As I noted earlier, following the disposition of the Brown 
nomination, we will move forward immediately with the cloture vote on 
the nomination of William Pryor to be a U.S. circuit judge for the 
Eleventh Circuit. Members should expect votes throughout the week as we 
consider these and other nominations that may proceed over the next 
several days.

                          ____________________
```
