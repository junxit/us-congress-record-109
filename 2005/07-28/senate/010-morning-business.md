# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for 1 
hour, with the first half of the time under the control of Democratic 
leader or his designee, and the second half of the time under the 
control of the majority leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Massachusetts is recognized.
```
