# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3453

```
               MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 2005, the 
Secretary of the Senate, on July 27, 2005, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the House has agreed to the following bill, in which it 
requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 3453. An act to provide an extension of highway, 
     highway safety, motor carrier safety, transit, and other 
     programs funded out of the Highway Trust Fund pending 
     enactment of a law reauthorizing the Transportation Equity 
     Act of the 21st Century.

                          ____________________
```
