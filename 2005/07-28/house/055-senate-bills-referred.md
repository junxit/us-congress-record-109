  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the

       S. 302. An act to make improvements in the Foundation for 
     the National Institutes of Health; to the Committee on Energy 
     and Commerce.
       S. 447. An act to authorize the conveyance of certain 
     Federal land in the State of New Mexico; to the Committee on

       S. 655. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act with

     Disease Control and Prevention; to the Committee on Energy 
     and Commerce.

     Committee on the Judiciary.
       S. 1517. An act to permit Women's Business Centers to re-
     compete for sustainability grants; to the Committee on Small 
     Business.
       S.J. Res. 19. Joint resolution calling upon the President 
     to issue a proclamation recognizing the 30th anniversary of 
     the Helsinki Final Act; to the Committee on International 
     Relations.
