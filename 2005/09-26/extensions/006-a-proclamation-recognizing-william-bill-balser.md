# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING WILLIAM ``BILL'' BALSER

```
           A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING WILLIAM ``BILL'' BALSER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 26, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas William ``Bill'' Balser was elected to the high office of 
Department Commander of Ohio on Sunday, July 10, 2005; and
  Whereas, William ``Bill'' Balser is a 30-year member of the American 
Legion Post 85, where he has held many offices including Post Commander 
and Finance Officer; and
  Whereas, William ``Bill'' Balser has exemplified the meaning of 
successful civic duty through his unselfish role to serve the greater 
good of the Ohio Valley; and
  Whereas, William ``Bill'' Balser recently celebrated his 50th wedding 
anniversary with his wife Trudy and is the loving father of 3 children 
and 2 grandchildren.
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District of Ohio in recognizing William ``Bill'' Balser for his 
longtime

[[Page 21259]]

dedication to the residents and children of Ohio's 18th district.

                          ____________________
```
