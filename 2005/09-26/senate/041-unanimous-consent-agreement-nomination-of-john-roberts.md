# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--NOMINATION OF JOHN ROBERTS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
        UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--NOMINATION OF JOHN ROBERTS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Thursday, 
the time from 10:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. be divided in the following 
manner: 10:30 a.m. to 10:45 a.m., Senator Leahy; 10:45 a.m. to 11 a.m., 
Senator Specter; 11 a.m. to 11:15 a.m., the Democratic leader; and 
11:15 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., the majority leader; provided further that at 
11:30 a.m. on Thursday, the Senate proceed to a vote on the 
confirmation of the nomination of John Roberts to be Chief Justice of 
the United States, with no further intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
