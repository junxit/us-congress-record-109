# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1761

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1761

  Mr. FRIST. I understand there is a bill at the desk due for its 
second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1761) to clarify the liability of Government 
     contractors assisting in rescue, recovery, repair and 
     reconstruction work in the Gulf Coast Region of the United 
     States affected by Hurricane Katrina or other major 
     disasters.

  Mr. FRIST. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill is 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
