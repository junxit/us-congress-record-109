# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 445

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 445) and I ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration.

[[Page H8061]]

  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 445

       Resolved, That the following Members be and are hereby 
     elected to the following standing committees of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on Agriculture: Mrs. Schmidt to rank after Mr. 
     Fortenberry.
       Committee on Government Reform: Mrs. Schmidt to rank after 
     Ms. Foxx.
       Committee on Homeland Security: Mr. King of New York, 
     Chairman; Ms. Ginny Brown-Waite of Florida to rank after Mr. 
     Dent.
       Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure: Mrs. 
     Schmidt to rank after Mr. Boustany.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
