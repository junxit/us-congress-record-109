# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, today, as I mentioned earlier, the Senate 
did complete action on and overwhelmingly pass the Commerce-Justice-
Science appropriations bill. Upon completion of the Commerce-Science-
Justice appropriations bill, we began consideration of the Agriculture 
appropriations bill.
  On Monday, we will resume consideration of this bill and, as always, 
I ask Senators to come forward and let us know if they intend to offer 
amendments. Our next votes will occur Tuesday morning, and we will 
probably begin voting very early Tuesday morning. We will be debating 
amendments on Monday; however, any votes that are ordered on Monday 
will be stacked to occur early on Tuesday morning.

                          ____________________
```
