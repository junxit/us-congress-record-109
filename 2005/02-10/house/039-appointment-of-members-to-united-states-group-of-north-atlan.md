# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES GROUP OF NORTH ATLANTIC ASSEMBLY

```
    APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES GROUP OF NORTH ATLANTIC 
                                ASSEMBLY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 1928a, and the order 
of the House of January 4, 2005, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Members of the House to the United States 
Group of the North Atlantic Assembly:
  Mr. Hefley of Colorado, Chairman;
  Mr. Burton of Indiana, Vice Chairman;
  Mr. Regula of Ohio;
  Mr. Gillmor of Ohio;
  Mr. Ehlers of Michigan;
  Mr. Bilirakis of Florida;
  Mr. Shimkus of Illinois; and
  Mr. Reynolds of New York.

                          ____________________
```
