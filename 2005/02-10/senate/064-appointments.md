# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, and upon the recommendation of the majority leader, pursuant 
to Public Law 96-388, as amended by Public Law 97-84, and Public Law 
106-292, appoints the following Senators to the United States Holocaust 
Memorial Council for the 109th Congress: The Senator from Utah, Mr. 
Hatch; the Senator from Maine, Ms. Collins; the Senator from Minnesota, 
Mr. Coleman.
  The Chair, on behalf of the President pro tempore, pursuant to Public 
Law 106-398, as amended by Public Law 108-7, in accordance with the 
qualifications specified under section 1238(b)(3)(E) of Public Law 106-
398, and upon the recommendation of the majority leader, in 
consultation with the chairmen of the Senate Committee on Armed 
Services and the Senate Committee on Finance, appoints the following 
individual to the United States-China Economic Security Review 
Commission: Mr. Thomas Donnelly of Maryland for a term expiring 
December 31, 2006.
```
