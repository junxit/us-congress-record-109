# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER, MAJORITY LEADER, AND MINORITY LEADER TO ACCEPT RESIGNATIONS AND TO MAKE APPOINTMENTS AUTHORIZED BY LAW OR BY THE HOUSE DURING SECOND SESSION OF THE 109TH CONGRESS

```
AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER, MAJORITY LEADER, AND MINORITY LEADER TO ACCEPT 
RESIGNATIONS AND TO MAKE APPOINTMENTS AUTHORIZED BY LAW OR BY THE HOUSE 
              DURING SECOND SESSION OF THE 109TH CONGRESS

  Mr. POMBO. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
Second Session of the 109th Congress, the Speaker, majority leader, and 
minority leader may accept resignations and make appointments 
authorized by law or by the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
