# APPOINTMENT OF HON. WAYNE T. GILCHREST OR HON. FRANK R. WOLF OR HON. TOM DAVIS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JANUARY 31, 2006

```
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. WAYNE T. GILCHREST OR HON. FRANK R. WOLF OR HON. 
  TOM DAVIS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND 
               JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JANUARY 31, 2006

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, December 18, 2005.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Wayne T. Gilchrest, the 
     Honorable Frank R. Wolf, and the Honorable Tom Davis to act 
     as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through January 31, 2006.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
