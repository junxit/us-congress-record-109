# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 2005-06-21

                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Commerce, Justice, 
Science and Related Agencies, business meeting to mark up H.R. 2862,

ending September 30, 2006, 2 p.m., S-128, Capitol.
  Subcommittee on Agriculture, Rural Development, and Related 
Agencies, business meeting to mark up H.R. 2744, making

September 30, 2006, 3 p.m., SD-192.

nature of the evolving Improvised Explosive Devices (IEDs) threat 
and the Department of Defense's approach to addressing this threat, 
9:30 a.m., SR-222.

the issue of voter verification in the Federal elections process, 10 
a.m., SR-301.
