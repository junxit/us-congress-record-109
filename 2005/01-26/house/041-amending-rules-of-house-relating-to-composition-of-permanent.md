# AMENDING RULES OF HOUSE RELATING TO COMPOSITION OF PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 51

```
  AMENDING RULES OF HOUSE RELATING TO COMPOSITION OF PERMANENT SELECT 
                       COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 51) amending 
the Rules of the House relating to the composition of the Permanent 
Select Committee on Intelligence, and ask unanimous consent for its 
immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 51

       Resolved, In clause 11(a)(1) of rule X--
       (a) strike ``18'' and insert ``21''; and (b) strike ``10'' 
     and insert ``12''.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
