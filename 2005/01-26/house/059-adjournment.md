# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. PAUL. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Accordingly, pursuant to the previous order 
of the House of today, the House stands adjourned until 2 p.m. on 
Friday, January 28, 2005, unless it sooner has received a message from 
the Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 21, 
in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.
  Thereupon (at 2 o'clock and 35 minutes p.m.), pursuant to the 
previous order of the House of today, the House adjourned until 2 p.m. 
on Friday, January 28, 2005, unless it sooner has received a message 
from the Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent 
Resolution 21, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant 
to that concurrent resolution.
```
