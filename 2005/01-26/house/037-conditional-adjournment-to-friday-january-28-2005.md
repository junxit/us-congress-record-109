# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JANUARY 28, 2005

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JANUARY 28, 2005

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Friday, January 28, 
2005, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 21, in 
which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
