# COMPOSITION OF MEMBERS OF PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE DURING 109TH CONGRESS

```
 COMPOSITION OF MEMBERS OF PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE 
                         DURING 109TH CONGRESS

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the requirement of clause 11(a)(1) of rule X, during 
the 109th Congress, the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence be 
composed of not more than 19 Members, of whom not more than 11 be from 
the same party.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
