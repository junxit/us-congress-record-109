# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will be a 
period for the transition of morning business until 10:30 a.m., with 
the first half of the time under the control of the Democratic leader 
or his designee, and the second half of the time under the control of 
the Senator from Kansas, Mr. Brownback, or his designee.
  Who yields time? The Senator from Washington.

                          ____________________
```
