# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. On Monday, the Senate will be in a period of morning 
business. It is our hope we will be able to consider any nominations 
available for Senate action. The nomination of Samuel Bodman was 
reported earlier today by the Energy and Natural Resources Committee. 
We hope that nomination can be cleared for action on Monday. We have no 
requests for a rollcall vote on the Bodman nomination. Therefore, 
rollcall votes are not anticipated during Monday's session.
  Also, earlier today, Chairman Specter and the Judiciary Committee 
reported out the nomination of Alberto Gonzales to be Attorney General. 
We will consider that nomination, as well, next week.

                          ____________________
```
