# EXPRESSING GRATITUDE TO THE MEMBERS OF BRAVO BATTERY, FIRST BATTALION, 109TH FIELD ARTILLERY DIVISION OF THE PENNSYLVANIA ARMY NATIONAL GUARD

**Speaking:** Kanjorski, Paul E. (K000008) [D-PA]

                         HON. PAUL E. KANJORSKI

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 26, 2005

  Mr. KANJORSKI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask you and my esteemed 
colleagues in the House of Representatives to pay tribute to the 126 
members of the Bravo Battery of the First Battalion of the 109th Field 
Artillery, based in Nanticoke, Pennsylvania, who are returning home 
this week after a year in Iraq.
  Our brave soldiers have served their country valiantly and we welcome 
them home with the utmost gratitude for their selflessness. In this 
time of war, it is important that we realize the sacrifices our troops 
endure. Americans have a history of making the necessary sacrifices for 
fighting for the ideals in which we believe. Americans have 
consistently stepped up and taken the lead in ensuring democracy 
through voluntary military service.
