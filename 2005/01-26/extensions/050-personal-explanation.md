                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JIM McDERMOTT

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 26, 2005

  Mr. McDERMOTT. Mr. Speaker, I apologize for missing votes on the 
evening of January 25, 2005. I was on my way back to the United States 
from Chad and Algeria, where I witnessed first-hand the results of the 
genocide occurring in Sudan. Had I been able to, I would have voted:
