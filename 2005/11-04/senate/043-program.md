# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, next week, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the Department of Defense authorization bill. The 
order provides for up to 12 relevant first-degree amendments on each 
side, with a limited amount of time for debate. The bill managers have 
made some progress today on the amendment process, and we will have at 
least one vote on an amendment on the Defense bill on Monday evening, 
at approximately 5:30 p.m. Senators should plan their schedules 
accordingly.
```
