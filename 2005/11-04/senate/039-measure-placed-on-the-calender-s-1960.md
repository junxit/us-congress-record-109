# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDER--S. 1960

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

**Measures:** S. 1960

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDER--S. 1960

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, under rule XIV, I understand there is a 
bill at the desk that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the bill for 
a second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1960) to protect the health and safety of all 
     athletes, to promote the integrity of professional sports by 
     establishing minimum standards for the testing of steroids 
     and other performance-enhancing substances and methods by 
     professional sports leagues, and for other purposes.

  Mr. WARNER. In order to place the bill on the calendar under 
provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection having been heard, the bill will 
be placed on the calendar pursuant to Rule XIV.

                          ____________________
```
