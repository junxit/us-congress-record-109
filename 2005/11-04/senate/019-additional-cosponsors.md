# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 832

  At the request of Mr. Bingaman, the name of the Senator from 
Massachusetts (Mr. Kerry) was added as a cosponsor of S. 832, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide taxpayer protection 
and assistance, and for other purposes.


                              S. RES. 273

  At the request of Mr. Coleman, the names of the Senator from Virginia 
(Mr. Warner) and the Senator from Georgia (Mr. Chambliss) were added as 
cosponsors of S. Res. 273, a resolution expressing the sense of the 
Senate that the United Nations and other international organizations 
shall not be allowed to exercise control over the Internet.


                              S. RES. 299

  At the request of Ms. Landrieu, the names of the Senator from New 
Jersey (Mr. Corzine), the Senator from Arkansas (Mrs. Lincoln) and the 
Senator from Ohio (Mr. DeWine) were added as cosponsors of S. Res. 299, 
a resolution to express support for the goals of National Adoption 
Month by promoting national awareness of adoption, celebrating children 
and families involved in adoption, and encouraging Americans to secure 
safety, permanency, and well-being for all children.
```
