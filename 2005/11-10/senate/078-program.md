# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, on Monday, the Senate will continue its 
consideration of the Defense authorization bill. Under the agreement 
reached this evening, we will have debate on only a few remaining 
amendments. We will

[[Page 25801]]

complete action on those amendments and proceed to passage of the bill 
with a series of votes that will start on Tuesday morning. We will have 
a vote on Monday. Under the order just entered, we will vote on the 
Energy and Water appropriations conference report at 5:30. We will also 
complete action on the State, Justice, Commerce appropriations 
conference report next week.
```
