# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2005 AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2005

```
    ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2005 AND HOUR OF MEETING ON 
                       TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2005

  Mr. BLUNT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 6 p.m. on Monday next, and

10:30 a.m. on Tuesday, November 15, 2005, for morning hour debate.

request of the gentleman from Missouri?
  There was no objection.
```
