# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. On Monday, following morning business, the Senate will 
begin debate on the nomination of Carlos Gutierrez to be Secretary of 
Commerce. We have not received any requests for a rollcall vote on the 
Gutierrez nomination and it is expected we will be able to dispose of 
that nomination on a voice vote. Therefore, no rollcall votes are 
expected during Monday's session. We

[[Page 299]]

will continue to work through the President's nominations as they 
become available. We will have more to say with respect to the voting 
schedule next week.
  I also remind all of my colleagues that we were able to lock in a 
time agreement on the nomination of Condoleezza Rice to be Secretary of 
State. We will debate the Rice nomination throughout the day Tuesday 
and vote on that nomination on Wednesday of next week.

                          ____________________
```
