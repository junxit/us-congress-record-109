# TRIBUTE TO McCROSSAN BOYS RANCH

```
                    TRIBUTE TO McCROSSAN BOYS RANCH

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I rise to congratulate the 
McCrossan Boys Ranch of South Dakota. McCrossan Boys Ranch is a 
nonprofit organization that provides mentoring services to troubled 
boys and helps guide them into becoming responsible and balanced 
adults.
  Some of the valuable services they provide are education and GED 
classes, help with chemical dependency, individual and group therapy, 
psychiatric care and moral development.
  They will be celebrating their 50th anniversary on June 29 and I 
would like to recognize the valuable service they have provided to the 
many boys and families they have helped over the years.●

                          ____________________
```
