for its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.

       A bill (H.R. 2745) to reform the United Nations, and for

  Mr. DOMENICI. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard. The bill will be 
placed on the calendar.
