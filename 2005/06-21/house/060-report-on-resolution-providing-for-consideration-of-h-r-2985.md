```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2985, 
              LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2006

submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 109-144) on the resolution (H.

September 30, 2006, and for other purposes, which was referred to the
```
