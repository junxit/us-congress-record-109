# A PROCLAMATION THANKING MICHAEL SIMPSON FOR HIS SERVICE TO OUR COUNTRY

```
 A PROCLAMATION THANKING MICHAEL SIMPSON FOR HIS SERVICE TO OUR COUNTRY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 26, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, whereas, Michael Simpson has served the United 
States in Operation Iraqi Freedom as a member of the United States 
Army; and
  Whereas, Michael Simpson is to be commended for the honor and bravery 
that he displayed while serving our Nation in this time of war; and
  Whereas, Michael Simpson has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence, and outstanding service;
  Therefore, I join with the family, friends and the entire 18th 
Congressional District of Ohio in thanking Michael Simpson of the 
United States Army for his service to our country. Your service has 
made us proud.

                          ____________________
```
