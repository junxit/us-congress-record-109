# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5, HELP EFFICIENT, ACCESSIBLE, LOW-COST, TIMELY HEALTHCARE (HEALTH) ACT OF 2005

**Measures:** H.R. 5, H.Res. 385

```
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5, HELP 
EFFICIENT, ACCESSIBLE, LOW-COST, TIMELY HEALTHCARE (HEALTH) ACT OF 2005

  Mr. PUTNAM, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 109-185) on the resolution (H. Res. 385) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5) to improve patient access to 
health care services and provide improved medical care by reducing the 
excessive burden the liability system places on the health care 
delivery system, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
```
