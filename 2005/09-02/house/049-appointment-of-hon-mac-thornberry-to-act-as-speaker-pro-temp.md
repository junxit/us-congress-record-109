# APPOINTMENT OF HON. MAC THORNBERRY TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 6, 2005

```
  APPOINTMENT OF HON. MAC THORNBERRY TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
  SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 6, 2005

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                Washington, DC, September 2, 2005.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Mac Thornberry to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through September 6, 2005.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
