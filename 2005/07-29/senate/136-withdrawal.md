# WITHDRAWAL

```

       ALBERT HENRY KONETZNI, JR., OF NEW YORK, TO BE A MEMBER OF 
     THE NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION FOR THE TERM OF FIVE YEARS 
     EXPIRING JUNE 30, 2009, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON 
     JANUARY 4, 2005.
```
