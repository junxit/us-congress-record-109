# AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
         AUTHORITY TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader and majority whip and 
both Senators from Virginia be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills 
or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
