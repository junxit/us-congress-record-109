# ORDER FOR NOMINATIONS TO REMAIN IN STATUS QUO

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
             ORDER FOR NOMINATIONS TO REMAIN IN STATUS QUO

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that all 
nominations received by the Senate during the 109th Congress remain in 
status quo during the August adjournment of the Senate under the 
provisions of rule XXXI, paragraph 6, of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, with the exception of the nomination of John Robert Bolton, 
PN326 and PN327.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
