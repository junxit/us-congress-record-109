# CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF AIRMEN JOHN LAYLAGIAN

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
             CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF AIRMEN JOHN LAYLAGIAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 28, 2005

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, today I am happy to 
congratulate Janelle and Leon Laylagian of Hopkinton, New Hampshire, on 
the birth of their son. Airmen John Laylagian was born on June 4, 2005 
at 5:58 p.m., weighing 6 pounds, 3 ounces and measuring 20 inches long. 
Airmen has been born into a loving home, where he will be raised by 
parents who are devoted to his well-being and bright future. His birth 
is a blessing. I appreciate the friendship I share with both Mr. and 
Mrs. Laylagian.

                          ____________________
```
