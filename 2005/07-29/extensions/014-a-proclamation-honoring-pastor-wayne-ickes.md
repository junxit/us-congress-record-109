# A PROCLAMATION HONORING PASTOR WAYNE ICKES

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
               A PROCLAMATION HONORING PASTOR WAYNE ICKES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 28, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, whereas, Wayne Ickes is celebrating 50 years of 
service and dedication to the ministry; and
  Whereas, Wayne Ickes is the well loved executive pastor of East 
Richland Evangelical Friends Church in East Richland, OH; and
  Whereas, Wayne Ickes is happily married to Barbara with sons David 
and Doug.
  Therefore, I join with the residents of East Richland, and the entire 
18th Congressional District of Ohio in congratulating Wayne Ickes as he 
celebrates his 50th Anniversary of service to the Lord.

                          ____________________
```
