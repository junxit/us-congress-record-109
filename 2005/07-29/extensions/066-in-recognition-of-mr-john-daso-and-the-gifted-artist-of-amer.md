# IN RECOGNITION OF MR. JOHN DASO AND THE GIFTED ARTIST OF AMERICA CENTER, INC.

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
   IN RECOGNITION OF MR. JOHN DASO AND THE GIFTED ARTIST OF AMERICA 
                              CENTER, INC.

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 28, 2005

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am proud today to 
recognize the valuable work of Mr. John Daso, a talented artist who 
resides in Lexington, South Carolina.
  As one of the most beautiful states in the nation, South Carolina's 
landscape is certainly not easy to accurately illustrate for most 
artists. However, Mr. Daso's work demonstrates his unique ability to 
reflect the beauty of our state. I am proud that we have such a 
remarkable artist living in the Second District of South Carolina.
  As the founder of the Gifted Artist of America Center, Inc., Mr. Daso 
is also generously serving our community. This valuable center helps 
educate, mentor and inspire young artists who are or were unable to 
afford a formal education. Specifically, the center offers business 
development, marketing skills, financial awareness, associative skill 
training and graphic design. By supporting our youth and encouraging 
public interest in the Arts, the Gifted Artist of America Center is 
inspiring individuals to make a difference in their communities by 
using their artistic skills.
  I am grateful for Mr. Daso's artistic talent and leadership in our 
community.
```
