# CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF LUCILLE "LUCY" BRIGGS TEMPLE

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
        CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF LUCILLE ``LUCY'' BRIGGS TEMPLE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 28, 2005

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, today, I am happy to 
congratulate Rachel and Adam Temple of Columbia, South Carolina, on the 
birth of their beautiful baby girl. Lucille Briggs Temple was born on 
April 19, 2005 at 6:30 p.m., weighing 7 pounds, 9 ounces and measuring 
21 inches long.
  Lucy has been born into a loving home, where she will be raised by 
parents who are devoted to her well-being and bright future. Her birth 
is a blessing. Adam is doing a wonderful job as Deputy Communications 
Director for U.S. Senator Jim DeMint, and I appreciate the friendship 
of both Mr. and Mrs. Temple.

                          ____________________
```
