# A PROCLAMATION HONORING EVA J. DENNEY ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
      A PROCLAMATION HONORING EVA J. DENNEY ON HER 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 28, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, whereas, Eva J. Denney was born on June 19, 
1905; and
  Whereas, Eva J. Denney is celebrating her 100th birthday today; and
  Whereas, Eva J. Denney, is a long-time active participant in the 
social and civic life of her community; and
  Whereas, Eva J. Denney has exemplified a love for her family and 
friends and must be commended for her life-long dedication to helping 
others.
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District of Ohio in wishing Eva J. Denney a very happy 100th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
