# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Bartlett, Roscoe G. (B000208) [R-MD]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 225

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. BARTLETT of Maryland. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 225, 109th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Price of Georgia). Pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 225, 109th Congress, the House stands adjourned 
until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, September 6, 2005.
  Thereupon (at 2 o'clock and 45 minutes p.m.), pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 225, the House adjourned until Tuesday, September 
6, 2005, at 2 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
