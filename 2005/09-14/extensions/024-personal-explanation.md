                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ELTON GALLEGLY

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 14, 2005

  Mr. GALLEGLY. Mr. Speaker, on Tuesday, September 13, 2005, I was 
unable to vote on the motion to suspend the rules and agree to S. Con. 
Res. 26, Honoring and memorializing the passengers and crew of United 
Airlines Flight 93 (rollcall 465); on the motion to suspend the rules 
and pass H.R. 3649, the Sportfishing and Recreational Boating Safety 
Amendments Act (rollcall 466); and on the motion to suspend the rules 
and pass S. 276, the Wind Cave National Boundary Revision Act (rollcall 
467). Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on all three 
measures.
