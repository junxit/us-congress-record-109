# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 05, H.J.Res. 1058, H.J.Res. 16

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       169. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the General 
     Assembly of the State of Colorado, relative to House Joint 
     Resolution 05-1058 expressing support for the ``25 By 25'' 
     initiative and promoting the increased producation of 
     renewableenergy by the agricultural community; to the 
     Committee on Agriculture.
       170. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Oregon, relative to House Joint Memorial 15 urging the 
     Congress of the United States to provide returning veterans 
     with the care and respect they deserve by ensuring that they 
     are allowed up to 21 days of ``decompression'' time following 
     combat duty to transition back into civilian life and 
     workplace; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
       171. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Oregon, relative to House Joint Resolution 16 urging the 
     Congress of the United States to establish capital funds for 
     grants to veterans starting new businesses; to the Committee 
     on Veterans' Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
