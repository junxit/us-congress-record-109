# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       10. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature of 
     Rockland County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 15 of 
     2005 petitioning the United States Congress to issue a 
     Congressional Gold Medal to Welles Remy Crowther for his 
     bravery and sacrifice in saving dozens of people from certain 
     death on September 11, 2001, resulting in his own death that 
     day; which was referred to the Committee on Financial 
     Services.
```
