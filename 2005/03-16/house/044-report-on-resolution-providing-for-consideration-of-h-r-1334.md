# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1334, PROTECTION OF INCAPACITATED PERSONS ACT OF 2005

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1334, 
            PROTECTION OF INCAPACITATED PERSONS ACT OF 2005

  Mr. GINGREY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 109-20) on the resolution (H. Res. 162) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1334) to amend title 28, United States 
Code, to provide for the removal to Federal court of certain State 
court cases involving the rights of incapacitated persons, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```
