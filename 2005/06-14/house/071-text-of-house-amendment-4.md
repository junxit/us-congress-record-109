# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** DeFazio, Peter A. (D000191) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 2863

```
H.R. 2863

                        Offered By: Mr. DeFazio

       Amendment No. 4: Page 117, after line 5, insert the 
     following title:

                 TITLE X--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 10___. None of the funds made available in this Act 
     may be used to initiate military operations that--
       (1) are against any country other than a country against 
     which military operations were being carried out as of the 
     date of the enactment of this Act; and
       (2) are not authorized by Congress pursuant to Article I, 
     Section 8 of the Constitution of the United States.
```
