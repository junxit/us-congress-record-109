# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2863, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2006

**Measures:** H.Res. 315, H.R. 2863

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2863, 
             DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2006

  Mr. COLE of Oklahoma, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 109-127) on the resolution (H. Res. 315) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2863) making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2006, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
