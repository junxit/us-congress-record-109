# Text of House Amendment 23

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2862

```
H.R. 2862

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 23: At the end of the bill (preceding the 
     short title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 8__. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to facilitate the issuance of affirmances by single 
     members of the Board of Immigration Appeals (BIA) without an 
     accompanying opinion.
```
