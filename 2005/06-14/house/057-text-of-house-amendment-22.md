# Text of House Amendment 22

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2862

```
H.R. 2862

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 22: At the end of the bill (preceding the 
     short title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 8__. None of the funds made available in this Act 
     under the heading ``Office of Justice Programs--justice 
     Assistance'' may be used to fund State or local anti-drug 
     task forces that do not collect, and make publicly available, 
     data as to the racial distribution of convictions made as a 
     result of their operation.
```
