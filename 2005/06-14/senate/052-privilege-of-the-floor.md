  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Sreela

Saavedra, an intern on the staff of Senator Bingaman, be granted the

  Mr. HARKIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Stephen 
Butschi of my staff be granted the privilege of the floor for the

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
