# SIGNING AUTHORIZATION

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                         SIGNING AUTHORIZATION

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader, majority whip, and 
senior Senator from Virginia be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills 
or joint resolutions.
```
