# REMOVAL OF CONFEREE AND APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREE ON S. 1932, DEFICIT REDUCTION ACT OF 2005

```
  REMOVAL OF CONFEREE AND APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREE ON S. 1932, DEFICIT 
                         REDUCTION ACT OF 2005

clause 11 of rule I, the Chair removes the gentleman from Michigan (Mr.
```
