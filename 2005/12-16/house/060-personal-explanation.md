# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Cole, Tom (C001053) [R-OK]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. COLE of Oklahoma (during the Special Order of Mr. King of Iowa). 
Mr. Speaker, late tonight I discovered there is a problem with my 
voting card. After returning home, I became aware that my vote was not 
recorded on roll call votes 661, 659, and 651.
  On each of these votes, I am sure I voted ``yes.'' Indeed, I checked 
my vote on the card receptacle. It clearly showed that I had voted.
  I will work with the Parliamentarian to resolve this issue with my 
voting card at the earliest possible time.

                          ____________________
```
