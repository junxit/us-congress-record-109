# TRIBUTE TO HUBERT WILKENS

```
                       TRIBUTE TO HUBERT WILKENS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. IKE SKELTON

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 16, 2005

  Mr. SKELTON. Mr. Speaker, let me take this means to congratulate and 
pay tribute to Hubert Wilkens of Concordia, MO, who recently received 
the Safe Driver Award from OATS Public Transportation. He has 
distinguished himself and the Concordia community with dedicated 
service.
  Hubert Wilkens was given the Safe Driver Award for 4 years of driving 
the OATS bus without an accident. Mr. Wilkens picks up senior citizens 
from the city of Concordia and the outlying rural area. He transports 
them to the grocery store, doctor, chiropractor, dentists appointments 
and to the Concordia Senior Center.
  Mr. Speaker, Hubert Wilkens has been dedicated to making the City of 
Concordia a better place to live. I am certain that my colleagues will 
join me in wishing him and his wife Eunice all the best.

                          ____________________
```
