# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will be a 
period for the transaction of morning business for up to 60 minutes, 
with the first half of the time under the control of the majority 
leader or his designee and the last half under the control of the 
Democratic leader or his designee.
```
