**Speaking:** Andrews, Robert E. (A000210) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT E. ANDREWS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 21, 2005

  Mr. ANDREWS. Mr. Speaker, I regret that I missed nine votes on April 
20th, 2005. The votes were on amendments to the Energy Policy Act of 
2005 (H.R. 6). Had I been present I would have voted ``yea'' on
```
