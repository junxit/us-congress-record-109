# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, on Monday, the Senate will resume the 
motion to proceed to the highway bill. There will be no rollcall votes 
on Monday but Senators are encouraged to come to the floor to make 
their statements with respect to the bill.
  As a reminder, a few minutes ago, cloture was filed on the motion to 
proceed to the bill and that cloture vote will occur at 11:45 on 
Tuesday of next week. That will be the first rollcall vote of next 
week. Next week is the last week prior to the Senate recess and 
Senators should expect a busy week with votes as we make progress on 
the highway legislation.
```
