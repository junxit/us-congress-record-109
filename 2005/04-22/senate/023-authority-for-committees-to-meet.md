# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


           subcommittee on emerging threats and capabilities

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on Emerging Threats and Capabilities be authorized to meet 
during the session of the Senate on April 22, 2005, at 9:30 a.m., in 
open and closed session to receive testimony on U.S. Special Operations 
Command in review of the defense authorization request for fiscal year 
2006.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
