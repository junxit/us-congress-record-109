# TRIBUTE TO JOAN PIERMARINI

**Speaking:** Roberts, Pat (R000307) [R-KS]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JOAN PIERMARINI

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I would like to take a moment to 
recognize Joan Piermarini, who is retiring after 20 years of service to 
the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence. Joan has served the 
committee under seven chairmen--a testament to her dedication and 
loyalty. I thank Joan for her many tireless efforts and the significant 
contributions she has made to the committee. We congratulate her on a 
job well done and wish her many years of happiness with her family, 
especially her grandson Luke.

                          ____________________
```
