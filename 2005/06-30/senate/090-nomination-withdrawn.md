# NOMINATION WITHDRAWN

```
                          NOMINATION WITHDRAWN

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
29, 2005 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       RONALD E. MEISBURG, OF VIRGINIA, TO BE A MEMBER OF THE 
     NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD FOR THE TERM OF FIVE YEARS 
     EXPIRING AUGUST 27, 2008, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON 
     JANUARY 24, 2005.
```
