# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, June 30, 2005, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 714. An act to amend section 227 of the Communications 
     Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 227) relating to the prohibition on 
     junk fax transmissions.
       S. 1282. An act to amend the Communications Satellite Act 
     of 1962 to strike the privatization criteria for INTELSAT 
     separated entities, remove certain restrictions on separated 
     and successor entities to INTELSAT, and for other purposes.
```
