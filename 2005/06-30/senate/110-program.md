# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow, we will be in a period of morning 
business throughout the day. There will be, as I mentioned earlier, no 
rollcall votes tomorrow. Senators who wish to speak prior to the recess 
should come to the floor tomorrow morning. When we return from the 
recess, we will begin consideration of the Homeland Security 
appropriations bill. I will have more to say on the post-July Fourth 
recess schedule tomorrow, but I would inform all Senators that the next 
rollcall vote will occur on Monday, July 11.
```
