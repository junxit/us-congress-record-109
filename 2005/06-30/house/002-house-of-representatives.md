# House of Representatives

```
[[Page H5481]]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

                        House of Representatives

  The House met at 10 a.m.
  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  May today's prayer rise before You, O Lord, and give You praise.
  May the acts of Congress be accomplished this day according to Your 
holy will, and generate a new spirit among the Members that will give 
them satisfaction in their work and bring justice and security to the 
Nation.
  May those who are in most need of Your mercy take refuge in You 
today.
  May this day bring joy and laughter to children, relief of suffering 
to the sick and loving consolation to the elderly.
  At the end of the day, may all rest from their labors and may You 
grant the world peace.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
