# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, JULY 5, 2005

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 198

```
            CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, JULY 5, 2005

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns on this legislative day, it adjourn to meet at 6 p.m. on the 
third constitutional day thereafter, unless it sooner has received a 
message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House 
Concurrent Resolution 198, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?

[[Page H5592]]

  There was no objection.
```
