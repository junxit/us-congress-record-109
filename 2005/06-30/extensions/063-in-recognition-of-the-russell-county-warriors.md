# IN RECOGNITION OF THE RUSSELL COUNTY WARRIORS

```
             IN RECOGNITION OF THE RUSSELL COUNTY WARRIORS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 30, 2005

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to 
the Russell County Warriors high school baseball team which recently 
won the Alabama High School Athletic Association Class 5A State 
Championship in Montgomery, Alabama.
  The Warriors are a highly talented group of athletes that are 
currently ranked Number One in the USA Today and Baseball America 
standings. The team has had a nearly perfect season at 38-1, and has 
provided their school, their families, and our community so much to be 
proud of.
  Let us all raise a cheer for the Warriors and congratulate them on 
their victory.
  Thank you, Mr. Speaker, for the House's attention on this day for 
these talented young Americans.

                          ____________________
```
