# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE


                      Amendment No. 2347 Withdrawn

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask for the regular order with respect to 
amendment No. 2347 and I ask that the amendment be withdrawn. I further 
ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to votes in relation to 
the pending amendments in the order offered; provided further that 
there be 2 minutes equally divided for debate prior to the votes in 
relation to any of the pending amendments, in addition to any second 
degrees offered.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection? Without objection, it 
is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, has the majority leader completed his 
statement?
```
