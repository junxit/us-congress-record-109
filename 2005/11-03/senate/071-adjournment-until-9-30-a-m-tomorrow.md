# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. FRIST. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment as a 
further mark of respect to the late Henry K. Giugni.

Friday, November 4, 2005, at 9:30 a.m.
```
