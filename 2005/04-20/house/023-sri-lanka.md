# SRI LANKA

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

```
                               SRI LANKA

  (Mr. DAVIS of Illinois asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, during the recess, I was 
privileged to visit the country of Sri Lanka where there exists tension 
and sometimes open warfare between the government and the Tamilians.
  Fortunately, partially as a result of the tsunami, there is a cease 
fire. I trust that the cease fire will continue, that a peaceful accord 
will be reached. But in the meantime, I would urge that we do 
everything within our power to make sure that relief resources are 
equally and fairly deployed throughout all areas of the country that 
were, in fact, affected. There is a tremendous resolve to try and 
arrive at peace. I hope, Mr. Speaker, that we will help.

                          ____________________
```
