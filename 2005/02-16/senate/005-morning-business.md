# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 90 
minutes with Senators permitted to speak therein, with the first 30 
minutes under the control of the majority leader or his designee, and 
the next 30 minutes under the control of the Democratic leader or his 
designee.
```
