# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will resume 
consideration of that Labor-HHS appropriations bill. Just a few moments 
ago I filed cloture on that bill. Senators who are serious about 
offering germane amendments should work with the bill managers to 
schedule floor consideration just as quickly as possible. We are on 
track, the track that we set out last week, to finish the bill this 
week. I encourage Senators to vote for cloture in order to speed 
passage of this, the very last, the final appropriations bill for this 
year.
```
