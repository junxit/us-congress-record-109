# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 975

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 975

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 975) to provide incentives to increase research 
     by private sector entities to develop medical countermeasures 
     to prevent, detect, identify, contain, and treat illnesses, 
     including those associated with a biological, chemical, 
     nuclear, or radiological weapons attack or an infectious 
     disease outbreak, and for other purposes.

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I now ask for a second reading and, in 
order to place the bill on the calendar under rule XIV, I object to my 
own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be read a second time on the 
next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
