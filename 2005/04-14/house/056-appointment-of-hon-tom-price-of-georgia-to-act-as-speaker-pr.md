# APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM PRICE OF GEORGIA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH APRIL 19, 2005

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM PRICE OF GEORGIA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE 
  TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH APRIL 19, 2005



 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  April 14, 2005--On Page H2087 the following appeared: 
APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM PRICE TO ACT AS SPEAKER.
  
  The online version should be corrected to read: APPOINTMENT OF 
HON. TOM PRICE OF GEORGIA TO ACT AS SPEAKER.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, April 14, 2005.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Tom Price to act as Speaker 
     pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions 
     through April 19, 2005.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
