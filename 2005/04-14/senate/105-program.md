# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Madam President, tomorrow, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the Iraq-Afghanistan supplemental. Although no 
rollcall votes will occur tomorrow, we hope to make additional progress 
on the bill. We expect to lock in some of the pending amendments for 
votes on Monday, and therefore Senators can expect a series of

[[Page S3714]]

votes to occur Monday evening. It is my intention to complete action on 
this bill early next week, and Members should not wait until the last 
minute to offer their amendments.
```
