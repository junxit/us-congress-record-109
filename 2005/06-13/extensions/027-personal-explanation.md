                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. STEPHANIE TUBBS JONES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 13, 2005

  Mrs. JONES of Ohio. On House rollcall vote No. 239, H.J. Res. 27 a 
bill ``Withdrawing approval of the United States from the Agreement 
establishing the World Trade Organization.'' I voted ``yea'' on this 
vote, and intended to vote ``no.'' I am asking that the record show 
that I am supportive of he United States membership in the World Trade 
Organization.
