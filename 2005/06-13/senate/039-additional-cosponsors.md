                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 37

  At the request of Mrs. Hutchison, the names of the Senator from 
Mississippi (Mr. Lott), the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. Santorum) 
and the Senator from Nebraska (Mr. Hagel) were added as cosponsors of 
S. 37, a bill to extend the special postage stamp for breast cancer 
research for 2 years.

Louisiana (Mr. Vitter), the Senator from Washington (Ms. Cantwell), the 
Senator from Indiana (Mr. Bayh), the Senator from Nevada (Mr. Ensign),

(Ms. Landrieu) were added as cosponsors of S. 37, supra.

Bennett) was added as a cosponsor of S. 52, a bill to direct the 
Secretary of the Interior to convey a parcel of real property to Beaver 
County, Utah.

  At the request of Mr. Hatch, the name of the Senator from Utah (Mr. 
Bennett) was added as a cosponsor of S. 54, a bill to amend the 
National Trails System Act to require the Secretary of the Interior to

  At the request of Mr. Inouye, the names of the Senator from Wisconsin 
(Mr. Feingold) and the Senator from Maine (Ms. Snowe) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 58, a bill to amend title 10, United States Code, to 
permit former members of the Armed Forces who have a service-connected 
disability rated as total to travel on military aircraft in the same 
manner and to the same extent as retired members of the Armed Forces
