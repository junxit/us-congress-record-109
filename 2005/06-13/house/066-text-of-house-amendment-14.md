# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 2862

```
H.R. 2862

                        Offered By: Mr. Sanders

       Amendment No. 14: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act to 
     the Federal Communications Commission may be used to impose a 
     forfeiture penalty under section 503 of the Communications 
     Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 503) for a violation of section 1464 
     of title 18, United States Code, or 47 CFR 73.3999, with 
     respect to any indecent utterance on any medium other than a 
     radio or television broadcasting station.
```
