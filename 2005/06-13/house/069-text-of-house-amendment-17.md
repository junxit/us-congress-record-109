# Text of House Amendment 17

**Speaking:** Stearns, Cliff (S000822) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2862

```
H.R. 2862

                        Offered By: Mr. Stearns

       Amendment No. 17: At the end of the bill, add the following 
     title:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for the design, construction, or rental of any new 
     headquarters for the United Nations in New York City or any 
     other location in the United States.
```
