# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Chocola, Chris (C001052) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2862

```
H.R. 2862

                        Offered By: Mr. Chocola

       Amendment No. 1: Page 108, after line 7, insert the 
     following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

     to employ any individual under the title ``artist in
```
