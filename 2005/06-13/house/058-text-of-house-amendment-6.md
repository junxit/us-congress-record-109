# Text of House Amendment 6

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2862

```
H.R. 2862

                        Offered By: Mrs. Maloney

       Amendment No. 6: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

               TITLE VIII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 801. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to enforce any provision of law that prohibits or 
     restricts funding for the United Nations Population Fund 
     (UNFPA).
```
