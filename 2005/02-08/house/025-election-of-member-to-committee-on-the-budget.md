# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON THE BUDGET

**Speaking:** Boustany, Charles W., Jr. (B001255) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 68, H.R. 68

```
             ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON THE BUDGET

  Mr. BOUSTANY. Madam Speaker, I offer a resolution (H.R. 68), and I 
ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the resolution.
  The Clerk read as follows:

                               H. Res. 68

       Resolved, That the following Member be and is hereby 
     elected to the following standing committee of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on the Budget: Mr. Simpson to rank after Mr. Ryan 
     of Wisconsin.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Louisiana?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
