# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Majority Leader, 
pursuant to Public Law 105-83, announces the appointment of the 
following individuals to serve as members of the National Council of 
the Arts: the Senator from Ohio, Mr. DeWine, and the Senator from Utah, 
Mr. Bennett.
  The Chair, pursuant to Executive Order No. 12131, reappoints the 
following Member to the President's Export Council: the Honorable Mike 
Enzi of Wyoming.
  The Chair, on behalf of the President of the Senate, pursuant to 
Public Law 85-874, as amended, appoints the Senator from Mississippi, 
Mr. Cochran, to the Board of Trustees of the John F. Kennedy Center for 
the Performing Arts, vice the Senator from Alaska, Mr. Stevens.
```
