# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING BEV RILEY

```
                  A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING BEV RILEY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 8, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Bev Riley has served continuously as the office manager for 
the Chillicothe Mayor's Office for twenty years; and
  Whereas, Bev Riley provided her services as office manager through 
the administration of three different Chillicothe mayors; and
  Whereas, Bev Riley's service to the residents of Chillicothe is 
greatly appreciated by all who have worked with her. She should be 
commended for the help that she provided to the people of Chillicothe 
and the surrounding area.
  Therefore, I join with the entire 18th Congressional District of Ohio 
in celebrating Bev Riley's twenty years of service to the Village of 
Chillicothe.

                          ____________________
```
