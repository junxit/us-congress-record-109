# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 3761, H.R. 3768

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 3761. An act to provide special rules for disaster 
     relief employment under the Workforce Investment Act of 1998 
     for individuals displaced by Hurricane Katrina.

  The message also announced that the Senate agreed to the amendment of 
the House to the amendment of the Senate to the text of the bill (H.R. 
3768) ``An act to provide emergency tax relief for persons affected by 
Hurricane Katrina.''.
  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 98-183, as 
amended by Public Law 103-419, the Chair, on behalf of the President 
pro tempore and upon the recommendation of the Democratic Leader, 
appoints Arlan D. Melendez, of Nevada, to the United States Commission 
on Civil Rights.

                          ____________________
```
