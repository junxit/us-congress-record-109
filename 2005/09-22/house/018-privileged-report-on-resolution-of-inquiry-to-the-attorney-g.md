# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

**Measures:** H.Res. 420

```
   PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

  Mr. COBLE, from the Committee on the Judiciary, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 109-230) on the resolution (H. Res. 420) 
directing the Attorney General to transmit to the House of 
Representatives not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption 
of this resolution documents in the possession of the Attorney General 
relating to the disclosure of the identity and employment of Ms. 
Valerie Plame, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```
