# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** S. 1745, S. 1748

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills were read the second time, and placed on the 
calendar:

       S. 1745. A bill to expand the availability of resources 
     under the Community Services Block Grant Act for individuals 
     affected by Hurricane Katrina.
       S. 1748. A bill to establish a congressional commission to 
     examine the Federal, State, and local response to the 
     devastation wrought by Hurricane Katrina in the Gulf Region 
     of the United States especially in the States of Louisiana, 
     Mississippi, Alabama, and other areas impacted in the 
     aftermath and make immediate corrective measures to improve 
     such responses in the future.

                          ____________________
```
