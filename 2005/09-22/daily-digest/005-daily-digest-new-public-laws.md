Law 109-69)
  S. 264, to amend the Reclamation Wastewater and Groundwater Study and 
Facilities Act to authorize certain projects in the State of Hawaii. Signed 
on September 21, 2005. (Public Law 109-70)
  S. 276, to revise the boundary of the Wind Cave National Park in the State 
of South Dakota. Signed on September 21, 2005. (Public Law 109-71)
