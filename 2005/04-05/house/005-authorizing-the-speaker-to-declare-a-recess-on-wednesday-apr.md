# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 6, 2005, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY, VIKTOR YUSHCHENKO, PRESIDENT OF UKRAINE

```
  AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 6, 
  2005, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY, 
                VIKTOR YUSHCHENKO, PRESIDENT OF UKRAINE

  Mr. SHAW. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order at any time on Wednesday, April 6, 2005, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency, Viktor Yushchenko, President 
of Ukraine.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
