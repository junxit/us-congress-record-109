# AUTHORITY TO APPOINT A COMMITTEE

```
                    AUTHORITY TO APPOINT A COMMITTEE

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the President of the 
Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the Senate 
to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort His Excellency Viktor Yushchenko, President 
of Ukraine, into the House Chamber for the joint meeting tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
