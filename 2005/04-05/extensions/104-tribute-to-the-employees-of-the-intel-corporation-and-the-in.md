# TRIBUTE TO THE EMPLOYEES OF THE INTEL CORPORATION AND THE INTEL FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Wu, David (W000793) [D-OR]

```
    TRIBUTE TO THE EMPLOYEES OF THE INTEL CORPORATION AND THE INTEL 
                               FOUNDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. DAVID WU

                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 5, 2005

  Mr. WU. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commend the employees of the 
Intel Corporation and the Intel Foundation for their efforts to provide 
tsunami relief to the Indian Ocean region.
  As we all know, on December 26, 2004, a 9.0 earthquake erupted off 
the coast of Indonesia. Following the earthquake, a major tsunami swept 
across the region, destroying lives, homes and businesses in Indonesia, 
Sri Lanka, India, the Maldives, and Thailand.
  Along with governments, citizens, businesses, and other private 
organizations around the world, Intel's employees and the Intel 
Foundation mobilized and delivered critical supplies and funds that 
helped save lives and begin the reconstruction process.
  I stand here today to applaud Intel and its employees, many of whom 
live and work in my district in Oregon. Without their good work, many 
more lives may have been lost because of the Indian Ocean Tsunami.

                          ____________________
```
