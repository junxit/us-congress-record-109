# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Allen, George (A000121) [R-VA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will resume the 
consideration of the nomination of Priscilla Owen to be a United States 
circuit court judge for the Fifth Circuit. We have had another day of 
substantive debate on the Owen nomination. As announced earlier today, 
there will be no rollcall votes tomorrow. We will have a busy day of 
debate, surely, and Senators are encouraged to come to the Senate 
during the session. As a reminder, the majority leader has announced we 
will have a vote next Monday at 5:30 p.m. That vote is likely to be a 
vote on a motion to instruct and to request Members' attendance. More 
will be said regarding Monday's schedule at the close of business 
tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```
