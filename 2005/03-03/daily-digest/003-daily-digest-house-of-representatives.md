Continuity in Representation Act of 2005: The House passed H.R. 841, to 
require States to hold special elections to fill vacancies in the House 
of Representatives not later than 45 days after the vacancy is 
announced by the Speaker of the House of Representatives in 
extraordinary circumstances, by voice vote. The voice vote was later 
vacated and the measure was passed by a recorded vote of 329 ayes to 68 
noes, Roll No. 52.
  Pages H948-70
  Rejected the Conyers motion to recommit the bill to the Committee on 
House Administration with instructions to report the bill back to the 
House forthwith with an amendment, by a recorded vote of 196 ayes to 
223 noes, Roll No. 51.
Pages H967-69
  The amendment in the nature of a substitute recommended by the 
Committee on House Administration, now printed in the bill, was 
considered as an original bill for the purpose of amendment.

Agreed To:
  Manager's amendment that increases the time frame for expedited 
special elections to 49 days.
Pages H970-71
Rejected:
  Millender-McDonald amendment (made in order under H. Res. 125 and in

after the Speaker of the House announces that such vacancies exist (by 
a recorded vote of 192 ayes to 229 noes, Roll No. 49); and 
                                                 Pages H961-64, H965-66
