# VACATING MOTION TO RECONSIDER ON H.R. 841, CONTINUITY IN REPRESENTATION ACT OF 2005

**Speaking:** Frank, Barney (F000339) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 841

```
VACATING MOTION TO RECONSIDER ON H.R. 841, CONTINUITY IN REPRESENTATION 
                              ACT OF 2005

  Mr. FRANK of Massachusetts. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent in 
the interest of civility that the vote on H.R. 841 be vacated and a 
rollcall be allowed.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Massachusetts?
  Without objection, the proceedings whereby the motion to reconsider 
was laid on the table and by which the bill was passed are vacated.
  There was no objection.
```
