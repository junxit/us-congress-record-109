# APPOINTMENT OF ADDITIONAL MEMBERS TO JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE

```
     APPOINTMENT OF ADDITIONAL MEMBERS TO JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to 15 USC 1024(a), and the order of 
the House of January 4, 2005, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Members of the House to the Joint Economic 
Committee, in addition to Mr. Saxton of New Jersey, appointed January 
20, 2005:
  Mr. Ryan of Wisconsin;
  Mr. English of Pennsylvania;
  Mr. Paul of Texas;
  Mr. Brady of Texas;
  Mr. McCotter of Michigan;
  Mrs. Maloney of New York;
  Mr. Hinchey of New York;
  Ms. Loretta Sanchez of California; and
  Mr. Cummings of Maryland.
```
