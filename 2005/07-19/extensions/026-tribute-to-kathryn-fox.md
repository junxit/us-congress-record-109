# TRIBUTE TO KATHRYN FOX

```
                         TRIBUTE TO KATHRYN FOX

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CHRIS VAN HOLLEN

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 19, 2005

  Mr. VAN HOLLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate to one of 
my constituents, Kathryn Fox, who recently celebrated her 102nd 
birthday.
  Mrs. Fox was born on July 16, 1903 in the small town of Middleport, 
Ohio. After attending the University of Ohio, she married Richard K. 
Fox, Sr. The two lived in St. Louis for over three decades, as Mr. Fox 
enjoyed a long career teaching high school mathematics.
  Among Mrs. Fox's favorite pastimes is travel. She and her husband 
made several trips to Europe and traveled extensively in the American 
West. Mrs. Fox has continued to visit the Tucson home of her son, 
Ambassador Richard Fox, Jr., in recent years.
  In her active retirement, Mrs. Fox enjoys needlepoint and attends 
church each Sunday.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Mrs. Fox on her birthday and wish her the 
best of health and much continued happiness in the future.

                          ____________________
```
