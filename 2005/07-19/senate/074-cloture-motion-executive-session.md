# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Given that objection, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on Executive 
     Calendar No. 101, the nomination of Thomas Dorr, of Iowa, to 
     be Under Secretary of Agriculture for Rural Development.
         Bill Frist, Saxby Chambliss, Ted Stevens, Wayne Allard, 
           Larry Craig, Pat Roberts, Chuck Hagel, Richard Burr, 
           James Inhofe, Thad Cochran, Chuck Grassley, John Thune, 
           Johnny Isakson, Bob Bennett, Mike Crapo, Mitch 
           McConnell, and Richard Lugar.

  Mr. FRIST. I ask unanimous consent that the live quorum be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
