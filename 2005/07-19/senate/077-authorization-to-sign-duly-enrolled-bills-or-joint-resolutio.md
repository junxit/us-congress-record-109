# AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
     AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader and majority whip be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
