```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on Tuesday, July 19, 2005, I was unable to 
cast my floor vote on rollcall Nos. 383 and 384. The votes I missed 
included ordering the previous question on the amendment and on 
agreeing to H. Res. 365, providing for consideration of H.R. 2601 to 
authorize appropriations for the Department of State for the fiscal 
years 2006 and 2007, and for other purposes.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``no'' on both rollcall votes 
383 and 384.
```
