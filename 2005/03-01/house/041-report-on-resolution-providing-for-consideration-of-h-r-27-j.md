# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 27, JOB TRAINING IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 2005

**Measures:** H.R. 27, H.Res. 126

```
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 27, JOB 
                    TRAINING IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 2005

  Mr. COLE of Oklahoma, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 109-11) on the resolution (H. Res. 126) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 27) to enhance the 
workforce investment system of the Nation by strengthening one-stop 
career centers, providing for more effective governance arrangements, 
promoting access to a more comprehensive array of employment, training, 
and related services, establishing a targeted approach to serving 
youth, and improving performance accountability, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
