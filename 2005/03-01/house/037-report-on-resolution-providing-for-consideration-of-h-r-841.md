# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 841, CONTINUITY IN REPRESENTATION ACT OF 2005

```
     REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 841, 
                CONTINUITY IN REPRESENTATION ACT OF 2005

  Mr. COLE of Oklahoma, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 109-10) on the resolution (H. Res. 125) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 841) to require States to 
hold special elections to fill vacancies in the House of 
Representatives not later than 45 days after the vacancy is announced 
by the Speaker of the House of Representatives in extraordinary 
circumstances, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
