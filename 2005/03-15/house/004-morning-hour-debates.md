# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
January 4, 2005, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists 
submitted by the majority and minority leaders for morning hour 
debates. The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with 
each party limited to 25 minutes, and each Member other than the 
majority and the minority leaders and the minority whip limited to 5 
minutes each, but in no event shall debate continue beyond 9:50 a.m.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Missouri (Mr. Skelton) for 5 
minutes.

                          ____________________
```
