# Text of House Amendment 9

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1268

```
H.R. 1268

                         Offered By: Mr. Weiner

       Amendment No. 9: Insert at the end of the bill, before the 
     short title, the following:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 7001. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for assistance to the Palestinian Authority (or to 
     any successor entity) or for programs, projects, and 
     activities in the West Bank or Gaza.
```
