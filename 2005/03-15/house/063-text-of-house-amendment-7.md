# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Upton, Fred (U000031) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 1268

```
H.R. 1268

                         Offered By: Mr. Upton

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 701. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for embassy security, construction, and maintenance.
```
