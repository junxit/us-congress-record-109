# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Barrow, John (B001252) [D-GA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN BARROW

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 6, 2005

  Mr. BARROW. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall No. 3, I was unable to cast my 
vote on January 3, 2005. The pager provided to me by House 
Administration, to notify me of votes, was defective. Consequently, I 
did not receive the announcement that the vote was taking place in time 
to cast my vote on the House Floor. Had I been present, I would have 
voted ``nay.''
```
