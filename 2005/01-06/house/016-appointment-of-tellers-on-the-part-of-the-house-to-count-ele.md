# APPOINTMENT OF TELLERS ON THE PART OF THE HOUSE TO COUNT ELECTORAL VOTES

**Measures:** S. 1

```
  APPOINTMENT OF TELLERS ON THE PART OF THE HOUSE TO COUNT ELECTORAL 
                                 VOTES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Pursuant to Senate Concurrent 
Resolution 1, 109th Congress, and the order of the House of January 4, 
2005, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment as tellers on the
```
