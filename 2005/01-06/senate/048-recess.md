# RECESS

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 2

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess, awaiting word from the House of Representatives that 
it is ready to receive the Senate.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 3:12 p.m, recessed until 
5:05 p.m., to reassembled in the House of Representatives to continue 
the joint session.
  Whereupon, at 5:20 p.m. the Senate adjourned, according to the 
provisions of H. Con. Res. 2, until Thursday, January 20, 2005, at 3 
p.m.

                          ____________________
```
