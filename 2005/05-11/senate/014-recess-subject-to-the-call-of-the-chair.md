# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair declares the Senate in recess 
subject to the call of the Chair.
  Thereupon, at 12:02 p.m., the Senate recessed until 1:02 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. DeMint).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.
```
