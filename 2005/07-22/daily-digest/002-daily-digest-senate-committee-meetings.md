# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the nominations of Karen P. Hughes, of Texas, to be Under 
Secretary of State for Public Diplomacy, with the rank of Ambassador, 
who was introduced by Senators Hutchison and Cornyn, Josette Sheeran 
Shiner, of Virginia, to be Under Secretary of State for Economic, 
Business, and Agricultural Affairs, Kristen Silverberg, of Texas, to be 
Assistant Secretary of State for International Organization Affairs, 
and Jendayi Elizabeth Frazer, of Virginia, to be Assistant Secretary of 
State for African Affairs, after the nominees testified and answered 
questions in their own behalf.
```
