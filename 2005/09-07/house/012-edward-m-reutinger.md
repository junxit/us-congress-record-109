# EDWARD M. REUTINGER

```
                          EDWARD M. REUTINGER

  (Mr. NEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the life of a great 
American, Edward M. Reutinger. Edward Reutinger passed away on August 
25, 2005, from a stroke at the age of 58. He was a Green Township 
trustee in Ross County, Ohio, for 12 years and planned on renewing his 
public service to Ohio this November by running for trustee again.
  Edward Reutinger was a devoted family man and a cherished husband, 
father, grandfather, and uncle. He and his wife were set to celebrate 
their 37th wedding anniversary this year.
  Mr. Speaker, Edward Reutinger represents some of the best that Ohio 
and America have to offer. I want to remember him for his upstanding 
citizenship and for the love he had for his township and his country. 
May God rest his soul.
```
