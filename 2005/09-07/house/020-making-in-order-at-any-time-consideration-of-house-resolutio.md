# MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 423, HONORING AND RECOGNIZING THE DISTINGUISHED SERVICE, CAREER, AND ACHIEVEMENTS OF CHIEF JUSTICE WILLIAM HUBBS REHNQUIST UPON HIS DEATH

```
  MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 423, 
    HONORING AND RECOGNIZING THE DISTINGUISHED SERVICE, CAREER, AND 
  ACHIEVEMENTS OF CHIEF JUSTICE WILLIAM HUBBS REHNQUIST UPON HIS DEATH

  Mr. SENSENBRENNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it shall 
be in order at any time to consider in the House the resolution (H. 
Res. 423); the resolution shall be considered as read; and the previous 
question shall be considered as ordered on the resolution to its 
adoption without intervening motion or demand for division of the 
question except: (1) 1 hour of debate equally divided and controlled by 
the chairman and ranking minority member of the Committee on the 
Judiciary; and (2) one motion to recommit.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.
```
