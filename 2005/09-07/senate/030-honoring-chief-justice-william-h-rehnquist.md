# HONORING CHIEF JUSTICE WILLIAM H. REHNQUIST

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

```
              HONORING CHIEF JUSTICE WILLIAM H. REHNQUIST

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I wish to join with all my colleagues and 
with America in expressing our condolences to the Rehnquist family and, 
obviously, our great appreciation for his extraordinary service to this 
Nation. I hope at a later date to put in a more extensive statement. He 
was a man whose commitment to the law was exceptional, but his 
commitment to the country was even higher. We are very fortunate to 
have had him as our Chief Justice and as a Justice on the Supreme Court 
for so long.

                          ____________________
```
