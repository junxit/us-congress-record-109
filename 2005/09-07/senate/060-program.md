# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will turn to the 
Commerce, Justice, Science appropriations bill. The managers will be 
ready to go through amendments and expedite consideration of this 
important bill. I encourage our colleagues who may have amendments to 
work with the managers tomorrow morning in order to schedule 
consideration.
  In addition to that measure, we anticipate a further supplemental 
appropriations bill to arrive from the House.

[[Page 19643]]

We will need to act quickly on that emergency funding to continue our 
efforts in our Southern Gulf States. Therefore, there will be votes 
during Thursday's session of the Senate, and I will announce Friday's 
voting schedule tomorrow once we can gauge our progress.

                          ____________________
```
