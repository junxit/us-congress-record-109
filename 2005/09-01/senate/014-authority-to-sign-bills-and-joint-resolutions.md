# AUTHORITY TO SIGN BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
             AUTHORITY TO SIGN BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the majority 
leader, the senior Senator from Mississippi, and the senior Senator 
from New Mexico be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint 
resolutions during this adjournment of the Senate.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
