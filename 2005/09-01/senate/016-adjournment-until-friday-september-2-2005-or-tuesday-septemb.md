# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 2, 2005, OR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 6, 2005

```
 ADJOURNMENT UNTIL FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 2, 2005, OR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 6, 
                                  2005

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the previous order and under the provisions of S. 
Con. Res. 51.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 10:30 p.m. adjourned until 4 
p.m. on Friday, September 2, 2005, or until 12 noon on Tuesday, 
September 6, 2005.
```
