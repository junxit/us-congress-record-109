# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 418, REAL ID ACT OF 2005

**Measures:** H.Res. 75, H.R. 418

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 418, 
                          REAL ID ACT OF 2005

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 109-4) on the resolution (H. Res. 75) providing for 
further consideration of the bill (H.R. 418) to establish and rapidly 
implement regulations for State driver's license and identification 
document security standards, to prevent terrorists from abusing the 
asylum laws of the United States, to unify terrorism-related grounds 
for inadmissibility and removal, and to ensure expeditious construction 
of the San Diego border fence, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
