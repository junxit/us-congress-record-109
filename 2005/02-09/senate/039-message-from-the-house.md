# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 315, H.R. 548

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:59 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 315. An act to designate the United States courthouse 
     at 300 North Hogan Street, Jacksonville, Florida, as the 
     ``John Milton Bryan Simpson United States Courthouse''.
       H.R. 548. An act to designate the Federal building and 
     United States courthouse located at 200 West 2nd Street in 
     Dayton, Ohio, as the ``Tony Hall Federal Building and United 
     States Courthouse''.

                          ____________________
```
