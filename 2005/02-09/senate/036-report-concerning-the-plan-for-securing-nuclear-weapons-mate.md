# REPORT CONCERNING THE PLAN FOR SECURING NUCLEAR WEAPONS, MATERIAL, AND EXPERTISE OF THE STATES OF THE FORMER SOVIET UNION--PM 4

```
REPORT CONCERNING THE PLAN FOR SECURING NUCLEAR WEAPONS, MATERIAL, AND 
        EXPERTISE OF THE STATES OF THE FORMER SOVIET UNION--PM 4

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Armed Services:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Consistent with section 1205 of the National Defense Authorization 
Act for Fiscal Year 2003 (Public Law 107-314), I am providing a report 
prepared by my Administration on implementation during 2003 of the plan 
for securing nuclear weapons, material, and expertise of the states of 
the former Soviet Union.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, February 8, 2005.

                          ____________________
```
