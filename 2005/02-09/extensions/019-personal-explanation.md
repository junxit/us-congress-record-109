```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 9, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, because US Airways canceled my flight into 
Washington, DC on February 8, 2005, I was unable to be present for 
rollcall vote no. 20, on agreeing to H. Res. 46; for rollcall vote no.

States Courthouse Designation Act; and, for rollcall vote no. 22, on

Courthouse Designation Act. Had I been present I would have voted 
``yes'' on rollcall vote no. 20, ``yes'' on rollcall vote no. 21, and 
``yes'' on rollcall vote no. 22.
```
