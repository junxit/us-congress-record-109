# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** LoBiondo, Frank A. (L000554) [R-NJ]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. FRANK A. LoBIONDO

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 9, 2005

  Mr. LoBIONDO. Mr. Speaker, I was not present in the House Chamber for 
votes on February 8, 2005, as I was attending the memorial services of 
a constituent, Corporal Harry Swain, IV of Millville, New Jersey, who 
died as the result of hostile action in Iraq. If I were present for 
votes on this day, I would have voted ``yea'' on Rollcall #20, ``yea'' 
on Rollcall #21, and ``yea'' on Rollcall #22.
```
