# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Larson, John B. (L000557) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.Res. 228, H.Res. 253

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, I would like to submit this 
statement for the Record and regret that I could not be present today, 
Tuesday, May 3, 2005 to vote on rollcall vote Nos. 151 and 152 due to a 
family medical emergency.
  Had I been present, I would have voted: ``No'' on rollcall vote No. 
151 on the motion to table H. Res. 253, the Conyers Resolution Raising 
a Question of the Privileges of the House; ``yea'' on rollcall vote No. 
152 on H. Res. 228--Observing the 30th anniversary of the fall of the 
Republic of Vietnam to the Communist forces of North Vietnam.
```
