# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

```
      PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

  Mr. HOEKSTRA, from the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, 
submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 109-228) on the resolution (H. 
Res. 418) requesting the President to transmit to the House of 
Representatives not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption 
of this resolution documents in the possession of the President 
relating to the disclosure of the identity and employment of Ms. 
Valerie Plame, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```
