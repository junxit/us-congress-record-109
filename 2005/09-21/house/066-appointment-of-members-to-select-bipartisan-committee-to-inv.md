# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO SELECT BIPARTISAN COMMITTEE TO INVESTIGATE PREPARATION FOR AND RESPONSE TO HURRICANE KATRINA

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO SELECT BIPARTISAN COMMITTEE TO INVESTIGATE 
           PREPARATION FOR AND RESPONSE TO HURRICANE KATRINA

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Schwarz of Michigan). Pursuant to 
section 2(a) of House Resolution 437, 109th Congress, and the order of 
the House of January 4, 2005, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Members of the House to the Select 
Bipartisan Committee to Investigate the Preparation for and Response to 
Hurricane Katrina:
  Mr. Tom Davis of Virginia, Chairman;
  Mr. Sensenbrenner of Wisconsin;
  Mr. Rogers of Kentucky;
  Mr. Shays of Connecticut;
  Mr. Bonilla of Texas;
  Mr. Buyer of Indiana;
  Mrs. Myrick of North Carolina;
  Mr. Thornberry of Texas;
  Ms. Granger of Texas;
  Mr. Pickering of Mississippi;
  Mr. Shuster of Pennsylvania.

                          ____________________
```
