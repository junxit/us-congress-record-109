until 5 o'clock.

p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. 
Coburn).

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I say to Senator Bennett that I know he is 
managing a bill, and I see no one else is here on that bill at this
