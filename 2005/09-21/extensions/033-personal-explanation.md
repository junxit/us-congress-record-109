# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Menendez, Robert (M000639) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.Res. 441, H.R. 3761

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT MENENDEZ

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 21, 2005

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I was absent from votes in the House on 
Tuesday, September 20th, due to a previous and unavoidable commitment. 
Therefore, I was unable to vote on H.R. 3761, the Flexibility for 
Displaced Workers Act (rollcall No. 476), and H. Res. 441, a resolution 
to congratulate the National Aeronautics and Space Administration and 
the Discovery crew (rollcall No. 477). Had I been present, I would have 
voted ``aye'' on both of these measures considered by the House.
```
