# Text of Senate Amendment 602

**Speaking:** Nelson, Ben (N000180) [D-NE]

**Measures:** H.R. 3

```
  SA 602. Mr. NELSON of Nebraska submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill H.R. 3, to authorize funds for Federal-aid 
highways, highway safety programs, and transit programs, and for other 
purposes.



 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S4654, May 9, 2005, under ``Texts of Amendments'', the 
following sentence appeared: SA 602. Mr. NELSON of Nebraska 
submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill, 
H.R. 3, Reserved;
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: SA 602. Mr. 
NELSON of Nebraska submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill, H.R. 3, to authorize funds for Federal-aid 
highways, highway safety programs, and transit programs, and for 
other purposes.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. NEBRASKA CUSTOM HARVESTERS LENGTH EXEMPTION.

       Section 31112(c) of title 49, United States Code, is 
     amended--
       (1) in paragraph (3), by striking ``and'' at the end;
       (2) in paragraph (4), by striking the period at the end and 
     inserting ``; and''; and
       (3) by adding at the end the following:
       ``(5) Nebraska may allow the operation of commercial motor 
     vehicle combinations that are not longer than 81 feet, 6 
     inches, and are only used for harvesting wheat, soybeans, and 
     milo on a contract basis for agricultural producers during 
     the harvest months for such crops (as defined by the State of 
     Nebraska).''.
                                 ______
```
