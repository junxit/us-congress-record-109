# Text of House Amendment 28

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3010

```
H.R. 3010

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 28: At the end of the bill (before the short
```
