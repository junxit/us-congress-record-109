# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--ADJOURNMENT RESOLUTION

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
          UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--ADJOURNMENT RESOLUTION

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the adjournment of the Senate, when the Senate receives 
from the House the adjournment resolution, the text of which is at the 
desk, the concurrent resolution be considered agreed to and the motion 
to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
