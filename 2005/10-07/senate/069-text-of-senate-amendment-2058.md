# Text of Senate Amendment 2058

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
  SA 2058. Mr. STEVENS (for Mr. Inhofe) proposed an amendment to the 
bill H.R. 3765, to extend through March 31, 2006, the authority of the 
Secretary of the Army to accept and expend funds contributed by non-
Federal public entities and to expedite the processing of permits; as 
follows:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``To extend through March 
     31, 2006, the authority of the Secretary of the Army to 
     accept and expend funds contributed by non-Federal public 
     entities and to expedite the processing of permits.''.
                                 ______
```
