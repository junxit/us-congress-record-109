# WITHDRAWALS

```
                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
October 7, 2005 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nominations:

       Philip D. Morrison, of the District of Columbia, to be an 
     Assistant Secretary of the Treasury, which was sent to the 
     Senate on May 26, 2005.
       Timothy Elliott Flanigan, of Virginia, to be Deputy 
     Attorney General, which was sent to the Senate on June 20, 
     2005.
```
