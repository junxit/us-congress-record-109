# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Baker, Richard H. (B000072) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 263

```
                  CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. BAKER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns on this legislative day, it adjourn to meet at noon on the 
third constitutional day thereafter, unless it sooner has received a 
message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House 
Concurrent Resolution 263, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Louisiana?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
