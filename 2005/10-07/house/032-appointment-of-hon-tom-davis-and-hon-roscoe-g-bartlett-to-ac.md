# APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM DAVIS AND HON. ROSCOE G. BARTLETT TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE, TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH OCTOBER 17, 2005

```
  APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM DAVIS AND HON. ROSCOE G. BARTLETT TO ACT AS 
   SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE, TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS 
                        THROUGH OCTOBER 17, 2005

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               The Speaker's Room,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                     Washington, DC, Oct. 7, 2005.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Tom Davis and the Honorable 
     Roscoe G. Bartlett to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign 
     enrolled bills and joint resolutions through October 17, 
     2005.
                                                   Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
