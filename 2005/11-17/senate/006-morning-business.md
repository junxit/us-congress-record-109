# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will be a 
period for the transaction of morning business for up to 30 minutes, 
with the first half of the time under the control of the minority 
leader or his designee and the second half under the control of the 
majority leader or his designee.
```
