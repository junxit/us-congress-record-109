# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 705, H.R. 4326

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 4326. An act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to 
     enter into a contract for the nuclear refueling and complex 
     overhaul of the U.S.S. Carl Vinson (CVN-70).

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 705. An act to establish the Interagency Council on 
     Meeting the Housing and Service Needs of Seniors, and for 
     other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
