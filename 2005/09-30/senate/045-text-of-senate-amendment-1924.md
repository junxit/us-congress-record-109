# Text of Senate Amendment 1924

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Edward M. (K000105) [D-MA]

```
  SA 1924. Mr. KENNEDY submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 2863, making appropriations for the Department 
of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2006, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __. Of the amount appropriated by title IV under the 
     heading ``Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation, 
     Army'', up to $1,000,000 may be used for Integrated Starter/
     Alternator for Up-Armored High Mobility Multi-Wheeled 
     Vehicles.
                                 ______
```
