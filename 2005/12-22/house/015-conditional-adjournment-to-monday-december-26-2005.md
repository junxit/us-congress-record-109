# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, DECEMBER 26, 2005

**Speaking:** Sensenbrenner, F. James, Jr. (S000244) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 326

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, DECEMBER 26, 2005

  Mr. SENSENBRENNER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today pursuant to this order, it adjourn to meet at 11 
a.m. on Monday, December 26, 2005, unless it sooner has received a 
message from the Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent 
Resolution 326, in which case the House shall stand adjourned sine die 
pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Wisconsin?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
