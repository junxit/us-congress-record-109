         BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates, he had approved and signed bills and joint resolutions of the 
following titles:

           September 8, 2005:
       H.R. 3673. An Act making further emergency supplemental 
     appropriations to meet immediate needs arising from the 
     consequences of Hurricane Katrina, for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2005, and for other purposes.
           September 9, 2005:
       H.R. 3650. An Act to allow United States courts to conduct 
     business during emergency conditions, and for other purposes.
