# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
16, 2005, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:
       THOMAS V. SKINNER, OF ILLINOIS, TO BE AN ASSISTANT 
     ADMINISTRATOR OF THE ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY, WHICH 
     WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 24, 2005.
```
