# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Kilpatrick, Carolyn C. (K000180) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 155, H.Res. 199

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. KILPATRICK of Michigan. Mr. Speaker, personal business prevents 
me from being present for legislative business scheduled for today, 
Monday, June 27, 2005. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' 
on H. Res. 199, a resolution expressing the sense of the House 
regarding the massacre at Srebrenica in July 1995 (Rollcall No. 322); 
and ``yea'' on H. Con. Res. 155, a resolution urging the Government of 
the Republic of Albania to ensure that the parliamentary elections to 
be held on July 3, 2005, are conducted in accordance with international 
standards for free and fair elections (Rollcall No. 323).
```
