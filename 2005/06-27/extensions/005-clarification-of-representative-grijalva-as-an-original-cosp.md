# CLARIFICATION OF REPRESENTATIVE GRIJALVA AS AN ORIGINAL COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3051

```
 CLARIFICATION OF REPRESENTATIVE GRIJALVA AS AN ORIGINAL COSPONSOR OF 
                               H.R. 3051

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM KOLBE

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 27, 2005

  Mr. KOLBE. Mr. Speaker, on Thursday, June 23, I introduced H.R. 3051, 
the Pima County Land Adjustment Act. At introduction I inadvertently 
did not indicate Representative Raul Grijalva as an original cosponsor. 
Representative Grijalva made significant contributions during the 
authoring of this legislation and played an integral role throughout 
the process. Although the House rules do not permit Representative 
Grijalva's name to be shown as an original cosponsor of H.R. 3051, I 
wish to clarify that he rightly deserves this recognition. I would like 
to express my sincerest apologies to Representative Grijalva and his 
staff for this inadvertent oversight.

                          ____________________
```
