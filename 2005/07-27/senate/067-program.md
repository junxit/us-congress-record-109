# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. Tomorrow, the Senate will continue its consideration 
of the gun liability bill. Under an agreement reached this evening, we 
will debate and vote on the Kohl amendment on trigger locks. That vote 
will occur before lunch tomorrow. As a remainder, first-degree 
amendments must be filed by 1 p.m. tomorrow afternoon. We will have a 
cloture vote on the pending legislation, and we will announce the exact 
timing of that vote tomorrow.
```
