# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--HIGHWAY EXTENSION

```
             UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--HIGHWAY EXTENSION

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the recess or adjournment of the Senate, when the 
Senate receives from the House a short-term highway extension, the text 
of which is at the desk, the bill be considered read the third time and 
passed and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, all without 
any intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
