# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276-276g, as amended, appoints the following 
Senators as members of the Senate Delegation to the Canada-U.S. 
Interparliamentary Group during the First Session of the 109th 
Congress: the Honorable Charles E. Grassley of Iowa, the Honorable 
Trent Lott of Mississippi, the Honorable George V. Voinovich of Ohio, 
the Honorable Saxby Chambliss of Georgia, and the Honorable Richard 
Burr of North Carolina.
  The Chair, on behalf of the President pro tempore, pursuant to Public 
Law 99-498, re-appoints the following individual to a 3-year term, 
commencing on October 1, 2005, as a member of the Advisory Committee on 
Student Financial Assistance: Claude O. Pressnell, Jr., of Tennessee.
```
