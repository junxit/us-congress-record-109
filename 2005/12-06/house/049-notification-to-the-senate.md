# NOTIFICATION TO THE SENATE

```
                       NOTIFICATION TO THE SENATE

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged 
resolution (H. Res. 581) and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 581

       Resolved, That the Senate be informed that Karen L. Haas, a 
     citizen of the State of Maryland, has been elected Clerk of 
     the House of Representatives of the One Hundred Ninth 
     Congress.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
