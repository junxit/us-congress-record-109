**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

missed rollcall votes Nos. 10-13. Had I been present, I would have 
voted ``nay'' on rollcall Nos. 10, 11, and 12, and ``yea'' on rollcall 
No. 13.
