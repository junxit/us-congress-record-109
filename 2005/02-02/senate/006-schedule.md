# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, this morning at 9:30, we will resume 
consideration of the nomination of Alberto Gonzales to be Attorney 
General of the United States. Yesterday, we were able to lock in an 
agreement on the nomination. We will debate the nomination throughout 
the course of the morning and the afternoon.
  As we all know, at 9 p.m. tonight, the President will deliver the 
State of the Union Address. Therefore, we will recess at approximately 
4:30 this afternoon to accommodate arrangements for that address. I do 
want to remind our colleagues that we will assemble in the Chamber at 
8:30 so we can proceed at 8:40 sharp to the Hall of the House of 
Representatives.
  Tomorrow, we will continue debate on the Gonzales nomination as the 
order provides, with the vote occurring Thursday afternoon or evening.

                          ____________________
```
