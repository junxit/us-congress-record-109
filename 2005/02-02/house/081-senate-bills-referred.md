# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 167

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  A bill of the Senate of the following title was taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 167. An act to provide for the protection of 
     intellectual property rights, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary; in addition to the Committee on 
     House Administration for a period to be subsequently 
     determined by the Speaker, in each case for consideration of 
     such provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of the 
     committee concerned.
```
