# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. CANTOR. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican Conference, I 
offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 65) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution as follows:

                               H. Res. 65

       Resolved, That the following Members be and are hereby 
     elected to the following standing committee of the House of 
     Representatives:
       Committee on Standards of Official Conduct: Mr. Hastings of 
     Washington, Chairman; Mrs. Biggert; Mr. Smith of Texas; Ms. 
     Hart and Mr. Cole.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
