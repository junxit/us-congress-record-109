# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2005

**Speaking:** DeLay, Tom (D000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 39

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2005

  Mr. DeLAY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Friday, February 4, 
2005, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 39, in 
which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.




                          ____________________
```
