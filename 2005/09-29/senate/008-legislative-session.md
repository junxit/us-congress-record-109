# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN], Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI]

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  Mr. FRIST. I ask that the Senate resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. FRIST. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. FEINGOLD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Thune). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
