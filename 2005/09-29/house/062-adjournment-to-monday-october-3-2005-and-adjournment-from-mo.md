# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2005, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM MONDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2005 TO THURSDAY, OCTOBER 6, 2005

```
 ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2005, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM MONDAY, 
              OCTOBER 3, 2005 TO THURSDAY, OCTOBER 6, 2005

  Mr. COLE of Oklahoma (during the special order of Mr. King of Iowa).
```
