# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Foley). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, 
proceedings will resume on questions previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  adoption of H. Res. 470, by the yeas and nays;
  motion to suspend the rules on H. Res. 388, by the yeas and nays;
  motion to suspend the rules on H. Con. Res. 245.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
