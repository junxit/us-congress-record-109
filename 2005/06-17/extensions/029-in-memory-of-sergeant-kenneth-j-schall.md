# IN MEMORY OF SERGEANT KENNETH J. SCHALL

```
                IN MEMORY OF SERGEANT KENNETH J. SCHALL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. TRENT FRANKS

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 17, 2005

  Mr. FRANKS of Arizona. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to a 
fallen hero--Sergeant Kenneth J. Schall of El Mirage, Arizona.
  On May 22, 2005, we tragically lost this soldier of freedom when his 
military vehicle was involved in an accident. Kenneth was nobly serving 
his country in Yusafiyah, Iraq as a vital and supportive member of 
Operation Iraqi Freedom.
  At the young age of 22, Kenneth has marked history as a true American 
hero. He sacrificed his life to protect the citizens of this great 
Nation and the cause of human freedom. Today, we deeply honor his 
memory with our profound gratitude and extend our most loving 
condolences to his family and parents, Mr. and Mrs. John and Terri 
Schall.

                          ____________________
```
