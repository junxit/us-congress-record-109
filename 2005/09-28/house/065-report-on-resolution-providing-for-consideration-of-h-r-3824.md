# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3824, THREATENED AND ENDANGERED SPECIES RECOVERY ACT OF 2005

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3824, 
         THREATENED AND ENDANGERED SPECIES RECOVERY ACT OF 2005

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 109-240) on the resolution (H. Res. 470) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3824) to amend and 
reauthorize the Endangered Species Act of 1973 to provide greater 
results conserving and recovering listed species, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
