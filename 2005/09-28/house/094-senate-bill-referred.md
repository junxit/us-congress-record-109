# SENATE BILL REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 37

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

  A bill of the Senate of the following title was taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 37. An act to extend the special postage stamp for 
     breast cancer research for 2 years; to the Committee on 
     Government Reform; in addition to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce and to the Committee on Armed Services for a period 
     to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case 
     for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
```
