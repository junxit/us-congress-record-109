# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business. There will be no rollcall votes during tomorrow's 
session. The next vote will occur on Tuesday of next week. It is my 
hope we will be able to begin consideration of the highway bill early 
next week, and I will have more to say on next week's schedule 
tomorrow.
  Before we close, I do want to congratulate Chairman Cochran as well 
as the ranking member for their efforts on the emergency supplemental 
today. With the passage vote of 99 to zero, that bill shortly will go 
to conference committee for a final product. I thank the two managers 
for their time and patience on the floor during the consideration of 
the bill.

                          ____________________
```
