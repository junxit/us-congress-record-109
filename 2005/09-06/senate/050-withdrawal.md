# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
September 6, 2005 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:

       John G. Roberts, Jr., of Maryland, to be an Associate 
     Justice of the Supreme Court of the United States, which was 
     sent to the Senate on July 29, 2005.
```
