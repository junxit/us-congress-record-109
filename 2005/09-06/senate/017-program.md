# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will pay its respects 
to the late Chief Justice William Rehnquist. Senators will be able and 
are encouraged to make statements tomorrow morning relating to the 
passing of Justice Rehnquist.
  We will be voting at noon tomorrow on a resolution which expresses 
the sense of the Senate. The Senate will recess during the funeral 
ceremonies as a further mark of respect. As I mentioned earlier, we 
will begin consideration of the Commerce, Justice and Science 
appropriations bill on Thursday this week.

                          ____________________
```
