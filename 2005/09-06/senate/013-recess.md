# RECESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT], Harkin, Tom (H000206) [D-IA]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess prior to the hour that was chosen, just a couple of 
minutes early.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:28 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m. and reconvened when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Voinovich).
  Mr. LEAHY. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. HARKIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
