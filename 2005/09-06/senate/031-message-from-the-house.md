                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  A message from the House of Representatives, delivered by one of its

bill:

       H.R. 3645. An act making emergency supplemental 
     appropriations to meet immediate needs arising from the 
     consequences of Hurricane Katrina, for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2005, and for other purposes.

adjournment of the Senate, by the Majority Leader (Mr. Frist).
