# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Energy and Natural Resources be authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, September 6 at 2:30 p.m. The purpose 
of this hearing is to receive testimony regarding oil and gasoline 
pricing.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
