# COMMUNICATION FROM ASSOCIATE JUSTICE OF SUPREME COURT

```
         COMMUNICATION FROM ASSOCIATE JUSTICE OF SUPREME COURT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Biggert) laid before the House the 
following communication from Antonin Scalia, Associate Justice of the 
Supreme Court of the United States:

                           Supreme Court of the United States,

                                Washington, DC, September 6, 2005.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to notify the House of 
     Representatives, through you, that the Chief Justice of the 
     United States died in Arlington, Virginia on Saturday, 
     September 3, 2005.
           Very truly yours,
                                                   Antonin Scalia,
     Associate Justice.

                          ____________________
```
