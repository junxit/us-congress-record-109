# APPOINTMENT AS HISTORIAN OF THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

```
 APPOINTMENT AS HISTORIAN OF THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

order of the House of January 4, 2005, the Chair announces that on
```
