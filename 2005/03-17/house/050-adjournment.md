# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. DAVIS of Kentucky. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now 
adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Accordingly, pursuant to the previous order of the House of today, 
the House stands adjourned until 2 p.m. on Monday, March 21, 2005,

previous order of the House of today, the House adjourned until 2 p.m. 
on Monday, March 21, 2005, unless it sooner has received a message from 
the Senate transmitting its adoption of House Concurrent Resolution 
103, in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.
```
