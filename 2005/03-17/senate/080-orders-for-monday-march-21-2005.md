# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 21, 2005

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 21, 2005

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 4 p.m. on Monday, 
March 21; I further ask that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed to have expired, the Journal of the proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved, and the 
Senate begin a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to 
speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
