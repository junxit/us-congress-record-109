# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1718

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1718

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the bill for 
the second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1718) to provide special rules for disaster 
     relief employment under the Workforce Investment Act of 1998 
     for individuals displaced by Hurricane Katrina.

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, in order to place the bill on the 
calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I object to further 
proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
