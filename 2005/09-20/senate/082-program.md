# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Bennett, Robert F. (B000382) [R-UT]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, tomorrow, the Senate will return to the 
consideration of the Agriculture appropriations bill. Under a previous 
order, there is a filing deadline of 4 p.m. for first-degree 
amendments. I urge Senators to come forward early with their amendments 
so we can finish the bill tomorrow night. Rollcall votes will occur 
throughout the day tomorrow, and I announce on behalf of the majority 
leader that a late night may be necessary in order to finish the bill.
```
