# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 4:39 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
agrees to the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 3649) to ensure 
funding for sportfishing and boating safety programs funded out of the 
Highway Trust Fund through the end of fiscal year 2005, and for other 
purposes.
  The message also announced that the House has passed the following 
bill, without amendment:

       S. 1340. An act to amend the Pittman-Robertson Wildlife 
     Restoration Act to extend the date after which surplus funds 
     in the wildlife restoration fund become available for 
     apportionment.

                          ____________________
```
