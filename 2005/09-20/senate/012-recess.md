# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I know of no other amendments available 
to us. Unless someone wishes to speak in morning business between now 
and the time we routinely break for the policy lunches, I ask unanimous 
consent that the Senate stand in recess until 2:15 p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:10 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Sununu).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Democratic 
leader is recognized.
```
