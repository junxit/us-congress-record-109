# CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1704

**Speaking:** Cannon, Chris (C000116) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.R. 1704

```
            CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1704

  Mr. CANNON. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 1704, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Portman of Ohio, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Miller of Michigan). Is there objection 
to the request of the gentleman from Utah?
  There was no objection.
```
