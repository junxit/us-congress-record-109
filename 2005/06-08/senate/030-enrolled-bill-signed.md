# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 1760, H.R. 1790

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  The message further announced that the Speaker of the House of 
Representatives has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 1760 An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 215 Martin Luther King, Jr. 
     Boulevard in Madison, Wisconsin, as the ``Robert M. 
     LaFollette, Sr. Post Office Building''.



 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S6223, June 8, 2005, under ``ENROLLED BILL SIGNED'', the 
following measure appeared: H.R. 1790.
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: H.R. 1760


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 

  The enrolled bill was signed subsequently by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Stevens).

                          ____________________
```
