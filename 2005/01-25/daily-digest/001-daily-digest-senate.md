26, 2005, with a vote on confirmation of the nomination to occur 
following 60 minutes for closing remarks.
Pages S378-S424, S507

Colorado, to be Secretary of Veterans Affairs, following the vote on 
the nomination of Condoleezza Rice, with 30 minutes of debate equally 
divided, followed by a vote on confirmation of the nomination.

Utah, to be Secretary of Health and Human Services, following the vote

divided, followed by a vote on confirmation of the nomination.
