# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                          committee on finance

  Mr. LUGAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Finance be authorized to meet in open executive session during the 
session on Tuesday, January 25, 2005, at 10 a.m., to organize for the 
109th Congress. The committee will also consider favorably reporting 
the nomination of Michael O. Leavitt, to be Secretary of Health and 
Human Services.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
