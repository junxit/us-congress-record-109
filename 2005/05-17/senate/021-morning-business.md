# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. INHOFE. I ask unanimous consent there now be a period for morning 
business, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes on any 
subject, with Senator Boxer going first.
  Mrs. BOXER. Reserving the right to object, and I will not object, but 
my statement will run 30 minutes. I ask that be amended at this point.
  Mr. INHOFE. I have no objection.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from California.
```
