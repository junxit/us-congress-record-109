# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 168, H.Res. 333, H.Res. 343

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on two of the motions to suspend the rules previously 
postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H. Con. Res. 168, by the yeas and nays; and
  H. Res. 333, by the yeas and nays.
  Proceedings on H. Res. 343 will resume tomorrow. This series will 
comprise two 15-minute votes.
```
