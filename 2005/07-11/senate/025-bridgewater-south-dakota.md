# BRIDGEWATER, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       BRIDGEWATER, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I offer my congratulations to 
the town of Bridgewater, located in McCook County, SD. The town will 
celebrate the 125th anniversary of its founding this year.
  The town was originally named Nation City for its founders, Robert 
and John B. Nation. However, it was renamed Bridgewater and moved to 
the northern side of the railroad tracks in 1880.
  Once again, I congratulate Bridgewater on their anniversary and wish 
them the best of luck in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
