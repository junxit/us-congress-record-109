# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Alexander, Lamar (A000360) [R-TN], Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. ALEXANDER. Mr. President, I see my friend from Oregon here. I ask 
unanimous consent to speak a little bit longer than 10 minutes if that 
would not inconvenience him, or would he like to go?

Alexander completes his remarks, Senator Smith, my colleague from 
Oregon, and I may speak for up to 30 minutes. We may not consume all of 
that time.

ordered.
  Mr. ALEXANDER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to speak for 
up to 20 minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
