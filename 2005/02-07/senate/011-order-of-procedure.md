# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mrs. BOXER. Madam President, may I ask what is the order at the 
current time?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Morning business, with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  Mrs. BOXER. Does one have to ask unanimous consent to go past the 10 
minutes?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Yes.
  Mrs. BOXER. I ask unanimous consent I be able to speak for up to 20 
minutes as in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
