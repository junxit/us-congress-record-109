# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. INHOFE. There being no further business to come before the 
Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment 
under the previous order, following the remarks of Senator Boxer for 
not to exceed 60 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. INHOFE. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
