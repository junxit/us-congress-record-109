# NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 271

```
                      NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS


                 committee on rules and administration

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee on 
Rules and Administration will meet on Wednesday, April 27, 2005, at 
9:30 a.m., to mark up S. 271, a bill which reforms the regulatory and 
reporting structure of organizations registered under Section 527 of 
the Internal Revenue Code.
  For further information regarding this hearing, please contact Susan 
Wells at the Rules and Administration Committee on 224-6352.
```
