# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 36

```
Committee Meetings
RESOLUTION--COLLEGE MILITARY RECRUITMENT; OVERSIGHT PLAN
Committee on Rules: Granted, by voice vote, a closed rule providing 1 
hour of debate in the House on H. Con. Res. 36, expressing the 
continued support of Congress for equal access of military recruiters 
to institutions of higher education, equally divided and controlled by 
the chairman and ranking minority member of the Committee on Armed 
Services. The rule provides one motion to recommit. Testimony was heard 
from Representatives Kline and Rogers of Alabama.
  The Committee also approved an Oversight Plan for the 109th Congress.
```
