# APPOINTMENT OF SENATOR BURR TO READ WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

```
   APPOINTMENT OF SENATOR BURR TO READ WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to the order of the Senate of January 24, 1901, appoints the 
Senator from North Carolina, Mr. Burr, to read Washington's Farewell 
Address on Friday, February 18, 2005.
```
