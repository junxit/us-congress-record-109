# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will resume 
consideration of the nomination of Alberto Gonzales to be Attorney 
General. A number of colleagues spoke on this nomination today, and we 
expect a full day of debate tomorrow as well. Under the agreement, we 
will alternate debate in 1-hour blocks throughout the day. Again, I 
encourage those Members who wish to speak on the Gonzales nomination to 
contact the chairman and ranking member of the Judiciary Committee as 
soon as possible.
  I also remind my colleagues the President's State of the Union 
Address will be tomorrow evening. Senators are asked to be in the 
Senate Chamber by 8:30 tomorrow night in order to proceed as a body to 
the House Chamber at 8:40 for the 9 o'clock address.

                          ____________________
```
