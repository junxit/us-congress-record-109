# EXECUTIVE REPORT OF COMMITTEE

```
                     EXECUTIVE REPORT OF COMMITTEE

  The following executive report of committee was submitted:

           By Mr. LUGAR, from the Committee on Foreign Relations:
       Convention Concerning Migratory Fish Stock in the Pacific 
     Ocean (Treaty Doc. 109-1) (Ex. Rept. 109-8).
       Text of the resolution of ratification as reported by the 
     Committee on Foreign Relations:
       Resolved (two-thirds of the Senators present concurring 
     therein),
       The Senate advises and consents to the ratification of the 
     Convention on the Conservation and Management of the Highly 
     Migratory Fish Stocks in the Western and Central Pacific 
     Ocean, with Annexes, adopted at Honolulu on September 5, 
     2000, by the Multilateral High Level Conference on the Highly 
     Migratory Fish Stocks in the Western and Central Pacific 
     Ocean, and signed by the United States on that date (Treaty 
     Doc. 109-1).

                          ____________________
```
