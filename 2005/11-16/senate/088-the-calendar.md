**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1972

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate en bloc consideration of the following bills 
reported out of the Energy Committee: Calendar Nos. 236 through 240; 
242 through 249; 262 through 273; and H.R. 1972, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

amendments at the desk be agreed to, the committee-reported amendments, 
as amended, if amended, be agreed to, the bills, as amended, if 
amended, be read a third time and passed, and the title amendments be 
agreed to, all en bloc.
```
