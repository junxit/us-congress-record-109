# IN RECOGNITION OF GERALD AND JULIA GEIER

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
                IN RECOGNITION OF GERALD AND JULIA GEIER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 16, 2005

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker I rise today to pay special 
recognition to Gerald and Julia Geier, who will be celebrating their 
50th wedding anniversary tomorrow, December 17, 2005. In fact, they 
intend to renew their vows at a celebration being held at Saks Baptist 
Church in my hometown of Anniston, AL.
  I attend church with this fine couple. It is wonderful to see two 
people who still care so much about each other after all these years. 
Their commitment is an inspiration to everyone who knows them.
  I salute Gerald and Julia Geier on their 50th anniversary and join 
their family in honoring them on this special occasion.

                          ____________________
```
