# ANNOUNCEMENT REGARDING RECOGNITION OF HELEN SEWELL'S RETIREMENT

```
    ANNOUNCEMENT REGARDING RECOGNITION OF HELEN SEWELL'S RETIREMENT

  Mr. BLUNT. Mr. Speaker, I have a brief announcement. The announcement 
is that we would also plan in our activities tomorrow to have a brief 
recognition of Helen Sewell, who has run the cloakroom here for a long 
period of time. Between she and her father, who started work here 87 
years ago, they have been a continued presence in the cloakroom on this 
side. Tomorrow will be Helen's last official day before she retires.

                          ____________________
```
