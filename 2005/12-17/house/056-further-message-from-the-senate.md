# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 75, S. 2141

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment a joint 
resolution of the House of the following title:

       H.J. Res. 75. Joint resolution making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2006, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 2141. An act to make improvements to the Federal Deposit 
     Insurance Act.

                          ____________________
```
