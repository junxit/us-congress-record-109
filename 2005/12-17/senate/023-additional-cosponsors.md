# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 2082

  At the request of Mr. Baucus, his name was added as a cosponsor of S. 
2082, a bill to amend the USA PATRIOT Act to extend the sunset of 
certain provisions of that Act and the lone wolf provision of the 
Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 to March 31, 
2006.


                           AMENDMENT NO. 2681

  At the request of Mr. Kennedy, his name was added as a cosponsor of 
amendment No. 2681 proposed to H.R. 3402, a bill to authorize 
appropriations for the Department of Justice for fiscal years 2006 
through 2009, and for other purposes.
```
