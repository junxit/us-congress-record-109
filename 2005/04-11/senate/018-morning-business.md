# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous 
consent that there now be a period of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the two 
Senators from Washington, Senators Cantwell and Murray, be recognized 
now to speak for up to 30 minutes and that I be recognized to speak for 
up to 30 minutes at the conclusion of their remarks.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Washington.

                          ____________________
```
