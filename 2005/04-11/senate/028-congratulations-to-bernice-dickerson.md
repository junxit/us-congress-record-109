# CONGRATULATIONS TO BERNICE DICKERSON

```
                  CONGRATULATIONS TO BERNICE DICKERSON

● Mr. BUNNING. Mr. President, I pay tribute and congratulate 
Bernice Dickerson of Adairville, KY, for her service on the Adairville 
City Council.
  At age 91, Mrs. Dickerson has dedicated herself to improving the 
community of Adairville. As a councilwoman for Adairville, Mrs. 
Dickerson has made a difference in people's lives and has served as an 
example for all who know her.
  The citizens of Kentucky are fortunate to have the leadership of 
Bernice Dickerson. Her example of dedication, hard work and compassion 
should be an inspiration to all throughout the Commonwealth. She has my 
most sincere appreciation for her work and I look forward to her 
continued service to Kentucky.●

                          ____________________
```
