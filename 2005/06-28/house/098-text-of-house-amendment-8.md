# Text of House Amendment 8

**Speaking:** Bradley, Jeb (B001246) [R-NH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3057

```
H.R. 3057

                Offered By: Mr. Bradley of New Hampshire

       Amendment No. 8: Page 132, after line 13, insert the 
     following:

limitation on assistance to Romania under the Support for East European 
                      Democracy (SEED) Act of 1989

       Sec. __. None of the funds appropriated in this Act under 
     the heading ``assistance for eastern europe and the baltic 
     states'' for assistance to Romania under the Support for East 
     European Democracy (SEED) Act of 1989 (except $15,000,000 of 
     such funds) may be obligated or expended before January 15, 
     2006.
```
