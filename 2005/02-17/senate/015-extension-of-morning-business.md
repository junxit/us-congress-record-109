# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that morning 
business be extended until 3 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I am sometimes asked back in Illinois how 
the Senate can have morning business in the afternoon. I still can't 
answer that question, but we will continue to have it this afternoon.

                          ____________________
```
