# AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
      AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the senior Senator from Virginia and the 
junior Senator from Virginia be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills 
and joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
