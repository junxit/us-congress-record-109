# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, FEBRUARY 21, 2005

```
          CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, FEBRUARY 21, 2005

  Mr. YOUNG of Alaska. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday, 
February 21, 2005, unless it sooner has received a message from the 
Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 66, 
in which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that 
concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Alaska?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
