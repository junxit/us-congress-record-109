# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT E. ANDREWS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 17, 2005

  Mr. ANDREWS. Mr. Speaker, I regret that I missed two votes on 
February 15th, 2005. One to recognize the contributions of Jibreel 
Khazan (Ezell Blair, Jr.), David Richmond, Joseph McNeil, and Franklin 
McCain, the ``Greensboro Four'', to the civil rights movement and 
another to designate the facility of the United States Postal Service 
located at 321 Montgomery Road in Altamonte Springs, Florida, as the 
``Arthur Stacey Mastrapa Post Office Building.'' Had I been present I 
would have voted ``yea'' on both rollcall 32 and rollcall 33.

                          ____________________
```
