# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 686

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Sunday, March 20, 2005

  Mrs. MALONEY. Mr. Speaker, on March 21, 2005, I was unavoidably 
detained and missed rollcall vote number 90. Rollcall vote 90 was on S. 
686, a private bill to provide for the relief of the parents of Theresa 
Marie Schiavo.
  Had I been present I would have voted ``nay'' on rollcall vote 90.
```
