# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:33 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its clerks, announced that it has passed the 
following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 3. An act to authorize funds for Federal-aid highways, 
     highway safety programs, and transit programs, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 1928a, the 
order of the House of January 4, 2005, and clause 10 of rule 1, the 
Speaker appoints the following Members of the House of Representatives 
to the United States Group of the North Atlantic Assembly: Mr. Tanner 
of Tennessee, Mr. Ross of Arkansas, Mr. Chandler of Kentucky, and Mrs. 
Tauscher of California.

                          ____________________
```
