# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 686, S. 687

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. FRIST (for himself, Mr. Martinez, and Mr. 
             Santorum):
       S. 686. A bill to provide for the relief of the parents of 
     Theresa Marie Schiavo; considered and passed.
           By Mr. BURNS (for himself, Mr. Wyden, Mrs. Boxer, and 
             Mr. Nelson of Florida):
       S. 687. A bill to regulate the unauthorized installation of 
     computer software, to require clear disclosure to computer 
     users of certain computer software features that may pose a 
     threat to user privacy, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
```
