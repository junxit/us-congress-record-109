# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
stand in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 2:05 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 4:30 p.m. when called to 
order by the Acting President pro tempore.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The majority leader is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
