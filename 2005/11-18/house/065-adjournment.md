# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Weldon, Curt (W000268) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 307

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. WELDON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 307, 109th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 307, 
109th Congress, the House stands adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, 
December 6, 2005.
  Thereupon (at midnight), pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 307, 
the House adjourned until Tuesday, December 6, 2005, at 2 p.m.
```
