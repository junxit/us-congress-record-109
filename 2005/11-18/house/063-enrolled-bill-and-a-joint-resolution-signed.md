# ENROLLED BILL AND A JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 72, H.R. 4326

```
              ENROLLED BILL AND A JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  Mr. Trandahl, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled a 
bill and a Joint Resolution of the House of the following titles, which 
were thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 4326. An act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to 
     enter into a contract for the nuclear refueling and complex 
     overhaul of the U.S.S. Carl Vinson (CVN-70).
       H.J. Res. 72. Joint resolution making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2006, and for other 
     purposes.
```
