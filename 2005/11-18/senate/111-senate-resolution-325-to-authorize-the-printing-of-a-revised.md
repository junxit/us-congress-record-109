# SENATE RESOLUTION 325--TO AUTHORIZE THE PRINTING OF A REVISED EDITION OF THE SENATE ELECTION LAW GUIDEBOOK

**Measures:** S.Res. 325

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 325--TO AUTHORIZE THE PRINTING OF A REVISED EDITION 
                  OF THE SENATE ELECTION LAW GUIDEBOOK

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 325

       Resolved,  That the Committee on Rules and Administration 
     shall prepare a revised edition of the Senate Election Law 
     Guidebook, Senate Document 106-14 , and that such document 
     shall be printed as a Senate document.
       Sec. 2. There shall be printed, beyond the usual number, 
     500 additional copies of the document specified in the first 
     section for the use of the Committee on Rules and 
     Administration.
```
