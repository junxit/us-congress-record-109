# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, November 18, 
2005, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bill:

       S. 1234. An act to increase, effective as of December 1, 
     2005, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled 
     veterans.
```
