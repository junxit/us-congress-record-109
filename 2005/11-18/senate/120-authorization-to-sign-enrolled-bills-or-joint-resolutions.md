# AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
       AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader, the majority whip, and 
the senior Senator from Virginia be authorized to sign duly enrolled 
bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
