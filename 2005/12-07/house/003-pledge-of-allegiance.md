# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER. Will the gentleman from Colorado (Mr. Hefley) come 
forward and lead the House in the Pledge of Allegiance.
  I would like to remind the House that today is December 7, a day, as 
announced from this podium, that would live in infamy, because this 
country was not ready at that time to protect and defend its borders 
and defend its people.
  I hope we have learned from the lessons of the past.
  Mr. HEFLEY led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.

                          ____________________
```
