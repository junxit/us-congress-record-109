                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                  S. 7

(Mr. Vitter) was added as a cosponsor of S. 7, a bill to increase 
American jobs and economic growth by making permanent the individual 
income tax rate reductions, the reduction in the capital gains and 
dividend tax rates, and the repeal of the estate, gift, and generation-
skipping transfer taxes.
