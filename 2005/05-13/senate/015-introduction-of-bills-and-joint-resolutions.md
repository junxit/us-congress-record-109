# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. ALEXANDER (for himself and Mr. Warner):
       S. 1034. A bill to provide for local control for the siting 
     of windmills; to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources.
           By Mr. INHOFE (for himself, Mr. Johnson, Mr. Thune, Mr. 
             Grassley, and Mr. Harkin):
       S. 1035. A bill to authorize the presentation of 
     commemorative medals on behalf of Congress to Native 
     Americans who served as Code Talkers during foreign conflicts 
     in which the United States was involved during the 20th 
     century in recognition of the service of those Native 
     Americans to the United States; to the Committee on Banking, 
     Housing, and Urban Affairs.
```
