# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1940, S. 1941

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. KYL:
       S. 1940. A bill to create a national register of cases of 
     child abuse or neglect; to the Committee on Health, 
     Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. HATCH (for himself, Mr. Bennett, Mr. Reid, and 
             Mr. Ensign):
       S. 1941. A bill to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to

     the State of Utah, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Energy and Natural Resources.
```
