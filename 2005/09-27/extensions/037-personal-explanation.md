# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Blumenauer, Earl (B000574) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.Res. 427, H.Res. 428, H.R. 3668

```
[[Page E1962]]
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. EARL BLUMENAUER

                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 27, 2005

  Mr. BLUMENAUER. Mr. Speaker, had I been present for the three final 
votes on Thursday, September 8th, 2005, I would have voted as follows:
  Rollcall vote No. 462: I would have voted ``aye'' on H.R. 3668, the 
``Student Grant Hurricane and Disaster Relief Act.''
  Rollcall vote No. 463: I would have voted ``aye'' on H. Res. 428, 
expressing the sincere gratitude of the House of Representatives to the 
foreign individuals, organizations, and governments that have offered 
material assistance and other forms of support to those who have been 
affected by Hurricane Katrina.
  Rollcall vote No. 464: I would have voted ``aye'' on H. Res. 427, 
relating to the terrorist attacks against the United States on 
September 11, 2001.
```
