# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1771

**Speaking:** Coburn, Tom (C000560) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S. 1771

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1771

  Mr. COBURN. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the second 
time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1771) to express the sense of Congress and to 
     improve reporting with respect to the safety of workers in 
     the response and recovery activities related to Hurricane 
     Katrina, and for other purposes.

  Mr. COBURN. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be placed on 
the calendar under rule XIV.

                          ____________________
```
