# CONGRATULATING RANDY EVANS AND RANDY EVANS CONSTRUCTION FOR WINNING THE SEORC BUSINESS OF THE YEAR AWARD

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
CONGRATULATING RANDY EVANS AND RANDY EVANS CONSTRUCTION FOR WINNING THE 
                    SEORC BUSINESS OF THE YEAR AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 19, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Randy Evans, with effort and desire, started Randy Evans 
Construction and has made it a significant competitor in the Jackson 
area construction business; and
  Whereas, Randy Evans and Randy Evans Construction have helped shape 
the Jackson area community; and
  Whereas, Randy Evans' commitment to perfection, perseverance and 
fortitude are an inspiration to everyone in Jackson County and beyond.
  Therefore, I join with family, friends and the entire 18th 
Congressional District of Ohio in congratulating Randy Evans and Randy 
Evans Construction for winning this prestigious award.
```
