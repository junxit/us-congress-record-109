# CONGRATULATING BEAR VILLAGE AND KEITH AND LYNN DENNY FOR WINNING THE BEAUTIFICATION AWARD

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
 CONGRATULATING BEAR VILLAGE AND KEITH AND LYNN DENNY FOR WINNING THE 
                          BEAUTIFICATION AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 19, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Keith and Lynn Denny have built one of the premier gift 
shops in Jackson County; and
  Whereas, Bear Village has grown to be a impeccable illustration of 
what Jackson County has to offer and what great potential the community 
has; and
  Whereas, Keith and Lynn Denny's vision has added greatly to the 
beauty of Jackson County and is further enhancing the attractiveness of 
Water Street.
  Therefore, I join with family, friends and the entire 18th 
Congressional District of Ohio in congratulating Bear Village and Keith 
and Lynn Denny for winning this prestigious award.

                          ____________________
```
