# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 258, H.R. 1268

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. COLE: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 258. 
     Resolution waiving points of order against the conference 
     report to accompany the bill (H.R. 1268) making Emergency 
     Supplemental Appropriations for Defense, the Global War on 
     Terror, and Tsunami Relief, for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2005, and for other purposes (Rept. 109-73). 
     Referred to the House Calendar.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  May 4, 2005--On Page H 2975 the following appeared: and for 
other purposes (Rept. 109-3). Referred to the House Calendar.
  
  The online version should be corrected to read: : and for other 
purposes (Rept. 109-73). Referred to the House Calendar.
```
