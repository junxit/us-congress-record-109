# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 8, 2005

  Mrs. MALONEY. Mr. Speaker, on September 7, 2005, I was unavoidably 
detained and missed rollcall votes numbered 456 and 457. Rollcall vote 
456 was on the motion to suspend the rules and agree to H.R. 3650, a 
bill to allow United States Courts to conduct business during emergency 
conditions, and for other purposes. Rollcall vote 457 was on the motion 
to suspend the rules and agree to H.R. 3169, a bill to provide the 
Secretary of Education with waiver authority for students who are 
eligible for Pell Grants who are adversely affected by a natural 
disaster.
  Had I been present I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall votes 456 
and 457.

                          ____________________
```
