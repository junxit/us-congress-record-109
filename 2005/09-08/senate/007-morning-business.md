# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for up to 
60 minutes, with the first half of the time to be controlled by the 
Democratic leader or his designee and the second half of the time to be 
controlled by the majority leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Hawaii.

                          ____________________
```
