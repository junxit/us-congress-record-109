# AUTHORITY TO SUBMIT TRIBUTES TO CHIEF JUSTICE REHNQUIST

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
        AUTHORITY TO SUBMIT TRIBUTES TO CHIEF JUSTICE REHNQUIST

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Senators be 
permitted to submit tributes to Chief Justice Rehnquist for the Record 
until September 30, 2005, and that all tributes be printed as a Senate 
document.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
