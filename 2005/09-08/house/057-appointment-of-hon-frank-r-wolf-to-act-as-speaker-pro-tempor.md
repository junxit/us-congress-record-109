# APPOINTMENT OF HON. FRANK R. WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 13, 2005

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. FRANK R. WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN 
    ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 13, 2005

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. King of Iowa) laid before the House the 
following communication from the Speaker:

                                              The Speaker's Rooms,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, September 8, 2005.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Frank R. Wolf to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through September 13, 2005.
                                                   Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
