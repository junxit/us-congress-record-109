# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Poe, Ted (P000592) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2360

```
H.R. 2360

                          Offered By: Mr. Poe

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following (and conform the table of 
     contents of the bill accordingly):
       Sec. 509. None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used to carry out section 105(a)(4) and (5) of the 
     Aviation and Transportation Security Act of 2001 (49 U.S.C. 
     44917(a)(4) and (5)).
```
