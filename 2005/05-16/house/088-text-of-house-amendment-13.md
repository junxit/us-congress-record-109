# Text of House Amendment 13

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2360

```
H.R. 2360

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 13: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 536. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be used to support or supplement 
     the the acts of volunteer patrols at or in the vicinity of 
     the borders of the United States.
```
