# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Honda, Michael M. (H001034) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1544

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL M. HONDA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 16, 2005

  Mr. HONDA. Mr. Speaker, on Thursday, May 12th, I was unavoidably 
detained and missed rollcall votes on that day.
  Had I been present I would have voted the following: ``no'' on 
rollcall vote number 169, the Weiner Amendment limiting the number of 
Urban Area Security Initiative grants during any given fiscal year to 
50, and ``yea'' on rollcall vote number 170, final passage of H.R. 
1544, the Faster & Smarter Funding for First Responders Act of 2005.
```
