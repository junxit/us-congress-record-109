# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. KATHERINE HARRIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 15, 2005

  Ms. HARRIS. Mr. Speaker, due to the recent devastation caused by 
Hurricane Wilma on the State of Florida, I was unable to attend votes 
on Thursday, October 27, 2005 due to a visit to the State with 
President Bush and other delegation Members. While I am disappointed I 
was not able to be present, I am asking, that you include the following 
intended votes in the Congressional Record.
  I would have voted nay on H.J. Res. 65, the Schiff Amendment to H.R. 
420, as well as the Democratic Motion to Recommit H.R. 420. I would 
have voted yea on H.R. 3945, H. Res. 368, Final Passage of H.R. 420, 
and the Motion to Instruct Conferees on H.R. 3047.
  Again, I apologize for my absence and thank you in advance for your 
time and attention to this matter.
```
