# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FAUQUIER TIMES-DEMOCRAT

**Speaking:** Wolf, Frank R. (W000672) [R-VA]

```
[[Page E2345]]


     HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FAUQUIER TIMES-DEMOCRAT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. FRANK R. WOLF

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 15, 2005

  Mr. WOLF. Mr. Speaker, I am proud today to honor the 100th year 
anniversary of publication of the Fauquier Times-Democrat. Over the 
years, the Democrat has proudly served the people of Fauquier and the 
Warrenton community in Virginia's 10th Congressional District. The 
Democrat has undoubtedly carved out a respected place in the family of 
Virginia newspapers.
  I would like to thank the Fauquier Times-Democrat and members of its 
staff for their tremendous dedication to the people of northern 
Virginia and wish them continued success for the future.
```
