# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

```
      PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

privileged report (Rept. No. 109-296) on the resolution (H. Res. 515) 
of inquiry requesting the President of the United States to provide to

regions of the United States, which was referred to the House Calendar
```
