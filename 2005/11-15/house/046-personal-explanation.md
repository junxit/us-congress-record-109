# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Andrews, Robert E. (A000210) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 323, H.R. 856, H.R. 1564

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. ANDREWS. Mr. Speaker, I regret that I missed three votes on 
November 15th, 2005. Had I been present I would have voted ``yes'' on 
H.R. 1564 (Yakima-Tieton Irrigation District Conveyance Act); ``yes'' 
on H.R 323 (to redesignate the Ellis Island Library on the third floor 
of the Ellis Island Immigration Museum, located on Ellis Island in New 
York Harbor, as the ``Bob Hope Memorial Library''); and ``yes'' on H.R. 
856 (Federal Youth Coordination Act).
```
