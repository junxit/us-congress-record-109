# DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2006

**Measures:** S. 1043

```
      DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2006

  The bill (S. 1043) to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2006 
for military activities of the Department of Defense, to prescribe 
personnel strengths for such fiscal year for the Armed Forces, and for 
other purposes, was considered, ordered to be engrossed for a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed, as amended.
  (The text of the bill will be printed in a future edition of the 
Record.)
```
