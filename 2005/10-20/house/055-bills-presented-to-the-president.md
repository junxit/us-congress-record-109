# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on October 20, 2005, 
he presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills.

       H.R. 3765. A bill to extend through March 31, 2006, the 
     authority of the Secretary of the Army to accept and expend 
     funds contributed by non-Federal public entities and to 
     expedite the processing of permits.
       H.R. 3971. Medicare Cost Sharing and Welfare Extension Act 
     of 2005.
```
