# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 24, 2005, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, OCTOBER 25, 2005

```
    ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 24, 2005, AND HOUR OF MEETING ON 
                       TUESDAY, OCTOBER 25, 2005
```
