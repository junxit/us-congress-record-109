# APPOINTMENT

```
                              APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President of the 
Senate, and after consultation with the Majority Leader, pursuant to 
Public Law 106-286, appoints the following Members to serve on the 
Congressional-Executive Commission on the People's Republic of China: 
The Senator from Nebraska, Mr. Hagel, Chairman; the Senator from 
Kansas, Mr. Brownback; the Senator from Oregon, Mr. Smith; the Senator 
from South Carolina, Mr. DeMint; and the Senator from Florida, Mr. 
Martinez.
```
