# JOINT MEETING DISSOLVED

```
                        JOINT MEETING DISSOLVED

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The purpose of the joint meeting having been 
completed, the Chair declares the joint meeting of the two Houses now 
dissolved.
  Accordingly, at 11 o'clock and 44 minutes a.m., the joint meeting of 
the two Houses was dissolved.
  The Members of the Senate retired to their Chamber.

                          ____________________
```
