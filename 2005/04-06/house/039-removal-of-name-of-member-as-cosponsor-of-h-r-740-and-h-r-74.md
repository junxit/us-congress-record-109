# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 740 and H.R. 742

**Speaking:** Price, David E. (P000523) [D-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 740, H.R. 742

```
    REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 740 and H.R. 742

  Mr. PRICE of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to 
have my name removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 740 and 742. My name was 
mistakenly added to these bills by the sponsor in place of my 
colleague, Representative Tom Price.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
