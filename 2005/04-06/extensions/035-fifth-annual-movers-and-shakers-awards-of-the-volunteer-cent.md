  Mr. MOORE of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to note an important 
event in the Third Congressional District of Kansas. On Apri1 18, 2005, 
the Volunteer Center of Johnson County in Overland Park, Kansas, will 
honor outstanding youth volunteers. Seventy-one young people have been 
nominated by school personnel and nonprofit organizations for their 
dedication and service to the community. Youth volunteerism continues 
to grow and be a strong force in Johnson County. These 71 youths 
exemplify the true meaning of volunteerism and giving back to their 
community. It is my honor to recognize each student volunteer, their 
school, their age, number of hours volunteered, and their hometown by 
listing them in the Congressional Record.
