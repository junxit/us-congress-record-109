# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Enzi, Michael B. (E000285) [R-WY]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business throughout the day. A number of our colleagues will be 
traveling to Rome to attend the funeral of Pope John Paul II. We will 
return next week and begin consideration of the Iraq-Afghanistan 
supplemental appropriations bill. Senators should expect a busy week 
with rollcall votes throughout. Senators should be aware that we will 
have a Monday evening vote at approximately 5:15, and we will lock that 
in tomorrow morning.
```
