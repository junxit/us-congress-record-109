# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be time for the transaction of morning business for up to 60 
minutes, with the first half hour under the control of the Democratic 
leader or his designee and the second half hour under the control of 
the majority leader or his designee.
  The Senator from New York.
```
