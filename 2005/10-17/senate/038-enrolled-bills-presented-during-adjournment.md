# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
              ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on Friday, October 7, 2005, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 1413. A act to redesignate the Crowne Plaza in Kingston, 
     Jamaica, as the Colin L. Powell Residential Plaza.
       S. 1786. An act to authorize the Secretary of 
     Transportation to make emergency airport improvement project 
     grants-in-aid under title 49, United States Code, for repairs 
     and costs related to damage from Hurricanes Katrina and Rita.
       S. 1858. An act to provide for community disaster loans.

                          ____________________
```
