# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
October 17, 2005 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:

       Mark McKinnon, of Texas, to be a Member of the Broadcasting 
     Board of Governors for a term expiring August 13, 2008, which 
     was sent to the Senate on September 6, 2005.
```
