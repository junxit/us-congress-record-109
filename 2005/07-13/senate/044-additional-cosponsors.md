                                 S. 21

Dakota (Mr. Conrad) was added as a cosponsor of S. 37, a bill to extend

cosponsors of S. 58, a bill to amend title 10, United States Code, to 
permit former members of the Armed Forces who have a service-connected 
disability rated as total to travel on military aircraft in the same 
manner and to the same extent as retired members of the Armed Forces 
are entitled to travel on such aircraft.
