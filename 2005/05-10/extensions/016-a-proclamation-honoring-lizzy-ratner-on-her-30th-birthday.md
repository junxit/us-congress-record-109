# A PROCLAMATION HONORING LIZZY RATNER ON HER 30TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
       A PROCLAMATION HONORING LIZZY RATNER ON HER 30TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 10, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Lizzy Ratner is celebrating her 30th Birthday today; and
  Whereas, Lizzy Ratner has been and continues to be a positive 
influence on all persons fortunate to meet her; and
  Whereas, Lizzy Ratner has shown love for her family and friends and 
must be commended for her dedication to these relationships.

[[Page E919]]

  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District of Ohio in congratulating Lizzy Ratner as she celebrates her 
30th Birthday.

                          ____________________
```
