# A PROCLAMATION HONORING BRUCE RATNER ON HIS 60TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
       A PROCLAMATION HONORING BRUCE RATNER ON HIS 60TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 10, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Bruce Ratner is celebrating his 60th birthday with family 
and friends; and
  Whereas, Bruce Ratner is a positive influence on all persons in his 
life, leading by example; and
  Whereas, Bruce Ratner has exemplified a love for his family, and must 
be commended for his dedicated service to the community.
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District of Ohio in congratulating Bruce Ratner as he celebrates his 
60th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
