# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Pombo, Richard W. (P000419) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 739, H.R. 740, H.R. 741, H.R. 742

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. POMBO. Mr. Speaker, on July 12, 2005, I missed four recorded 
votes. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on H.R. 739, the 
Occupational Safety and Health Small Business Day in Court Act of 2005 
(rollcall No. 369); ``yea'' on H.R. 740, the Occupational Safety and 
Health Review Commission Efficiency Act of 2005 (rollcall No. 370); 
``yea'' on H.R. 741, the Occupational Safety and Health Independent 
Review of OSHA Citations Act of 2005 (rollcall No. 371); and ``yea'' on 
H.R. 742, the Occupational Safety and Health Small Employer Access to 
Justice Act of 2005 (rollcall No. 372).
```
