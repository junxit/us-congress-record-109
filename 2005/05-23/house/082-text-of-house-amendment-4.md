# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Stupak, Bart (S001045) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2419

```
H.R. 2419

                         Offered By: Mr. Stupak

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill, add the following 
     new section:

       Sec. 503. None of the funds made available by this Act 
     shall be used to accept deliveries of petroleum products to 
     the Strategic Petroleum Reserve.
```
