# Text of House Amendment 3

**Speaking:** Spratt, John M., Jr. (S000749) [D-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2419

```
H.R. 2419

                         Offered By: Mr. Spratt

       Amendment No. 3: At the end of the bill, add the following 
     new section:
       Sec. 503. None of the funds made available by this Act 
     shall be obligated or expended in contravention of the 
     Nuclear Waste Policy Act of 1982.
```
