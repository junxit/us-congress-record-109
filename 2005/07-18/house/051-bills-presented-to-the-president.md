# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on July 15, 2005 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.R. 1001. To designate the facility of the United States 
     Postal Service located at 301 South Heatherwilde Boulevard in 
     Pflugerville, Texas, as the ``Sergeant Byron W. Norwood Post 
     Office Building''.
       H.R. 3071. To permit the individuals currently serving as 
     Executive Director, Deputy Executive Directors, and General 
     Counsel of the Office of Compliance to serve one additional 
     term.

                          ____________________
```
