# AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT HIS EXCELLENCY, DR. MANMOHAN SINGH

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
  AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF COMMITTEE TO ESCORT HIS EXCELLENCY, DR. 
                             MANMOHAN SINGH

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
President of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the 
part of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the 
House of Representatives to escort his Excellency, Dr. Manmohan Singh, 
the Prime Minister of India, into the House Chamber for a joint meeting 
tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
