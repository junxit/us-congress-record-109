# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. Tomorrow morning, the Senate should be aware that 
Senators should meet in the Chamber at 9:40 to proceed as a body to the 
joint meeting of Congress to hear from Prime Minister Singh of India. 
At 11 a.m. the Senate will convene to debate and vote on the Burma 
trade resolution. If all time is used, a vote on the Burma resolution 
will occur around 12:20.

[[Page 16261]]

  At 2:15, after the respective party luncheons, we will resume 
consideration of the Foreign Operations appropriations bill. Let me 
interject as one of the managers of that bill we intend to finish that 
bill tomorrow. For any Members who have amendments, we would rather do 
them in the daylight than at night. We intend to have a busy afternoon.

                          ____________________
```
