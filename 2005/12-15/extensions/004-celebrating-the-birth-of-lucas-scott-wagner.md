# CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF LUCAS SCOTT WAGNER

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
              CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF LUCAS SCOTT WAGNER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 15, 2005

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, today I am happy to 
congratulate Matt and Kate Wagner of Columbus, Georgia, on the birth of 
their new baby son. Lucas Scott Wagner was born on December 15, 2005 at 
1:29 PM, weighing 6 pounds, 11 ounces and measuring 19 inches long. 
Luke has been born into a loving home, where he will be raised by 
parents who are devoted to his well-being and bright future. His birth 
is a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
