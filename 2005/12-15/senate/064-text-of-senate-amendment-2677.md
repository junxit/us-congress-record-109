# Text of Senate Amendment 2677

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
  SA 2677. Mr. McCONNELL (for Mr. Stevens) proposed an amendment to the 
bill S. 1390, to reauthorize the Coral Reef Conservation Act of 2000, 
and for other purposes; as follows:

       On page 3, beginning in line 24, strike ``impacts or other 
     physical damage to coral reefs, including'' and insert 
     ``impacts, derelict fishing gear, vessel anchors and anchor 
     chains, or''.
                                 ______
```
