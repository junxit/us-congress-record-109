# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2892

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2892

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2892) to amend section 255 of the National 
     Housing Act to remove the limitation on the number of reverse 
     mortgages that may be insured under the FHA mortgage 
     insurance program for such mortgages.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I now ask for its second reading and, in order to 
place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read 
again on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
