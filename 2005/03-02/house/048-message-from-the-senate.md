# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment bills of the 
House of the following titles:

       H. Con. Res. 5. Concurrent resolution providing for the 
     acceptance of a statue of Sarah Winnemucca, presented by the 
     people of Nevada, for placement in National Statuary Hall, 
     and for other purposes.
       H. Con. Res. 63. Concurrent resolution permitting the use 
     of the rotunda of the Capitol for a ceremony as part of the 
     commemoration of the days of remembrance of victims of the 
     Holocaust.

                          ____________________
```
