# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 126, H.R. 912

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule xx, proceedings 
will resume on questions previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H. Res. 126, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 912, by the yeas and nays.
  Without objection, the minimum time for electronic voting on the 
second question will be reduced to 5 minutes, notwithstanding the 
intervention of remarks concerning the passing of a former Member.
  There was no objection.
```
