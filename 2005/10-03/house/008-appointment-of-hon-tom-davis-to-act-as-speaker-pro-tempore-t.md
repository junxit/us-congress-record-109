# APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM DAVIS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS ON TODAY

```
  APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM DAVIS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN 
             ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS ON TODAY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                  October 3, 2005.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Tom Davis to act as Speaker 
     pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions on 
     this day.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
