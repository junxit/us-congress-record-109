                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 258

  At the request of Mr. DeWine, the name of the Senator from New Mexico 
(Mr. Bingaman) was added as a cosponsor of S. 258, a bill to amend the 
Public Health Service Act to enhance research, training, and health 
information dissemination with respect to urologic diseases, and for 
other purposes.

  At the request of Mr. Levin, the names of the Senator from Tennessee 
(Mr. Frist) and the Senator from Kentucky (Mr. McConnell) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 392, a bill to authorize the President to award a gold 
medal on behalf of Congress, collectively, to the Tuskegee Airmen in 
recognition of their unique military record, which inspired 
revolutionary reform in the Armed Forces.

  At the request of Mr. Hagel, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Ms. Mikulski) was added as a cosponsor of S. 506, a bill to amend the

Federal, State, local, and tribal public health agencies.

Dakota (Mr. Thune) was added as a cosponsor of S. 603, a bill to amend

costs to consumers under such agreements, to provide certain 
substantive rights to consumers under such agreements, and for other 
purposes.
