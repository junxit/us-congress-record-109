# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 4:50 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered

       S. 252. An act to direct the Secretary of the Interior to

     Regents of the University and Community College System of 
     Nevada.

     Groundwater Study and Facilities Act to authorize certain
