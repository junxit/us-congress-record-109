# THANKING THE SPRING 2005 PAGES

```
                     THANKING THE SPRING 2005 PAGES

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, before we leave I want to give a final 
thanks to the page class of the spring of 2005. Tomorrow is a special

your service, and thus we do so tonight. They have done a tremendous 
job over the past several weeks. We thank them for their hard work.
```
