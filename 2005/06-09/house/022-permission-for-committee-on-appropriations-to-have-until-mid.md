# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT JUNE 10, 2005, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2006

```
PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT JUNE 
     10, 2005, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2006

  Mr. WOLF. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee on

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1 of rule XXI, points of 
order are reserved.
```
