# Text of House Amendment 2

**Speaking:** Tancredo, Thomas G. (T000458) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 3010

```
H.R. 3010

                        Offered By: Mr. Tancredo

       Amendment No. 2: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec.    . None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be used to pay the salaries and 
     expenses of personnel to carry out the provisions of section 
     1011 of the Medicare Prescription Drug, Improvement, and 
     Modernization Act of 2003 (Public Law 108-173).
```
