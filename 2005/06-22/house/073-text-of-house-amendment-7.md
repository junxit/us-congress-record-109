# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Hefley, Joel (H000444) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 3010

```
H.R. 3010

                         Offered By: Mr. Hefley

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following 
     section:
       Sec. 5__. Of the amounts made available under title IV for 
     the account ``Corporation for Public Broadcasting'', 
     $40,000,000 is transferred and made available under title II 
     as an additional amount for the account ``National Institutes 
     of Health--office of the director''.
```
