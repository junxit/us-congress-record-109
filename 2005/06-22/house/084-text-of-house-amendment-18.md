# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Brown-Waite, Ginny (B001247) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3010

```
H.R. 3010

              Offered By: Ms. Ginny Brown-Waite of Florida

       Amendment No. 18: In title III in the item relating to 
     ``School Improvement Programs'' insert before the period at 
     the end the following: ``: Provided further, That, of the 
     funds made available under this heading, $25,296,000 is for 
     carrying out subpart V of part D of title V of the Elementary 
     and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 7251 et seq.) 
     (relating to the Reading is Fundamental inexpensive book 
     distribution program)''.
```
