# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 26, 2005

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 26, 2005

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, the Senate stand in adjournment 
until 9:30 in the morning, Thursday, May 26. I further ask that 
following the prayer and the pledge, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved, and that the Senate then return to 
executive session and resume consideration of the nomination of John 
Bolton to be U.S. ambassador to the U.N. as provided under the previous 
order; provided that 1 hour be under the control of Senator Voinovich.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
