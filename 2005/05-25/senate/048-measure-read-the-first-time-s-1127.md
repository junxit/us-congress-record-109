# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1127

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1127

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk, and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1127) to require the Secretary of Defense to 
     submit to Congress all documentation related to the 
     Secretary's recommendations for the 2005 round of defense 
     base closure and realignment.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I now ask for its second reading, and in order to 
place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
