# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2528, MILITARY QUALITY OF LIFE AND VETERANS AFFAIRS APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2006

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2528, MILITARY 
     QUALITY OF LIFE AND VETERANS AFFAIRS APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2006

  Mr. GINGREY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 109-97) on the resolution (H. Res. 298) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2528) making appropriations for 
military quality of life functions of the Department of Defense, 
military construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2006, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be
```
