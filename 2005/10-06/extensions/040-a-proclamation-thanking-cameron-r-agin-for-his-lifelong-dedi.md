# A PROCLAMATION THANKING CAMERON R. AGIN FOR HIS LIFELONG DEDICATION TO HIS COMMUNITY AND HIS COUNTRY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
A PROCLAMATION THANKING CAMERON R. AGIN FOR HIS LIFELONG DEDICATION TO 
                     HIS COMMUNITY AND HIS COUNTRY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 6, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas Cameron R. Agin honorably served his country during a time of 
conflict in WorId War II; and
  Whereas, Cameron R. Agin continued his life of service with the 
Zanesville Police Department, earning the rank of Captain; and
  Whereas, Cameron R. Agin led a life devoted to civic responsibility 
and true patriotism in the Jaycees, Charity Newsies, American Legion, 
Boy Scouts, Grotto, Shrine and the VFW.
  Therefore, I join with family, friends and the entire 18th 
Congressional district of Ohio in thanking Mr. Cameron R. Agin for his 
inspiring service both in war and at peace.

                          ____________________
```
