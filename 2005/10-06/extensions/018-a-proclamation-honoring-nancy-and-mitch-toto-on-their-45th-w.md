# A PROCLAMATION HONORING NANCY AND MITCH TOTO ON THEIR 45TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

```
  A PROCLAMATION HONORING NANCY AND MITCH TOTO ON THEIR 45TH WEDDING 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 6, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Nancy and Mitch Toto were united in marriage October 1, 
1960, and are celebrating 45 years of marriage; and
  Whereas, Nancy and Mitch Toto were united in marriage at St. Casimirs 
in Adena, Ohio; and
  Whereas, Nancy and Mitch Toto are the loving parents of two children 
and five grandchildren.
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District of Ohio in congratulating Nancy and Mitch Toto as they 
celebrate their 45th Wedding Anniversary.

                          ____________________
```
