# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, tomorrow, following morning business, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the Bankruptcy Reform Act. Under 
the previous order, upon returning to the bill at 11 a.m., the Senate 
will proceed to a series of stacked rollcall votes on two Kennedy 
amendments and the Akaka amendment to the bill. We will then have an 
additional series of votes a little later in the afternoon which will 
culminate with a vote on final passage.
  I want to thank my colleagues for their work on the bill. The 
schedule for the completion of this bill was worked out on both sides 
and will allow us to finish the bill at a reasonable hour tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```
