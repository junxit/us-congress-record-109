# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God most high, You rule forever and supervise the nations with 
justice. We thank You for Your grace and mercy. You are faithful to all 
who depend on You. Keep us from the gates that lead to ruin.
  Bless our Senators; empower them to speak for justice, to love mercy, 
and to embrace humility. This day, give them the wisdom to plant seeds 
that will produce a bountiful harvest in the months ahead. Keep them in 
Your care and make certain that each step they take is sure.
  Bless the members of each Senator's staff. Give each of us love that 
will follow You into a bright future. We pray in Your powerful Name. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
