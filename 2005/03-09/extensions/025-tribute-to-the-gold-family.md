# TRIBUTE TO THE GOLD FAMILY

```
                       TRIBUTE TO THE GOLD FAMILY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ERIC CANTOR

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 9, 2005

  Mr. CANTOR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Melinda and 
Merrill Gold on the birth of their twin daughters, Eliana Paige and 
Molly Gabrielle. Born February 12, 2005, Eliana and Molly are named in 
loving memory of their great grandmothers Ita Akerman and Mollie 
Freedman. Mr. Speaker, I hope you will join me in wishing the Gold 
family great happiness and joy in the coming years.
```
