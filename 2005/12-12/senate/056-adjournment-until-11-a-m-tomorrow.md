# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 11 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 330

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 11 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. McCONNELL. If there is no further business to come before the 
Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment 
under the provisions of S. Res. 330 as a further mark of respect to the 
late Senator McCarthy.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 5:41 p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, December 13, 2005, at 11 a.m.
```
