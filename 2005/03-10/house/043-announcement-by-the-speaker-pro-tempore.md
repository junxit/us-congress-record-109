# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                              {time}  1500
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Dent). Under clause 5(d) of rule XX, the 
Chair announces to the House that, in light of the administration of 
the oath to the gentlewoman from California (Ms. Matsui), the whole 
number of the House is adjusted to 435.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  March 10, 2005--On Page H1327 the following appeared: Under 
clause 5(c) of rule XX, the Chair announces.
  
  The online version should be corrected to read: Under clause 
5(d) of rule XX, the Chair announces.


 ========================= END NOTE =========================
```
