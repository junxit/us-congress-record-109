# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Voinovich, George V. (V000126) [R-OH]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 2 p.m. 
today the Senate proceed to votes in relation to the next two 
amendments; provided further that all votes after the first be limited 
to 10 minutes each. The amendments are Leahy amendment No. 83 and 
Durbin amendment No. 112.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. VOINOVICH. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
```
