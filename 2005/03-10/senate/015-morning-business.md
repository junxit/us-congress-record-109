# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate now stand 
in a period for morning business until 2 p.m., with the time equally 
divided between the two leaders or their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to be permitted to 
speak in morning business up to 25 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
