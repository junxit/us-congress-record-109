# A PROCLAMATION HONORING SEAN WYATT DILLON

```
               A PROCLAMATION HONORING SEAN WYATT DILLON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 10, 2005

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker:
  Whereas, Matthew and Elizabeth Dillon are celebrating the arrival of 
their son, Sean Wyatt DIllon; and
  Whereas, Sean Wyatt Dillon was born on January 6, 2005 and weighed 
eight pounds and two ounces; and
  Whereas, Matthew and Elizabeth Dillon are proud to welcome their new 
son into their home; and
  Whereas, Sean Wyatt Dillon will be a blessed addition to his family, 
bringing love, joy, and happiness for many years to come.
  Therefore, I join with Members of Congress and Congressional Staff in 
celebrating with Matthew and Elizabeth Dillon the birth of Sean Wyatt 
Dillon.

                          ____________________
```
