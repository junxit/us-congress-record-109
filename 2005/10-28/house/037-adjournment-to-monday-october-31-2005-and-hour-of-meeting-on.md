# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2005 AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 2005

```
ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2005 AND HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, 
                            NOVEMBER 1, 2005

  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 3 p.m. on Monday next, and

12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, November 1, 2005, for morning hour debate.

gentlewoman from Tennessee?
```
