```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

for rollcall vote No. 555, on agreeing to the Conference Report to H.R. 
2744, Making Appropriations for Agriculture, Rural Development, Food

President Mahmoud Ahmadinejad's threats against Israel. Had I been
```
