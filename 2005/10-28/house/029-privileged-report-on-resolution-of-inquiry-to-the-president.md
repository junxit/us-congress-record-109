# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

```
      PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

  Mr. BOEHNER, from the Committee on Education and the Workforce, 
submitted an adverse privileged report (Rept. No. 109-258) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 467) requesting that the President transmit to the 
House of Representatives information in his possession relating to 
contracts for services or construction related to Hurricane Katrina 
recovery that relate to wages and benefits to be paid to workers, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
