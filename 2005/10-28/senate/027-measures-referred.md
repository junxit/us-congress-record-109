# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 3945

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bill was read the first and the second times by

     Bill to facilitate recovery from the effects of Hurricane

     waivers of certain requirements and fees imposed on,

     regulatory agencies, and for other purposes''; to the
```
