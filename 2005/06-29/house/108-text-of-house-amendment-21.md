# Text of House Amendment 21

**Speaking:** Tiahrt, Todd (T000260) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 3058

```
H.R. 3058

                    Offered By: Mr. Tiahrt of Kansas

       Amendment No. 21: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to promulgate regulations without consideration of 
     the effect of such regulations on the competitiveness of 
     American businesses.
```
