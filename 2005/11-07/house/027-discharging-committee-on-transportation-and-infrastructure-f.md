# DISCHARGING COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION AND INFRASTRUCTURE FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 69, RELATING TO A NATIONAL EMERGENCY DECLARED BY THE PRESIDENT ON SEPTEMBER 8, 2005

**Speaking:** Miller, George (M000725) [D-CA], Kildee, Dale E. (K000172) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 69

```
DISCHARGING COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION AND INFRASTRUCTURE FROM FURTHER 
  CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 69, RELATING TO A NATIONAL 
        EMERGENCY DECLARED BY THE PRESIDENT ON SEPTEMBER 8, 2005

  Mr. GEORGE MILLER of California. Madam Speaker, I offer a privileged 
motion.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the motion.
  The Clerk read as follows:

       Mr. George Miller of California moves that the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure be discharged from further 
     consideration of House Joint Resolution 69.


                 Motion to Table Offered by Mr. Kildee

  Mr. KILDEE. Madam Speaker, I move to table the motion.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the motion to table the 
motion to discharge is adopted and, without objection, a motion to 
reconsider is laid on the table.
  There was no objection.
```
