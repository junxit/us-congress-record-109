# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutknecht, Gil (G000536) [R-MN]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 260, H.Res. 444, H.R. 1973

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. GUTKNECHT. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent from the House 
on November 7, 2005 due to an important meeting I had with the New 
Zealand Ambassador in St. Paul, Minnesota. During this meeting, the 
Ambassador and I discussed agricultural trade issues.

following bills: H. Con. Res. 260, H.R. 1973, and H. Res. 444.
```
