# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 4176

**Speaking:** Doolittle, John T. (D000429) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4176

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 4176

  Mr. DOOLITTLE. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to have the 
gentleman from Nevada (Mr. Gibbons) removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 
4176.
  This bill involves public lands that cross our adjacent districts. 
The gentleman's name was added to the bill erroneously.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Nevada?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
