# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1969

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1969

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk, and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1969) to express the sense of the Senate 
     regarding Medicaid reconciliation legislation to be reported 
     by a conference committee during the 109th Congress.

  Mr. SESSIONS. I now ask for its second reading and, in order to place 
the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I object to 
my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read the 
next time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
